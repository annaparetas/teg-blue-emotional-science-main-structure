Where is my organisation landing, and what can I read of it?

A reading of your own present organisation: the position it has taken, whether it is still updating with present conditions, what that compresses to in the four modes, and what ME can read of it through the ME access capacities. Organisation determines the mode; access is placed beside it and never used to infer it. Not a type, score or verdict. Never a reading of someone else.

Step 1 · organisation

What is the organism organised for right now?

Choose the position that fits this situation, in this context, now. Read it from conditions and organisation, not from how safe you feel alone. A position is a coordinate, not who you are.

Step 2 · relationship with present conditions

Is this organisation still updating, or has it become the repeated home?

Keep current demand visible

A pattern that persists because danger, deprivation, coercion or illness continues is not evidence of failed updating. Ask first: is the demand still present, uncertain or mixed, changed, or is there not enough information? Fluid does not mean calm; Chronic does not mean severe, permanent or chosen.

Step 5 · the reading

Model 2's row beside what ME can read of it.

Each card first shows the unchanged Model 2 row for this Position. Where a card connects to an ME access capacity, a separate Model 1 reading explains what is currently readable and usable. Changing an access band changes that reading, not the biological process or the Gradient Position.

Not on the Compass

Behaviour, power, impact, response to feedback, recurrence and repair are assessed on their own evidence plane, never read from a position, a plane or a capacity. Domination is observable conduct, not the fourth mode. See Behaviour and responsibility.

Reading rules

What a reading is.

One situation, one time

A reading locates a pattern in a context. The same person shows different positions, planes and access by relationship, condition and time. No reading is a whole-person location.

No arrow runs backwards

A mode does not establish conduct; conduct does not establish a mode. Reduced access explains nothing about innocence or guilt. Observing another person, name what was done, with what power, and what happened to them.
