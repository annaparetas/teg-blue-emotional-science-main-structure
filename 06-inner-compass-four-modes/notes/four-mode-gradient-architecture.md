## Decision in one sentence

**Inner Compass is the complete public experience, and the 4-Mode Gradient is its central orientation map: a synthesis layer that helps a person understand how ME participates in regulation, protection, power and repair without replacing the Emotional Signal Map, Model 1, Model 2, Model 3 (ESC), the Fluid–Chronic framework or the separate conduct-and-power evidence plane.**

The 4-Mode Gradient is not Model 4. It does not sit above the existing architecture as a superior cause or explanation. It translates several deeper records into a public orientation while leaving their conceptual ownership intact.

## Governing question

> **How are internal organisation, information access and ME participation changing through time, and what does the separate conduct-and-power record show about action, impact, response to boundaries, accountability and repair?**

The tool may help a person hold emotional and bodily information, interpretation, external evidence, available action and relational impact together. It must not collapse them into one score or verdict.

## Complete mode mapping

| Public mode | Underlying coordinate | Architectural meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Connection** | Every Fluid Position: X, A, A↔B, B, C, D and Z | Present-responsive awareness and participation remain sufficiently available. Organisation remains sufficiently responsive to present or predicted conditions, available action, outcome information and updating. Connection can include mobilisation, defence, strategic action, power mobilisation or shutdown. It does not mean calm, complete conscious awareness or Position A alone. |
| **Protection** | Chronic X, A, A↔B, B and Z | Persistent protective organisation may appear through elevated readiness, conditional belonging, repeated Safety Checking, Protection/Defence or collapse/conservation. These routes remain distinct beneath the public umbrella. |
| **Control** | Chronic C | Persistent strategic management increasingly organises uncertainty, conditions, outcomes or other people. Chronic C alone does not establish manipulation, coercion, concealed intent or harm. |
| **Dominance** | Chronic D | Regulation is organised as though maintaining asymmetric control over relevant others is the only viable route to stability, certainty or continuation. “Only viable route” describes what the organisation treats as functionally available; it does not claim that biology contains no other capacity. Dominance does not establish Domination. |

Mixed, partial, changing and unknown records remain possible. The four modes are not four kinds of person, diagnoses, moral identities, biological states or positions on a severity scale.

## Regulation across the four modes

Regulation remains the ongoing organisation and allocation of biological resources and capacities in relation to demand. It does not mean calm.

| Mode | Regulation and participation orientation |
| --- | --- |
| **Connection** | Organisation can move across the full Fluid Gradient and update with conditions, outcomes and usable feedback. Wider access and Return remain sufficiently available, even during intense mobilisation. |
| **Protection** | Regulation is recurrently organised around protecting the self, preserving belonging, anticipating danger, defending, withdrawing or conserving. The pattern may still correspond to continuing adversity. |
| **Control** | Regulation increasingly depends on sustained monitoring, certainty, strategy or management of conditions and outcomes. Whether another person's agency is being constrained must be assessed separately. |
| **Dominance** | Regulation increasingly depends on maintaining superior or asymmetric control over relevant others. This is the Chronic D internal orientation; it is not confidence, leadership, legitimate authority, assertiveness, boundary-setting, personality or diagnosis, and it does not establish harmful enactment. |

No mode is identified from arousal, one action, one answer or one internal sensation. The relevant record includes context, current demand, recurrence, flexibility, updating and what happens after feedback.

## Domination conduct must remain explicit

**Domination** names the observable enactment of power that reduces another person's agency. It is not the fourth internal Mode. Domination must not be softened into generic conflict, poor communication or mutual dysregulation when the evidence shows unequal power or constrained agency.

Relevant observable conduct can include:

- coercion, intimidation, threats and punishment;
- forced compliance or refusal to accept another person's no;
- controlling money, movement, relationships, information, communication or access;
- gaslighting or the imposition of a required account of reality;
- surveillance, isolation and deprivation;
- strategic deception where the evidence supports it;
- exploitation of dependency, vulnerability or unequal power;
- dehumanisation and treatment of another person as an instrument or entitlement;
- retaliation after resistance, disclosure, boundary-setting or help-seeking;
- refusal of accountability, cessation, restitution or repair;
- narcissistic behaviours when the observable pattern supports that description.

### Required language safeguards

- **Narcissistic behaviours** does not silently become a diagnosis of Narcissistic Personality Disorder.
- Manipulative tactics and patterns may be named from observable evidence. Concealed intent or a harmful goal requires its own supporting evidence.
- Domination may be calm, planned, institutional or highly activated.
- Protective or developmental origins never erase impact, agency, responsibility or the need for protection and repair.
- Understanding an internal route is never a condition for another person to set a boundary, seek support or leave.

The complete technical relationship is:

```text
Chronic D → Dominance Mode
Dominance enacted through agency-reducing power → Domination
```

The second line does not claim that every act of Domination proves Dominance Mode. It describes the route only when the internal and conduct records have each been established independently.

“Dominance Mode—Domination not established” may be useful as an internal technical guardrail. It is not approved public-facing copy.

The inverse guardrail is equally required:

> **Domination established—Dominance Mode and Chronic D not inferred.**

## The two evidence planes

### Plane 1 · Internal organisation and participation

This plane may record:

- current and predicted conditions;
- bodily and emotional information available to ME;
- reflective-symbolic information and interpretation;
- differentiation among feeling, interpretation and external evidence;
- present Gradient organisation and Fluid–Chronic profile;
- action range, controllability, updating, recovery and Return;
- what remains mixed, unavailable or unknown.

It cannot establish another person's conduct, intent, harmfulness, diagnosis, responsibility or repair capacity.

### Plane 2 · Conduct, power, impact and repair

This plane may record:

- what was done and what pattern recurred;
- which power, authority, dependency or vulnerability was involved;
- whether refusal and exit were viable;
- what happened to another person's agency, safety, resources or reality;
- what happened when feedback, resistance or a boundary arrived;
- whether the conduct stopped, escalated, shifted tactics or recurred;
- accountability, restitution, protection and repair.

It cannot establish a private nervous-system state, information-access configuration, physiology, childhood history or personal motive without separate evidence.

### Different use rules

- **Self-reflection** may provisionally connect internal access and organisation with one's own known conduct, while preserving unknown and mixed fields.
- **Observing another person:** name observable conduct, power and impact. Do not assign their internal organisation. Domination may be named when the conduct-and-power evidence supports it, without inferring Dominance Mode, Chronic D, physiology, childhood history, concealed intent or diagnosis.

Neither plane may be inferred backwards from the other.

**Feedback retains two named fields and owners.** **Outcome information and temporal updating** belongs to ESC. **Response to boundaries, impact and accountability** belongs to Behaviour/HPR.

## Terminology rules

Until Anna makes a later naming decision, the canonical working names are:

- **Connection**;
- **Protection**;
- **Control**;
- **Dominance**.

Keep these distinctions explicit:

- **Connection Mode** means the full Fluid Gradient; **Connection / Belonging Position A** means one Gradient organisation.
- **Protection Mode** is the public umbrella for Chronic X, A, A↔B, B and Z; **Protection / Defence Position B** is one route within it.
- **Control Mode** translates the Chronic C territory but does not by itself establish controlling behaviour or manipulation.
- **Dominance Mode** translates Chronic D. Its core orientation depends on maintaining asymmetric control as the only functionally available route; it does not establish Domination.
- **Domination** is not an internal Mode. It is established from observable agency-reducing conduct and power without inferring Dominance Mode.

All four Modes translate internal organisation for self-reflection. Domination belongs to the separate conduct-and-power record and may be established even when the internal organisation remains unknown.

The following are first ideas for later structural and visual exploration, not adopted terminology:

- Connection Awareness;
- Protection–Defensiveness;
- Sustained Control.

**Power Mode** and **Power–Domination** are rejected. Power is too broad and may sound like a desirable capacity or achievement rather than an internal organisation.

## Relationship to the 14 formations

The 4-Mode Gradient does not replace or reduce the fourteen Fluid and Chronic formations.

- The fourteen formations remain the deeper organisational engine.
- The four modes group them for public orientation.
- Position-specific physiology, access, action, context and Return remain owned by Model 2 and its companions.
- Fluid–Chronic status concerns responsiveness, persistence, flexibility and updating—not mildness, severity or moral value.
- The persistence-and-flexibility profile remains multidimensional and must not become a mode percentage or total person score.
- Emotion families retain their established meanings and are not reassigned or transformed by mode.

## Historical recovery decisions

### Retain

- the memorable four-part public orientation;
- the continuous visual relationship and four-colour recognisability;
- movement rather than fixed personality types;
- use across personal, relational and institutional contexts;
- body, perception, emotion, relationship, conduct and power as questions the tool can route to their correct owners.

### Revise

- “Connection” from one calm or affiliative state into the whole Fluid Gradient;
- “Protection” from one acute state into the public umbrella for non-C/D Chronic protective routes;
- “Control” into a Chronic C orientation whose behavioural meaning still requires observable evidence;
- “Oppression” into **Dominance** for the Chronic D internal Mode and **Domination** for agency-reducing conduct where the conduct-and-power requirements are met;
- one linear biological scale into a synthesis map with separate internal and external evidence planes;
- awareness as one on/off quantity into differentiated access, use and revisability.

### Retire from current authority

- four fixed nervous-system types or a complete spectrum of human behaviour;
- percentages such as “31% Protection” and any averaged person score;
- good-to-bad, calm-to-dangerous or low-to-high severity readings;
- one emotion meaning or transformed emotion family assigned to each mode;
- automatic linear escalation from protection to domination;
- claims that empathy, cognition, mentalizing or the “thinking brain” simply switch off;
- specific neurochemical or autonomic signatures used to identify a mode without claim-level evidence;
- any inference that internal organisation proves manipulation, abuse, domination, innocence or guilt.

Historical files remain provenance. They do not instruct or override the current architecture.

## Settled decisions

- Inner Compass is the complete public experience.
- Inner Compass is reserved for the complete public product rather than reused as the name of the Model 1 access construct.
- Model 1 is **The Two Biological Information Systems and ME**, with **ESS · CLS · ME** as its short navigation label.
- Model 2 is **The Nervous System Organisation Gradient**, with **Nervous System Gradient** as its short navigation label.
- Model 1's canonical access page is `models/01-information-systems/coordinated-conscious-access.html`. Emotional Signal Map records use **Conscious availability to ME** for the variable being examined and keep it separate from possible ME participation.
- The 4-Mode Gradient is its central orientation map and a synthesis layer, not Model 4.
- All Fluid positions map to Connection.
- Chronic X, A, A↔B, B and Z map to Protection.
- Chronic C maps to Control.
- Chronic D maps to Dominance Mode.
- Domination is the observable enactment of power that reduces another person's agency. Dominance does not establish Domination, and Domination does not establish Dominance or Chronic D.
- The four modes describe organisations of regulation and participation rather than people.
- Emotion families remain unchanged.
- Internal organisation and external conduct/power/impact/repair remain separate evidence planes.
- Domination must name harmful conduct directly while preserving diagnostic and intent boundaries.
- Power Mode and Power–Domination are rejected names.
