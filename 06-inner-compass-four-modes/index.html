Approved product architecture

The product and its central map are not the same thing.

Complete public experience

Inner Compass

Brings together emotional information, Models 1 to 3, Fluid and Chronic organisation and the separate conduct-and-power record. Named after the capacity it supports: coordinated access to bodily information, feeling, context and direction. Until 26 August 2026 that access relationship itself was called the Inner Compass; the name is now reserved for the product, and the relationship is coordinated conscious access in Model 1.

Central orientation map

The 4-Mode Gradient

Translates the deeper architecture into four memorable modes while leaving conceptual ownership intact. It does not sit above the models as a superior cause. The fourteen formations remain the engine; the modes group them for public orientation.

Governing question

How are internal organisation, information access and ME participation changing through time, and what does the separate conduct-and-power record show about action, impact, response to boundaries, accountability and repair?

The tool may help a person hold emotional and bodily information, interpretation, external evidence, available action and relational impact together. It must not collapse them into one score or verdict.

Modes are not people

The four modes are organisations of regulation and participation. They are not four kinds of person, diagnoses, moral identities, biological states or points on a severity scale. Mixed, partial, changing and unknown records are valid. No mode is identified from arousal, one action, one answer or one sensation; the record includes context, current demand, recurrence, flexibility, updating and what happens after feedback. Emotion families are not reassigned by mode.

Four home positions, two planes

A mode is the position the organisation is organised around and returns to.

  1. Which position now?X, A, A↔B, B, C, D or Z, read from conditions and organisation. The four mode words also name the core positions as they show in one behaviour: Connection A, Protection B, Control C, Dominance D.
  2. Still returning?Fluid: yes. The organisation is organised around A and is visiting this position. Its mode is Connection whatever it is visiting. Chronic: no, go on.
  3. Organised around which position?B and its protecting neighbours (Protection), C (Control), D (Dominance).
  4. What can ME read of it?The ME access capacities from Model 1, set on the ME Access Dials.
  5. What was done?Not on the map. The conduct plane.
Visited or stabilised

Every core position can be visited or stabilised. A proportionate protective, strategic or forceful response that fits present conditions and updates is a position visited from Connection. The same position kept across contexts, no longer returned from, is a stabilised mode: what regulation has come to depend on is protecting or preserving, certainty and management, or asymmetric control over relevant others. Stabilised Dominance is organised around Chronic D. Its access configuration must be observed separately; no capacity pattern is required or inferred. Fluid D is different again because it is a position visited under present-responsive maximum survival mobilisation.

The old ruler drew one line from teal to pink. The line survives as the order of the four positions, not as an automatic escalation or an access scale: there is no inevitable movement from protection to dominance, and capacities are observed separately. The public tools read one behaviour at one of the four positions; the Compass reads the position now, the plane, and from those the mode. Until 3 September 2026 this section called the map a fork and a mode a regulation dependency; the mapping is unchanged, the reading is now the home position.

Essential ethical boundary

Dominance is internal. Domination is observable.

Dominance Mode

Chronic D as internal organisation, for self-reflection. Dominance Mode does not establish Domination.

Domination

The observable enactment of power that reduces another person's agency: coercion, intimidation, threats and punishment; forced compliance or refusal to accept a no; controlling money, movement, relationships, information or access; gaslighting or an imposed account of reality; surveillance, isolation and deprivation; exploitation of dependency; dehumanisation; retaliation after resistance, disclosure or help-seeking; refusal of accountability, cessation or repair. Observable Domination does not establish Chronic D, physiology, history, concealed intent or diagnosis.

Language safeguards

Narcissistic behaviours never silently become a diagnosis. Manipulative tactics may be named from observable evidence; concealed intent needs its own evidence. Domination may be calm, planned, institutional or highly activated. Protective or developmental origins never erase impact, agency, responsibility or the need for protection and repair. Understanding an internal route is never a condition for another person to set a boundary, seek support or leave. Domination must not be softened into generic conflict or mutual dysregulation when the evidence shows unequal power or constrained agency.

Two evidence planes

Neither plane is inferred from the other.

Plane 1

Internal organisation and participation

Current and predicted conditions; bodily and emotional information available to ME; reflective information and interpretation; present Gradient organisation and Fluid–Chronic profile; action range, controllability, updating, recovery and Return; what remains mixed, unavailable or unknown. Cannot establish another person's conduct, intent, harmfulness, diagnosis, responsibility or repair capacity.

Plane 2

Conduct, power, impact and repair

What was done and what recurred; which power, dependency or vulnerability was involved; whether refusal and exit were viable; what happened to another person's agency, safety, resources or reality; what happened when feedback or a boundary arrived; accountability, restitution, protection and repair. Cannot establish a private nervous-system state, access configuration, physiology, history or motive.

Use rules

Self-reflection may provisionally connect one's own access and organisation with one's own known conduct, while preserving unknown and mixed fields. Observing another person: name observable conduct, power and impact. Do not assign their internal organisation. Feedback keeps two owners: outcome information and temporal updating belong to Model 3; response to boundaries, impact and accountability belong to Behaviour and responsibility.

What a reading is

Position, plane, mode, access. Never a score.

A Compass reading locates one pattern in one context at one time. It shows the position the organisation is at now, whether it is visiting it from Connection or has stabilised there, which of the four modes that is, and how much of it ME can read through the ME access capacities. Organisation determines the mode; access is placed beside it and never used to infer it. The reading cards are Model 2's own rows. Behaviour and repair are not on it. The old Compass cards for perception, emotions, empathy, behaviours and repair survive as questions; behaviours and Domination have moved to the conduct plane, and empathy has become the ME access capacities plus concern.

Retired from current authority

Four fixed nervous-system types; percentages such as "31% Protection" and any averaged person score; good-to-bad, calm-to-dangerous or low-to-high severity readings; one emotion per mode; automatic linear escalation from protection to domination; claims that empathy or the thinking brain simply switch off; neurochemical or autonomic signatures used to identify a mode; any inference that internal organisation proves manipulation, abuse, innocence or guilt.
