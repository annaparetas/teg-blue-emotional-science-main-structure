Before the sequence

The two signal groups begin in different conditions.

Safety and threat evaluation matter, but they are not the universal first step. A need arising inside the body and a signal about the organism’s relationship to a situation enter the architecture differently.

Body Signals

What does the body itself need?

They begin with internal physiological conditions: respiratory chemistry, tissue condition, temperature, visceral filling, hydration, available energy and recovery demand.

Situation Signals

What does the organism register about its relationship to conditions?

They begin with current or represented relational, physical-environmental, action/goal, collective-systemic, remembered, imagined or anticipated conditions.

Model 1 inside a changing event

Information becomes available while the event keeps moving.

Stages 01–07 separate the processing functions developed on this Model 1 page. Stages 08–11 keep the wider event and its later effects visible. ESC indexes the whole line through time; it does not begin only after conscious access or action. The line is not a claim that biology follows eleven discrete stations, one anatomical pathway or one fixed speed.

Coordinated conscious access is not the signal. It is the access relationship through which some of the organism’s information may become usable by ME.

Bodily information access and use: What is happening in my body, how clearly can I differentiate it, and what can it help me do?

Emotional awareness + differentiation: What am I feeling?

Situational awareness: What is this signal about?

The Gradient: How much remains accessible and usable in the current organisation?

Three simultaneous questions

Signal, organisation and completion are three views of one event.

The sequence above follows change through time. These questions cut across the sequence while it is happening. They connect emotion, nervous-system organisation and regulation without turning them into three separate systems or a rigid handoff.

SIGNAL

What has become relevant?

What condition or need is being registered? How much priority has it gained, what direction does it make available, and what information could update it?

How is the organism reallocating around it?

What changes across physiology, energy, attention, perception, cognition, relational availability and action readiness? How flexibly can the organisation change?

COMPLETION + UPDATING

What would let the event change course or finish?

Which condition, action, evidence, boundary, relational response, recovery or reorganisation would let priority update, recruitment stop and Return become available?

route availability, safety and fit; whether pressure reduction is mistaken for completion; and whether Return or recovery is repeatedly interrupted.

No handoff: the signal can keep changing while the organisation holds; action and feedback can alter both; and completion conditions matter throughout the event, not only after it.

Worked Body Signal

Hunger shows the architecture without requiring an emotion label.

Hunger

No single chemical equals hunger. Information about energy stores, gastrointestinal conditions, nutrients and meal state is integrated with sensory, reward and motivational processes.

01

Condition: feeding-related physiological conditions change.

02

Detection: peripheral and central systems carry information about energy, GI conditions, nutrients and meal state.

03

Evaluation: brainstem, hypothalamic, metabolic, reward, sensory and motivational networks integrate it.

04

Signal: a hunger-related physiological and motivational pattern develops.

05

Organisation: attention, motivation and resources increasingly prioritise nourishment.

06

Readiness: seeking, approaching, obtaining and eating become prepared.

07

Conscious access: bodily information and food salience become “I am hungry”.

08

Action: search → obtain → eat.

09

Updating: eating produces GI, nutrient, neural and endocrine feedback.

10

Completion + Return: food-seeking priority diminishes while processing continues.

11

Learning: update where, when, what and how nourishment can be obtained.

Three Situation Signal paths

The architecture stays shared while the route to completion changes.

Fear on unstable ground

Physical-environmental condition → sensory, vestibular and proprioceptive detection → threat/relevance evaluation → Situation Signal → protective organisation → stabilise, stop, move or escape readiness → access → action → new evidence → Return.

Fear about an anticipated attack

Represented condition → memory and prediction → threat evaluation → Situation Signal → protective organisation → preparation, avoidance or protection readiness → access → action or inhibition → new information → continued protection or Return.

Shame about possible rejection

Relational or represented condition → social information and learned expectations → belonging/safety evaluation → Belonging-at-Risk signal → A↔B may be recruited → repair, hide, check or reconnect readiness → action → relational evidence → update or persistence.

Body Signals and Situation Signals are not two separate nervous systems. They are two groups of biological information with different starting conditions and different routes to completion. Both are embodied.

How organisation can change

The Gradient is not a staircase.

A Position is a current nervous-system organisation. Movement can reverse when conditions change, and Z is not simply a stage after D.

A

Connection / Belonging

Safe participation supports wide perception, reciprocity and flexible action.

A↔B

Safety Checking

Change or uncertainty recruits provisional orienting.

B

Protection / Defence

Established threat gives protection priority.

C

Strategic Management

Persistent threat recruits prediction and planning.

D

Power Mobilisation

Life threat can make force and outcome dominant.

Z

Shutdown

Conservation may become relevant when mobilisation is unavailable or too costly.

Signals tell the organism what may matter. Organisation changes what can be done with that information. Coordinated conscious access is the relationship through which some of it can become usable by ME.

Scientific boundaries

What this page does—and does not—claim

Functional sequence, not serial anatomy. Functions overlap and recur.

No one chemical equals one feeling. Ghrelin is not hunger, adrenaline is not fear, and oxytocin is not love.

Defensive processing is not conscious fear. Below-awareness processing and conscious experience remain distinct.

Neuroception is theory-specific. It is Porges’s term for proposed below-awareness safety, danger and life-threat evaluation.

Action readiness is not behaviour. A tendency can be inhibited, changed or opposed.

A signal is not an organisation. Fear is a Situation Signal; fear is not Organisation B.

Return, recovery and learning differ. Return may begin before full recovery; learning continues beyond both.

Conscious interoception is not Body Signals. It accesses bodily information from both groups.
