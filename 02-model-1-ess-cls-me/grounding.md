This file says what kind of claim each part of Model 1 makes and where its
evidence lives. It does not restate the model. Read [index.html](index.html)
first.

## Four levels of claim used in this model

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Established research construct | A defined scientific process supported within stated limits: interoception, working memory, cognitive control, affective sharing, mentalizing. |
| TEG-Blue educational grouping | ESS or CLS as a useful integration of several such processes. Not itself an established biological system. |
| TEG-Blue synthesis or hypothesis | A proposed relationship among access, organisation, interpretation, participation and consequence that requires testing. |
| Public-facing shorthand | A phrase or image retained only beside its scientific boundary. |

Dual-process theory (fast and slow, automatic and deliberative) is an analogy,
not validation. Brain regions cannot be assigned to ESS or CLS from prose.
Convergence across components does not establish the two groupings as
natural kinds.

## Provenance

ESS and CLS were created to make visible a pattern that cognition-only
accounts miss: people can believe they act from reason while
emotional–somatic organisation shapes what feels urgent, necessary or
justified. The names were retained on 19 August 2026; the accessible name,
subtitle and short label were settled on 26 August 2026, when the access
relationship was renamed from Inner Compass to coordinated conscious access.
The canon page synthesises the 23 August companion draft (`foundation/21`),
the 21 August model sketch, the 19 August decision and the 27 August summary
from the former main structure. Those Engine records remain active sources and
retain their own paths, history and ownership. The earliest public statement of the
distinction is the old `what-is-teg-blue/foundation/10-ess-cls-educational-model.md`
in the teg-blue workspace, which expands ESS as Embodied Somatic System.
