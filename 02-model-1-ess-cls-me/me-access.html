Two systems, three dials

What ME can read of each information system.

ESS and CLS keep working whether or not their information becomes readable to ME. The three ME access capacities draw on overlapping processing across the organism. Bodily access and affective sharing draw primarily on emotional–somatic processing. Mentalizing draws primarily on reflective–symbolic processing. These are primary contributions, not exclusive system ownership.

Draws primarily on ESS · emotional–somatic processing

Feel myself, feel another

Both are embodied information. Without self–other distinction the second becomes contagion rather than sharing.

Draws primarily on CLS · reflective–symbolic processing

Read and revise another

Can stay detailed while the two ESS dials are low, and can serve reciprocal, defensive or instrumental action.

  • Mentalizing access and use

Primary relationship, not exclusive ownership

Affective sharing treats resonance with another person as embodied information, but differentiation, interpretation and use can also recruit reflective processing. Mentalizing is primarily reflective–symbolic, but what it represents can include emotional and bodily information. The dials describe access territories within one interacting organism.

The controls

Move each dial separately.

Uneven settings are the point: one capacity can stay usable while another continues biologically without becoming available. Beneath each dial the reading says what ME can and cannot use at that band.

Four bands, not a measurement

Not reliably available to ME · fragments may become readable · partly available and usable · broadly available to ME. Conceptual bands, not a clinical score. Availability is not accuracy, concern or ethical use. Low access is not absent processing: the system may be intact but inhibited, unreadable or too costly for ME to use in the current organisation.

This configuration

Which capacities are in the lower and upper bands.

A configuration is a binary landmark: a capacity enters the upper group at the two upper bands. The title names only that pattern. It does not name a person or establish that any process underneath is present or absent.

    Cross-model boundary

    Access does not determine a mode.

    The same access configuration can occur briefly, under present demand, through learned narrowing, during professional focus or for reasons this tool cannot identify. Model 1 can describe what is readable and usable by ME. It cannot infer what the organism is organised for, whether the pattern is Fluid or Chronic, or which Inner Compass mode applies.

    Where a mode comes from

    The Inner Compass reads a mode from Model 2 organisation, position and plane in a specific situation. It then places the ME access capacities beside that reading. No arrow runs backwards: low or uneven access does not establish Protection, Control, Dominance, shutdown, conduct, intent or character.

    The eight configurations

    Eight binary landmarks, with this one marked.

    Each title states which capacities fall in the lower or upper group. The examples beneath it describe possibilities, not causes or person-types. The same pattern may appear briefly, recur in one context or change when conditions change. Anna's three worked examples are marked.

    Quality dimensions

    Binary access is only the first pass.

    To connect the eight configurations to the Fluid and Chronic planes, each form of access must also be read through five quality dimensions. A capacity may be faint, selective, distorted, fused, over-recruited, or intact but not currently needed.

    Availability

    Can the capacity participate at all, and at what cost?

    Differentiation

    Is the information specific, or fused into undifferentiated pressure?

    Self–other distinction

    Can my experience and your experience remain related but separate?

    Accuracy and updating

    Can the representation be corrected by present evidence?

    Flexible recruitment

    Can the capacity enter and leave according to actual conditions?

    Empathic concern is separate

    Whether another person's welfare can motivate, restrain or redirect action is a relational output. It draws on the capacities but is not a fourth one and cannot be inferred from any configuration. Each card says what concern may become; none says what it is.

    Reading rules

    Self-reflection only.

    Your own access, in a context and time

    Set the dials for yourself, for one situation. The same person shows different configurations by relationship, condition and time. Nothing here classifies a person.

    Never another person

    When observing someone else, name observable conduct, power and impact. Do not assign their capacities, their configuration, their organisation or their history.

    Where the reading happens

    Model 2's tables are the territory and never change with the setting. The Inner Compass reads them through this setting, for one position on one plane, and shows which rows become readable, faint or unavailable.
