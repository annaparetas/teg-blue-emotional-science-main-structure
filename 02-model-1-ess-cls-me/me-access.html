Two systems, three dials

What ME can read of each information system.

ESS and CLS keep working whether or not ME can read them. The three capacities name how much of each system's information becomes readable and usable by ME. Two of the dials read ESS: one's own bodily and emotional information, and another person's feeling as it arises in one's own body. One dial uses CLS: representing, checking and revising an understanding of another person.

Reading ESS · the emotional–somatic system

Feel myself, feel another

Both are embodied information. Without self–other distinction the second becomes contagion rather than sharing.

  • Affective-sharing access

Using CLS · the reflective–symbolic system

Read and revise another

Can stay detailed while the two ESS dials are low, and can serve reciprocal, defensive or instrumental action.

  • Mentalizing access and use

A claim to confirm

Assigning affective sharing to ESS treats resonance with another person as embodied information first. The glossary still defines the three capacities by their process, not by the system they read. Anna's decision of 3 September; recorded, not yet approved.

The controls

Move each dial separately.

Uneven settings are the point: one capacity can stay usable while another continues biologically without becoming available. Beneath each dial the reading says what ME can and cannot use at that band.

Four bands, not a measurement

Not reliably available to ME · fragments may become readable · partly available and usable · broadly available to ME. Conceptual bands, not a clinical score. Availability is not accuracy, concern or ethical use. Low access is not absent processing: the system may be intact but inhibited, unreadable or too costly for ME to use in the current organisation.

This configuration

What this combination may look like.

A configuration is the binary first pass: a capacity counts as available at the two upper bands. It is a pattern of ME access, not a person, and not the presence or absence of the processes underneath.

    If it stabilises

    The mode this pattern of access tends toward.

    Any configuration can be momentary, protective or professional. When one becomes the default across relationships and time, its pattern of access is what the four modes describe. Bodily and affective access fall step by step from Connection to Dominance; mentalizing does not fall, it changes in kind. The setting alone never says where the organisation is on Model 2: that needs the situation and the plane, which the Inner Compass reads.

    Working rule, for Anna to confirm

    Connection when all three dials are at the two upper bands. Dominance when both ESS dials are not reliably available and mentalizing is at the two upper bands. Control when mentalizing is at the two upper bands and both ESS dials are low without being absent. Everything else with some relational access is Protection, whether narrowed toward threat or absorbed into the other. When nothing relational is readable the pattern sits outside the four modes, closer to shutdown. The row texts are drawn from Model 2's Chronic capacity rows and from Anna's descriptions of 3 September; Dominance follows the evidence that manipulation does not require superior mentalizing. Fluid D is different: during maximum survival mobilisation everything drops out, mentalizing included, so this table describes stabilised forms only.

    The eight configurations

    The whole field, with this one marked.

    Each card is a pattern of ME access. The same configuration can be adaptive for a moment, protective under pressure, or personality-like when it becomes the default. Anna's three worked examples are marked.

    Quality dimensions

    Binary access is only the first pass.

    To connect the eight configurations to the Fluid and Chronic planes, each form of access must also be read through five quality dimensions. A capacity may be faint, selective, distorted, fused, over-recruited, or intact but not currently needed.

    Availability

    Can the capacity participate at all, and at what cost?

    Differentiation

    Is the information specific, or fused into undifferentiated pressure?

    Self–other distinction

    Can my experience and your experience remain related but separate?

    Accuracy and updating

    Can the representation be corrected by present evidence?

    Flexible recruitment

    Can the capacity enter and leave according to actual conditions?

    Empathic concern is separate

    Whether another person's welfare can motivate, restrain or redirect action is a relational output. It draws on the capacities but is not a fourth one and cannot be inferred from any configuration. Each card says what concern may become; none says what it is.

    Reading rules

    Self-reflection only.

    Your own access, in a context and time

    Set the dials for yourself, for one situation. The same person shows different configurations by relationship, condition and time. Nothing here classifies a person.

    Never another person

    When observing someone else, name observable conduct, power and impact. Do not assign their capacities, their configuration, their organisation or their history.

    Where the reading happens

    Model 2's tables are the territory and never change with the setting. The Inner Compass reads them through this setting, for one position on one plane, and shows which rows become readable, faint or unavailable.
