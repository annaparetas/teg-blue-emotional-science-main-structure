# Working Architectural Language and Evidence Levels

Status: accepted and implemented as a governing foundation · 23 August 2026
Scope: all TEG-Blue terminology, scientific reviews, evidence passes, model pages, frameworks, diagrams, crosswalks and Signal Map work
Purpose: preserve scientific boundaries without making the existence of TEG-Blue's own architectural vocabulary a recurring objection to synthesis

Canonical foundation: [Working Architectural Language and Scientific Synthesis](20-working-architectural-language.md)

## Decision

TEG-Blue terms such as **connection-preservation, organism-protection, ESS, CLS, ME, Fluid Positions** and **Situation Signal families** are working architectural language.

Their scientific status should be assessed through three levels:

1. **Established evidence** — what independent research directly supports.
2. **Grounded synthesis** — what can reasonably be concluded by bringing convergent findings together.
3. **TEG-Blue integration** — the terminology and architecture used to organise that synthesis across the system.

A Level 3 term does not need to appear under the same name in Level 1 research. The relevant test is whether Levels 1 and 2 provide a responsible foundation for the functional territory the term organises.

## What changes

- Scientific review continues to identify the evidence status and genuine inference boundaries of each claim.
- Reviews should no longer treat the absence of an identical scientific label as evidence against a TEG-Blue term.
- Once a term's definition, grounding and status are established, current pages should use it consistently without repeated tentative phrasing.
- Boundaries should be repeated when a context could confuse an architectural term with an anatomical structure, independently validated category or established scientific mechanism—not every time the term appears.
- The Science Grounding Protocol retains finer evidence classifications within Level 1 while adopting the three-level chain for movement from research into architecture.

## What does not change

- No single theory becomes the sole foundation for a TEG-Blue construct.
- Convergence does not permit false anatomical, causal or universal claims.
- TEG-Blue integration does not become established evidence merely because its components are grounded.
- The scientific review can narrow, revise or retire architectural language when Levels 1 and 2 do not provide a responsible foundation.

## First implementation

The decision is first applied in [Human Sociality and the Two Survival Functions](../../07-reference/sociality.html), where convergent research grounds the proposition that maintaining access to socially consequential others is biologically meaningful for humans and TEG-Blue names that functional territory **connection-preservation**.

The distinction allows scientific caution to constrain the claim while leaving the architecture free to do its own integrative work.
