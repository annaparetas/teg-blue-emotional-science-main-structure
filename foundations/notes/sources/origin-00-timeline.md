# Timeline — how TEG-Blue's structure changed

The rest of `what-is-teg-blue/` says what is true now. This file says when it
became true, and what forced the change. Started 2026-08-02.

**Why it exists.** A settled question that leaves no record gets re-opened, and
the second answer is never quite the first one. That is where duplicate copies
of the framework come from.

Dates below are evidenced from the repo. Where a date is unknown it says so —
do not fill one in by estimate.

---

## The spine: the mode count kept rising

One thing changed more than anything else, and most other changes follow it.

**Not a clean chronology.** Map 1 and the Full Breakdown both existed by
2025-11-14, so two modes and four modes were in use at the same time. Map 1's own
filename — *Public in general Diagram Edition* — says why: two modes is the entry
presentation, not an earlier stage of the thinking. The rising count below is
rising **resolution**, and only the last two rows are securely later in time.

| Stage | Count | Named as | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Map 1 | 2 | Connection Mode, Protection Mode | Map 1 artwork, 2025-11-14 |
| 4-Mode set | 4 | Connection, Protection, Control, Oppression | Full Breakdown 2025-11-14; standalone ruler 2025-12-06 |
| Written layer | 4 | Safety & Openness, Threat & Defence, Strategy & Management, Power & Dominance | `M2-Nervous-System-States-Concept-Architecture.md`, April 2026 |
| Map 7 | 5 named states inside modes 2–4 | Self-Protection → Self-Preservation → Power Preservation, plus Compensatory / Dominance / Power-Seeking and Pathological Power / Systemic Coercion | Map 7 artwork |
| Palette | 7 | — | `_Graphic-Assets-TEG-Blue/` 7-mode gradient palette |
| Engine 2 | 7 Positions × 2 Organisations = 14 Formations | X Baseline, A Connection/Belonging, A↔B Safety Checking, B Protection/Defence, C Strategic Management, D Power Mobilisation, Z Shutdown | `teg-blue-engine-2/source/THE-GRADIENT.md` |

Each stage adds resolution rather than replacing the last, and the Engine says
so directly: the four modes are a compression layer over the seven Positions.
Compression increases usability by removing distinctions; it does not replace
the distinctions removed.

Position, State, Formation and Mode are four different structures. The visual
layer uses "mode" for all of them, which is correct for its own surface and
wrong anywhere else.

**Map 1 already held four zones.** Its gradient bar runs teal → yellow → orange
→ red → pink while only two modes are labelled. The colour carried the
distinction before the words did.

**The question was answered in April 2026 and the answer is not in the visual
layer.** `F1-The-Emotional-Gradient-Concept-Architecture-Origin.md` § 4 is
titled "Why M2 Has Four Modes and Not Two". Its answer: human cortical expansion
recruited cognition into the existing defensive circuit, producing Strategy &
Management and Power & Dominance as cognitive extensions of the sympathetic
branch. No map states this.

## Two naming systems for the same four modes

Both are in current use. Neither has been retired.

| Visual layer | Written layer |
| --- | --- |
| CONNECTION / BELONGING | Safety & Openness |
| PROTECTION / DEFENSE | Threat & Defence |
| CONTROL / MANIPULATION | Strategy & Management |
| DOMINATION / OPPRESSION | Power & Dominance |

**The row alignment is inferred, not confirmed.** It rests on F1 § 4 naming
Strategy & Management and Power & Dominance as the two additions beyond a
two-mode system, which matches Control and Oppression as the two additions in
the artwork. Confirm against the M2 Concept Architecture before treating this
table as settled.

Related drift, same layer: `Threat & Defence` and `Threat & Defense` both appear
inside `M2-Nervous-System-States-Concept-Architecture.md`.

about five months — artwork November and December 2025, the CAO rebuild opening
2026-04-11. The maps were not illustrations of the frameworks. The frameworks
were written up from the maps.
