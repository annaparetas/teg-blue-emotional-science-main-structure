# Where TEG-Blue Sits

Status: recovered foundation, 2026-08-01. Source: earlier `What Is TEG-Blue?`
material.

**TEG-Blue places what established fields make visible into one relation, and it
does a different job from therapy, coaching and self-help. That is the whole
file.**

Recovered because the recent phase answered what TEG-Blue is *made of*. This
answers where it *sits* — and a first-time reader asks that first.

---

## Connecting The Dots

TEG-Blue is grounded in science. It draws on established research across
biology and physiology, affective and autonomic neuroscience, psychology,
attachment research, trauma research, developmental science, and others. Each
field makes one part of the pattern visible. Each field remains itself.

The models people already know — Plutchik's Wheel of Emotions, Nonviolent
Communication, CBT, Polyvagal Theory, Zones of Regulation, IFS — each answer
their own question well. None of them was built to answer how the answers
relate to each other.

That relation is what TEG-Blue contributes.

> The fields describe the parts. TEG-Blue places the parts in relation. That
> relation is the architecture, and the architecture is the contribution.

TEG-Blue acts as connective tissue — bridging psychology, education and
trauma-informed practice into one integrative, neurodivergent-friendly system
for understanding emotion, harm and repair. It replaces none of them.

### What The Integration Makes Visible

Placing the parts in relation shows two things that are hard to see from inside
any single field, and that trauma and abuse survivors recognise immediately.

**Emotion distorted under survival.** The same emotion carries different
information depending on the state it arises from. Anger from safety and anger
from chronic threat are not the same signal.

**Harm that is aimed.** Harm can be explained as capacity failing — someone
flooded, triggered, overwhelmed. That happens, and it is not the whole picture.
Harm can also be repeated, selective and organised around another person's fear,
hope or dependence.

Both come from one distinction: whether a state is **acute** movement the
organism can return from, or **chronic** organisation it is now living inside.

Without that distinction an emotion reads the same wherever it came from, and
every harm reads as capacity failing — which is an acute account, and the only
one available if chronic organisation has no name. Chronic organisation is what
makes room for harm that is not a failure of capacity at all.

Acute and chronic are TEG-Blue architecture, not a finding lifted from one
field. Each dimension of them needs its own source grounding — see
`foundation/02-established-research.md`.

## What TEG-Blue Is Not

**Not therapy.** Does not treat, heal, diagnose or provide clinical care.

**Not coaching.** Does not set goals, build plans or tell you what to decide.

**Not motivational content.** Does not promise an outcome or say what you
should do.

**Not spiritual teaching.** Does not prescribe belief or practice.

> TEG-Blue does not tell you what to feel, what to do, or what to believe.
>
> We map the territory. You choose how to navigate it.

**Not a ranking.** People need therapy, and TEG-Blue does not replace it.
Recognising a pattern is not recovering from it. Where someone needs clinical
care, crisis support, legal support or safety planning, a map is not the thing
they need. Any public use of this section carries that, or it has failed.

| | Therapy | Coaching | Self-help | TEG-Blue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Question it answers** | How do I recover? | How do I get where I want to go? | How do I keep going? | What is actually happening here? |
| **Method** | Clinical treatment and relationship | Goal-setting and accountability | Advice and encouragement | Mapping how patterns are organised, and where research grounds each part |
| **Best used** | When you are in pain and need care | When you want to move forward | When you need motivation | When you need to see the pattern before deciding anything |
| **What you leave with** | Treatment and recovery | Progress toward a goal | Motivation | Recognition — you can see the pattern and name it |

The table is `.org` orientation language, for someone deciding whether TEG-Blue
is what they are looking for. `.com` and applied builds should not inherit it.

## The Three Scales

**Personal.** Recognise your own states, and notice when your read of a
situation is being shaped rather than informed.

**Relational.** Tell the difference between a boundary and quiet control.
Recognise a pattern before it has done its full damage.

**Systemic.** See how the same patterns repeat in families, workplaces and
institutions, and where they can be interrupted.

Public copy leads with personal and relational (`README.md` rule 11). Systemic
stays deeper, and its claims stay modest.

## Rules For Using It

This section takes its posture from `02-established-research.md`: name what a
field makes visible, never what it fails at. A field or model is described by
the question it answers, and by the fact that it was not built to answer how the
answers relate — which is a statement about scope, not a shortfall.

Do not write that other models leave gaps, fall short, are incomplete or
outdated, or that TEG-Blue has filled anything in. The earlier version of this
file led with `each one also leaves spaces`; it was dropped on 2026-08-01
because it passes a verdict on other people's work, and the claim does not need
it.

The two things under *What The Integration Makes Visible* are consequences of
the acute/chronic distinction, which is TEG-Blue architecture — not claims that
established research missed something.

Not `peer-reviewed science` — TEG-Blue also draws on contemplative traditions.
Use `established research`.

Counted claims (`+30 theories`, `20+ tools`, `10 frameworks`) stay out until the
list exists and each entry traces. Route through `foundation/03-claim-audit.md`.

The models above are listed, not characterised. Characterising is `17`'s job and
is unaudited — rule in `02-established-research.md` § Named Models.

## What Did Not Come Back

- **`Emotional technology` / `infrastructure` / `architecture` as core nouns** —
  demoted by existing decision (`open-questions.md` § Identity;
  `site-origin/01` § Demote Emotional Technology; `/emotional-technology`
  parked). Concept survives above without the vocabulary.
- **All measurement language** — `measurable and trackable`, `objectively
  trackable`, `thermometer for emotional states`, `radar for the inner world`,
  `harm-measurement scales`. Each implies a reading taken *of* a person.
- **`Emotional miswiring`** — says broken. TEG-Blue says adapted.
- **`First comprehensive emotional technology system`** — unverifiable.
- **AI as an audience** — parked (`open-questions.md` § AI Alignment).

## Open Decision

Does `emotional technology` return, and where?

The concept is sound and is preserved above. What was demoted was the phrase as
*identity*, for two reasons: it foregrounded healing/repair before the
architecture was clear, and `technology` reads as software. The architecture is
clearer now, which is the condition for reopening it.

Options: keep it retired / return it as an Engine and licensing descriptor /
return it to public identity.

Recommendation: the middle one, decided separately. This file does not reopen
the identity question.
