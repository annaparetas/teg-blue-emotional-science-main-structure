# Working Architectural Language and Scientific Synthesis

> **Foundation status:** Governing · rebuild-native
> **Role:** Defines how TEG-Blue terminology should be scientifically reviewed, grounded and then used across the architecture.
> **Established:** 23 August 2026
> **Applies to:** Scientific reviews, evidence passes, terminology decisions, model pages, framework reviews, diagrams, crosswalks and Signal Map work.

TEG-Blue creates working architectural language so concepts can remain connected across models, frameworks, diagrams and files. Scientific review should test whether that language is responsibly grounded. It should not require science to have already created the same term or assembled the same architecture.

## Do not become unnecessarily rigid when reviewing TEG-Blue terminology

TEG-Blue uses its own terms—including **connection-preservation, organism-protection, ESS, CLS, ME, Fluid Positions** and **Situation Signal families**—as working architectural language.

These terms allow us to connect concepts consistently across the system. They are not necessarily claims that science has already identified a biological system, neural circuit, mechanism or established construct with that exact name.

The scientific task is therefore not:

> Can we find a paper proving that connection-preservation exists as a named scientific system?

The scientific task is:

> Do established findings provide a sufficiently strong and convergent foundation for the functional territory that TEG-Blue calls connection-preservation?

The distinction matters. One question searches for a pre-existing label. The other evaluates whether the architecture has a responsible scientific foundation.

## Example: connection-preservation

Research on:

- human cooperative sociality;
- prolonged infant dependency;
- attachment and proximity seeking;
- caregiver availability and buffering;
- social support;
- interpersonal emotion regulation;
- social regulation of risk and effort;
- social loss, exclusion and separation;
- cooperation and reciprocal resource access;

can collectively ground the proposition that **maintaining access to socially consequential others is a biologically meaningful problem for humans**.

TEG-Blue gives that functional territory the concise working name **connection-preservation**.

The final naming and organisation is a **TEG-Blue integration**. It does not need to have been independently discovered under the same name in the scientific literature in order to be useful or scientifically grounded.

The complete grounding and its architectural handoffs live in [Human Sociality and the Two Survival Functions](../../07-reference/sociality.html).

## Preserve scientific boundaries without interrupting synthesis

Continue identifying genuine scientific boundaries.

For example:

- Do not claim that social rejection and physical pain are simply the same neural system.
- Do not claim that one theory proves connection-preservation.
- Do not turn Social Baseline Theory, attachment theory, Polyvagal Theory or any other framework into the sole foundation of the architecture.
- Do not present a TEG-Blue term as an established anatomical structure or validated biological category.

Once those boundaries are clear, allow convergent evidence to support a higher-level synthesis.

TEG-Blue deliberately integrates findings across fields. The existence of a boundary between scientific evidence and architectural synthesis is not a weakness. The boundary tells us which part of the claim science supports directly and which part TEG-Blue is responsible for organising.

## The three evidence levels

### 1 · Established evidence

What independent research directly supports.

This level records the observed findings, methods, populations, measures, timescales, limitations and status of any scientific theory being used. The [Science Grounding Protocol](../../evidence/protocol.html) governs the finer evidence classifications within this level.

### 2 · Grounded synthesis

What can reasonably be concluded by bringing multiple established findings together.

A grounded synthesis may connect findings that are usually studied in different fields. Its strength depends on convergence, coherence and proportionate inference—not on finding one study that already states the complete conclusion.

### 3 · TEG-Blue integration

The terminology and architecture TEG-Blue uses to organise the grounded synthesis across its models.

This level includes names such as **connection-preservation** and **organism-protection**, as well as the relationships among ESS, CLS, ME, Fluid Positions, Situation Signal families and the Emotional–Somatic Cycle (ESC).

Do not demand Level 1 evidence for a Level 3 term. Test whether Levels 1 and 2 provide a responsible foundation for the Level 3 integration.

## Use established TEG-Blue terms consistently

Internal terminology serves a practical purpose. It lets a reader—and any Codex session working in this repository—move between frameworks without repeatedly reproducing a long scientific description.

For example, **connection-preservation** is shorthand within TEG-Blue for a larger functional territory concerning the biological importance of maintaining access to socially consequential others.

Define the shorthand carefully once. Establish its scientific foundation and evidence status. Then use it consistently across the architecture.

Do not repeatedly weaken an established architectural term into phrases such as “what TEG-Blue tentatively calls connection-preservation”. Repeat the evidence boundary only when the context could reasonably confuse an architectural term with a scientific construct, anatomical structure or independently validated category.

## Working rule

**Be rigorous about the evidence. Be flexible about the vocabulary we create to organise it.**

Scientific caution should constrain what we claim, not prevent us from building a model. The purpose of scientific review is to prevent false or exaggerated claims while allowing TEG-Blue to create useful conceptual relationships across established bodies of knowledge.

Clearly label where science ends, where grounded synthesis begins and where TEG-Blue takes responsibility for the final integration. Do not treat the existence of that boundary as a flaw.

TEG-Blue exists to build a coherent architecture across bodies of knowledge that science often studies separately.
