# What Is TEG-Blue

Status: current read-first foundation

This file holds the current central answer to:

> What exactly is TEG-Blue?

For the short reusable definition ladder, use `foundation/01-core-definition-stack.md`.

## Core Identity

TEG-Blue is The Emotional Gradient Blueprint: a layered visual framework that maps how emotions, nervous systems, survival strategies, identity, social patterns, and repair capacity form and evolve.

It shows how patterns that begin in the body can shape individual experience and relationships across safety, threat, control, shutdown, regulation, and repair.

In the current phase, this blueprint is expressed most directly through The Nervous System Gradient: the public tool that makes the gradient visible, usable, and discussable.

Shorter:

> TEG-Blue builds tools that make emotional and social patterns legible.

## Plain-Language Definition

TEG-Blue is a way to read emotional patterns as structured biological and relational information.

It maps how the body organises itself around safety, threat, connection, protection, control, shutdown, regulation, and repair.

Instead of treating emotions as vague feelings or personal labels, TEG-Blue shows them as structured information moving through the whole organism. Emotions can carry signals about body state, safety, threat, need, boundary, memory, connection, impact, and repair. They are real information, but they are not automatic facts. See `foundation/12-emotions-as-information.md`.

Those patterned states shape behaviour, individual experience, and relationships.

The purpose of TEG-Blue is to make those patterns visible enough to understand what is happening, reduce ambiguity, and support repair.

This includes perception itself. Body and nervous-system states do not only change what people feel; they change what feels true. TEG-Blue should therefore keep the difference visible between what happened, what was felt, what was assumed, what evidence is available, and what needs care, boundary, clarification, or repair. See `foundation/06-state-shaped-perception.md`.

It also includes emotional access. When emotion and body state are unavailable as information, cognition may still sound coherent while modelling the situation from incomplete data. Disconnection from emotion is not objectivity. See `foundation/13-emotional-access-and-reality-contact.md`.

Related educational model:

TEG-Blue may use the ESS / CLS distinction to explain body information and cognitive modelling in plain language. The model should help people notice what the body is carrying, what story the mind is building, what evidence is available, and what needs care, boundary, clarification, or repair. It is not a literal split-brain claim, and it should not rank body information over thought or thought over body information. See `foundation/10-ess-cls-educational-model.md`.

## Ecosystem Distinctions

TEG-Blue should currently be understood through four distinct layers:

1. Public framework / research grounding: `teg-blue.org` is the public framework and research-grounding home of The Emotional Gradient Blueprint. It should lead with emotional-pattern legibility and introduce The Nervous System Gradient as the central public map.
2. TEG-Blue Engine: the internal and potentially licensable tool-building system in `teg-blue-engine`.
3. Self-facing public tools: `teg-blue.com` helps people understand and work on their own emotional and nervous-system patterns, responses, responsibility, regulation and repair.
4. Applied or licensed builds: specific tools built from the framework and Engine for a particular institution, audience, risk context, or public-interest need.

These layers should be connected, but not collapsed into one another.

The first three name surfaces TEG-Blue owns. The fourth names a position — a slot an applied
build occupies when it draws on the framework and the Engine. A build can stand in that
position and still belong to someone else, which is the current case: the slot's one occupant,
the Violentómetro, was created by Dra. Martha Alicia Tronco Rosas and is owned by IPN. It
inherits TEG-Blue's language and claim boundaries; it does not become TEG-Blue by doing so.

## What The Name Holds

Emotional Gradient:

Emotional and behavioural organisation moves across a gradient of whole-organism states, rather than fixed categories.

Blueprint:

TEG-Blue maps the architecture of that gradient: what the body configures at each position, how patterns form, and what regulation and repair require.

## What The Gradient Does

The Nervous System Gradient is the central tool of the current TEG-Blue phase.

The gradient helps people orient:

- what kind of pattern may be present
- whether a pattern is moving toward safety, threat, control, shutdown, regulation, or repair
- how visible behaviour may differ from underlying organisation
- how feeling, interpretation, evidence, and fact may separate or fuse
- how established research can be translated into educational map language without overstating what the research proves
- how body information and cognitive modelling may cooperate, miss each other, or distort under state pressure; see `foundation/10-ess-cls-educational-model.md`
- how losing access to emotion and body state can make cognition coherent but incomplete; see `foundation/13-emotional-access-and-reality-contact.md`
- how patterns that begin in the body can shape individual experience and relationships
- where repair, interruption, or clearer understanding may be needed

It also distinguishes fluid movement from chronic rigidity. The issue is not only which state is present, but whether movement, return, and repair are still available.

The gradient is a tool for legibility. It is educational and reflective, not diagnostic.

## What Makes It TEG-Blue

TEG-Blue becomes TEG-Blue when these pieces are held together:

1. The body and nervous system organise around safety, threat, protection, control, shutdown, regulation, and repair.
2. Emotional patterns are understood as structured biological and relational information, not random feelings, moral labels, irrational noise, or automatic facts.
3. Identity and survival strategies form through repeated patterns of organisation.
4. Individual patterns and social patterns are read as connected scales of the same gradient.
5. Established research can underwrite specific parts of the architecture.
6. The integration of those parts into a visual, usable gradient is TEG-Blue's contribution.

## Research Stance

TEG-Blue is grounded in science and draws from established research across several fields, but it should not imply that TEG-Blue itself is scientifically validated as a whole.

The public framework names the integration, then shows which research areas help illuminate specific parts of the map. Each field remains itself; TEG-Blue places the parts in relation.

Safe public claims should stay structural and modest.

TEG-Blue may say:

- it is a layered visual framework
- it is grounded in science
- The Nervous System Gradient is the current central public map inside TEG-Blue
- emotions can be understood as patterned biological and relational information
- nervous-system states shape how people relate, protect, control, shut down, and repair
- patterns that begin in the body can shape individual experience and relationships
- its contribution is the integration

Claims about specific mechanisms, clinical outcomes, named theories, diagnosis, treatment, AI interpretation, or reliable external identification of internal states need citation or audit before public use.

## Ethics And Responsibility

TEG-Blue can explain the mechanism behind a pattern without excusing its impact or removing responsibility.

The gradient explains organisation, not innocence.

Calm is not the same as safety. Distress is not the same as danger. Control can look composed while its impact appears in the person being controlled.

Responsibility increases with awareness, repetition, power, available choice, refusal to repair, and impact on others.

TEG-Blue should keep harm attached to behaviours, patterns, mechanisms, and conditions rather than turning people into fixed moral categories.

## Product And Practice

For now, TEG-Blue is a layered visual framework and knowledge architecture expressed through a usable central gradient.

The Nervous System Gradient is the current central public tool on `.org`. The `.com` site contains practical interactive tools. The TEG-Blue Engine can later support licensed or commissioned tool builds for therapists, NGOs, institutions, education, prevention, and repair.

TEG-Blue has built a digital version of Violentómetro 2.0 as an emerging applied public-education tool intended for possible review/use by Dra. Martha Alicia Tronco Rosas / IPN. This is not signed or formalised yet, and the Violentómetro remains IPN's instrument.

These tools are application surfaces of TEG-Blue, not the whole identity.

TEG-Blue is not therapy, coaching, diagnosis, or treatment, and should not imply clinical authority or replace professional care.

## What TEG-Blue Is Not

TEG-Blue is not:

- therapy, coaching, diagnosis, treatment, or medical care
- a claim that one discipline already contains the full account
- a personality typology
- a moral ranking of people
- a way to identify someone's true internal state from the outside
- a way to excuse harm because a mechanism exists
- only a website, map, diagram, brand, or educational project
- an AI-aligned visual system
- a bundle of citations used as symbolic authority
