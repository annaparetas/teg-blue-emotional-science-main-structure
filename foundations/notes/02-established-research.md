# Established Research

Status: active foundation

This file defines how TEG-Blue talks about science, research grounding, source discipline, and the relationship between established fields and TEG-Blue's own contribution.

It is written to stand alone. General pages, research pages, FAQs, media language, and future page briefs should be able to use this file without needing another source file to understand the research posture.

## Public Framing

Public heading:

> Research areas behind the map

Public summary:

> The public framework names the integration, then shows which research areas help illuminate specific parts of the map. Each field remains itself; TEG-Blue places the parts in relation.

Short version:

> TEG-Blue is grounded in science, and its contribution is the integration.

Expanded version:

> TEG-Blue is grounded in science. It draws from established research across fields including biology, physiology, affective neuroscience, autonomic neuroscience, psychology, attachment research, trauma research, developmental science, anthropology, sociology, social psychology, and contemplative traditions. Those fields help illuminate specific parts of the map. TEG-Blue does not claim that any one field already says TEG-Blue, or that the whole framework has been clinically validated as a single system. Its contribution is the integration: placing those parts into a visual, usable architecture.

## Core Rule

TEG-Blue does not connect research by collecting theories side by side.

It connects research by asking what each field helps make visible inside one larger pattern.

The source-level movement is:

> field -> finding -> function -> position in the sequence -> relationship to the whole system

That is the difference between citation and substrate.

A citation supports a sentence.

Established research underwrites part of the architecture.

TEG-Blue places those parts into relation.

That relation is the architecture.

That architecture is the contribution.

## Research-To-Application Chain

TEG-Blue carries research through the ecosystem in a specific order:

> established field -> finding -> function -> position in the architecture -> Engine structure -> public translation -> context-specific tool

Each step has a different job:

- established research supplies findings, mechanisms, relationships, and limits
- TEG-Blue identifies the function of those parts and places them in relation
- the Engine preserves source trace, ownership boundaries, resolution, and translation logic
- `.org` makes the complete Gradient and its organised grounding publicly accountable
- `.com` compresses the architecture into self-facing reflection tools
- applied builds translate it for a specific audience and risk context

Moving downstream does not strengthen a scientific claim. A tool derived from the Engine is not proof that the framework, Gradient, mode, Formation-state position, or outcome has been validated.

## Grounded In Science, Not Validated As A Whole

`Grounded in science` is approved public language when it means:

- established fields help illuminate specific parts of the map
- research underwrites mechanisms, capacities, patterns, or relationships at specific points
- each field remains itself
- TEG-Blue names the integration and places the parts in relation

`Grounded in science` must not mean:

- science proves TEG-Blue as a whole
- the whole framework has been clinically validated
- one study, theory, field, or citation validates the whole architecture
- TEG-Blue is a diagnostic, treatment, or assessment system
- TEG-Blue can reliably identify someone's true internal state from the outside

## Research Areas Behind The Map

Established research should be named when it performs a structural function.

| Research Area | What It Helps Illuminate | Public Claim Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Biology and physiology | survival, adaptation, organism-environment response, energy mobilization, protection, shutdown, regulation, return, repair capacity, and source-trace concepts such as homeostasis, allostasis, stress physiology, and allostatic load | Load-bearing for the Nervous System Gradient; keep claims structural and avoid implying direct measurement or biological certainty from the outside. Do not make named physiology concepts the public doorway unless the page is explicitly a research/source-trace page. |
| Affective neuroscience | primary emotional systems, affective patterning, emotional salience | Do not imply one brain model proves the whole framework. |
| Autonomic neuroscience | state organization, arousal, flexibility, mobilization, shutdown, regulation | Avoid precise mechanism claims without citation. |
| Psychology | cognition, behaviour, development, personality organization, clinical patterns, social perception, learning, interpretation, and relational dynamics | Do not present TEG-Blue as a psychology model, therapy model, diagnosis, or replacement for clinical assessment. |
| Clinical, abnormal, and personality psychology | chronic patterns, defensive organization, personality structure, narcissistic patterns, personality-disorder research, rigidity, relational impact, and repair limits | These areas are pivotal for chronic states, but public language must not diagnose people or turn disorders into labels for gradient positions. |
| Cognitive, behavioural, and experimental psychology | attention, appraisal, interpretation, learning, reinforcement, avoidance, prediction, bias, memory, and evidence-testing | Do not reduce the whole framework to cognition or behaviour alone. |
| Attachment research | relational safety, proximity, separation, rupture, repair, expectation | Do not turn attachment patterns into fixed identity labels. |
| Trauma research | chronic threat adaptation, dissociation, hypervigilance, defensive reorganization | Do not imply TEG-Blue diagnoses trauma or treats trauma. |
| Developmental psychology and developmental science | early shaping of capacity, expectation, regulation, identity, relational learning, developmental adaptation | Avoid deterministic claims about childhood causing a specific adult pattern. |
| Evolutionary psychology, comparative psychology, anthropology, and evolutionary research | social survival, belonging, status, care, threat detection, hierarchy, mating/competition patterns, cross-species comparison, adaptive function | Use carefully; avoid simplistic evolutionary stories or deterministic claims about human behaviour. |
| Sociology and social psychology | relational patterning, social norms, group effects, power, collective behaviour | Keep this as a later or deeper scale when the public doorway is focused on individuals and relationships. |
| Contemplative traditions and attention research | attention, awareness, witnessing, self-observation, direct experience | Do not present contemplative traditions as laboratory science. Name their role clearly. |

Each field remains itself. TEG-Blue does not blur the evidence base or claim that all fields say the same thing.

This is the safe meaning of `grounded in science`: established fields illuminate and underwrite specific parts of the map; TEG-Blue contributes the relational architecture.

## Named Models

This file governs how TEG-Blue talks about research *areas*. Named models and
their authors — Plutchik, Rosenberg, Porges, Kuypers, Winnicott, Rogers, Jung,
Schwartz and others — are held separately in
`foundation/17-model-by-model-map.md`, which is staging material and not yet
publishable.

Citing a field and characterising a model are different acts. Leah Kuypers can
read what we wrote about Zones of Regulation and answer back. Richard Schwartz
can. A research field cannot. If we describe someone's model wrongly and they
say so, we have attacked the models and claimed to build on them in the same
breath.

Rule:

> Describe a model by what it does and what it was built to answer. Where
> TEG-Blue names a boundary, name it as scope, not as deficiency. Where TEG-Blue
> relies on an existing critique of a model, cite that critique rather than
> asserting it.

A model that does not address something has not failed at it, unless it set out
to do it.

For `.org`, The Nervous System Gradient is the public map that shows the
architecture.

The public science-grounding layer should explain the recurring dimensions
behind the map without implying that any one field or theory proves the whole
Gradient.

Each Formation-state position can be read across recurring grounded
dimensions:

- autonomic state
- safety / threat read
- perception
- cognition
- awareness / interoception
- body activation
- time / tempo
- emotion
- empathy / other-read
- behaviour / response
- repair / return
- chronic organization

Public-use line:

> Each Formation-state position is read across recurring grounded dimensions: autonomic state,
> perception, cognition, body activation, emotion, relational access, action,
> chronic organization, and repair. The science exists in established fields;
> TEG-Blue makes the relationships visible as one visual map.

Keep home-page references field-level and readable. Put named sources,
DOI-level traces, Formation-by-Formation source tables, and unresolved source choices in
`/scientific-foundations`, publications, or a future claim-audit/source-trace
layer.

Vocabulary boundary:

- `.org` and the Deep Engine use seven Formation coordinates in acute and chronic organisation: 14 Formation-state positions
- the Visible Engine and `.com` use four practical modes: Connection, Protection, Control, and Domination
- do not call the 14 Formation-state positions `modes`
- do not imply the four modes exhaust or validate the deeper architecture

The seven acute and seven chronic positions are TEG-Blue's integrative map. Research should ground the described dimensions of each position, not be cited as though established fields already contain the 14-position map.

## Engine Source Discipline

The Engine separates a map coordinate from the mechanisms read at that coordinate.

Its internal vocabulary includes:

- Actual Conditions and condition-reading
- emotional-information signal
- nervous-system state configuration
- response route, recovery, and restoration
- recurrence, carryover, and accumulation
- access to awareness, empathy, impact, and self-other differentiation
- cognitive modelling, interpretation, prediction, and narrative

Research may underwrite parts of this sequence through biology, physiology, neuroscience, psychology, attachment, trauma, development, learning, stress, and social research. No single field owns the complete sequence.

The Engine must keep three kinds of material visibly distinct:

1. grounded science: what an established source supports
2. TEG-Blue integration: where that supported part sits in the architecture
3. working hypothesis: a promising relationship that has not yet been adequately sourced or tested

This distinction should survive compression into public language and tools.

## Biology And Physiology As Load-Bearing Ground

Biology and physiology are load-bearing for The Nervous System Gradient.

They help ground the claim that patterns are not only ideas, interpretations, or psychological descriptions. They can also be understood as forms of organism-level organization: how a body mobilizes energy, protects itself, adapts to threat, shifts arousal, constrains perception, shuts down, regulates, and becomes available or unavailable for repair.

From a biological and physiological angle, the map can show:

- how survival and adaptation shape state
- how arousal, mobilization, inhibition, and shutdown change what becomes possible
- how repeated activation and regulatory burden can support chronic patterns
- how regulation involves body state, energy, timing, and return, not only insight or intention
- how protection and control can be read as organized responses rather than random traits
- how repair capacity depends partly on physiological state, not only moral willingness or cognitive understanding

Homeostasis, allostasis, stress physiology, and allostatic load can be useful research-trace language when a page is showing how physiology underwrites part of the map. In public or methodology introductions, translate that layer first into activation, regulation, return, burden, rigidity, and repair capacity.

This does not mean every TEG-Blue pattern can be reduced to one biological mechanism. It also does not mean TEG-Blue can measure someone's physiology, diagnose their state, or infer biological certainty from the outside.

Public boundary:

> Biology and physiology help ground the body-level organization of the map. They do not make TEG-Blue a diagnostic instrument, a biological assessment, or a claim that visible behaviour reveals someone's true internal state.

## Psychology As One Research Angle

Psychology is a major grounding family for TEG-Blue, even when TEG-Blue does not explain itself primarily as a psychology framework.

Psychology does not own these patterns. It helps illuminate them from one research angle, while biology, neuroscience, attachment research, trauma research, sociology, anthropology, contemplative traditions, and other fields illuminate overlapping parts of the same map.

From a psychological angle, the map can show:

- how perception, attention, appraisal, memory, and interpretation shape what feels true
- how behaviour is learned, reinforced, avoided, repeated, or interrupted
- how relational expectations and identity patterns develop
- how chronic patterns can become rigid, defensive, self-protective, or harmful
- how personality organization can shape repair capacity, accountability, empathy, control, shame, collapse, or domination
- how social perception, group pressure, status, comparison, and belonging shape behaviour
- how experimental findings can test specific mechanisms without validating the whole TEG-Blue architecture

Clinical psychology, abnormal psychology, personality psychology, and research on narcissism and personality disorders are especially important angles for understanding chronic states and rigid relational patterns.

Public boundary:

> These fields can help illuminate chronicity, rigidity, defence, relational impact, and repair limits. They should not be used to diagnose a person through TEG-Blue, label someone publicly, or imply that a gradient position is the same thing as a clinical disorder.

## What Established Research Does

Established research can:

- support a specific mechanism, condition, or capacity
- help explain why a pattern may appear in the body, emotion, perception, behaviour, or relationship
- help trace patterns to organism-level biology and physiology without reducing the whole person to biology
- help explain how cognitive, behavioural, developmental, clinical, personality, and social patterns form or repeat
- show that a part of the map is not invented from nothing
- give language for what a state, adaptation, or relational pattern may be doing
- help distinguish observation from interpretation
- keep the framework accountable to existing knowledge

Established research cannot automatically:

- validate the whole TEG-Blue framework
- prove a person's true internal state
- prove intent from visible behaviour
- guarantee that one state causes one specific behaviour
- replace clinical assessment, therapy, medical care, legal support, or crisis support
- make citations decorative instead of structural

## Safe Public Claims

TEG-Blue may say:

- TEG-Blue is grounded in science.
- TEG-Blue draws from established research across several fields.
- Research areas help illuminate specific parts of the map.
- Each field remains itself.
- TEG-Blue places those parts in relation.
- Established research underwrites specific parts of the architecture.
- The integration is TEG-Blue's contribution.
- The Nervous System Gradient is educational and reflective, not diagnostic.
- TEG-Blue should not be used to claim certainty about another person's true internal state, intention, or whole identity.

TEG-Blue may also say, with source discipline:

- TEG-Blue translates established research into visual educational maps for emotional-pattern legibility, as candidate whole-system language under review
- emotions can be understood as patterned biological and relational information
- nervous-system states can shape perception, emotion, behaviour, and repair capacity
- patterns that begin in the body can shape individual experience and relationships
- biology and physiology help ground how patterns organize through survival, adaptation, arousal, shutdown, regulation, and repair capacity
- chronic threat, control, or shutdown can narrow perception and repair capacity
- ESS / CLS can be presented as an educational model for distinguishing body information from cognitive modelling, as long as it is not described as a literal split-brain claim or proven brain-system taxonomy
- chronic states can be illuminated by clinical psychology, abnormal psychology, personality psychology, narcissism research, and personality-disorder studies when used carefully and non-diagnostically
- regulation and repair are not only ideas, but involve body state, perception, relationship, and behaviour
- acute names responsive state movement and chronic names rigid or repeatedly organised patterning, as TEG-Blue architecture that requires dimension-level source grounding
- a Formation names a coordinate in the Gradient, not a person type, diagnosis, or clinical category

## Claims That Need Citation Or Audit

Use citation, source trace, or further audit before public use when a claim names:

- a specific brain structure, neural pathway, hormone, or biological mechanism
- a specific physiological marker, stress pathway, immune/endocrine mechanism, or bodily measure
- a specific autonomic process
- a specific ESS / CLS biological mechanism, brain system, pathway, timing claim, or named research mapping
- polyvagal theory, affective neuroscience, attachment theory, trauma theory, homeostasis, allostasis, allostatic load, HRV, dissociation, oxytocin, or another named theory / mechanism
- clinical psychology, abnormal psychology, personality psychology, narcissism, personality disorders, or another clinical / personality construct
- a clinical population or clinical outcome
- diagnosis, treatment, healing outcome, symptom reduction, or behaviour change
- AI interpretation, AI detection, or computational inference of emotional state
- a causal link between one gradient position and one specific behaviour
- a claim that one research field proves the four modes or the 14 Formation-state positions as a complete taxonomy
- a claim that control, shutdown, manipulation, safety, or threat can be reliably identified from external behaviour alone

## Avoid

Do not write:

- "science proves TEG-Blue"
- "research validates TEG-Blue"
- "clinically validated framework"
- "science-backed framework" if it sounds like whole-system validation
- "Panksepp / Porges / Bowlby / Damasio / any named source already said TEG-Blue"
- "the citations are the foundation" without naming the integration
- "the integration is the whole contribution" without naming the research debt
- "TEG-Blue can read someone's nervous system"
- "TEG-Blue can identify someone's true state"
- "TEG-Blue can identify narcissism or personality disorders"
- "ESS and CLS are two literal brain systems"
- "the body always knows the truth"
- "cognition is automatically more accurate"
- "this gradient position means this person has a personality disorder"
- "TEG-Blue diagnoses"
- "TEG-Blue treats"
- "TEG-Blue predicts behaviour"

## How To Write The Relationship

Use:

- grounded in science
- established research
- research areas behind the map
- helps illuminate
- underwrites
- load-bearing
- draws from
- integrates
- synthesises
- makes visible
- places the parts in relation
- contributes the architecture

Prefer:

> This field helps illuminate this part of the map.

or:

> This research area helps underwrite this mechanism, capacity, or pattern.

or:

> TEG-Blue places these parts into relation; that integration is its contribution.

Avoid:

> This source proves TEG-Blue.

or:

> Science says TEG-Blue is true.

or:

> This research validates the whole system.

## Non-Collapse Principle

Do not write as if a source already says what TEG-Blue says.

A source may underwrite one part of the architecture without supporting the entire framework. A theory may help explain a mechanism without sharing TEG-Blue's terminology. A discipline may provide evidence for one layer of the sequence without naming the full pattern.

The sources describe parts from their own fields.

TEG-Blue places those parts into relation.

That relation is the architecture.

That architecture is the contribution.

Use this when someone asks whether TEG-Blue is scientifically validated:

> Not as a whole framework.
>

>
> Public claims should stay specific: this source helps underwrite this part of the architecture.

## Practical Test

Before using a source, answer:

1. What does this finding establish?
2. Which part of TEG-Blue does it underwrite?
3. What mechanism, condition, capacity, or pattern does it make visible?
4. Where does it sit in the sequence?
5. What other findings does it connect to?
6. What would be overstated if we cited it too broadly?

Before using `grounded in science`, answer:

1. Does the sentence also make clear that research areas illuminate parts of the map?
2. Does it avoid implying whole-framework validation?
3. Does it preserve the distinction between established research and TEG-Blue's integration?
4. Does it avoid diagnosis, treatment, external-state certainty, and outcome claims?
