The Foundations are not themselves scientific claims. They are the identity,
premises, posture and ethics under which every scientific claim in this
structure is made. Their grounding is therefore a set of rules rather than a
set of sources.

## The rules

- **Three evidence levels** govern every grounding file in this structure:
  established evidence, grounded synthesis, TEG-Blue integration. Stated in
  [notes/20-working-architectural-language.md](notes/20-working-architectural-language.md).
- **Research-to-application chain**: established field → finding → function
  → position in the architecture → Engine structure → public translation →
  context-specific tool. Stated in
  [notes/02-established-research.md](notes/02-established-research.md),
  which also holds the safe public claims, the claims needing citation or
  audit, and the avoid list.
- **Claim-level classification** within Level 1 is governed by the Science
  Grounding Protocol, which stays in the frozen working repository until
  `evidence/` is transferred.
- **The ethical constraint** on every claim, mechanism does not erase impact,
  is stated in [notes/04-ethics-and-responsibility.md](notes/04-ethics-and-responsibility.md).
