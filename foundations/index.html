What TEG-Blue is, and the rules it works under.

TEG-Blue is The Emotional Gradient Blueprint: a layered visual framework that maps how emotions, nervous systems, survival strategies, identity, social patterns and repair capacity form and evolve. It shows how patterns that begin in the body can shape individual experience and relationships across safety, threat, control, shutdown, regulation and repair.

Identity

A way to read emotional patterns as structured biological and relational information.

Compact formulation

TEG-Blue is The Emotional Gradient Blueprint: a layered visual framework that maps how emotions, nervous systems, survival strategies, identity, social patterns and repair capacity form and evolve.

Emotional Gradient: emotional and behavioural organisation moves across a gradient of whole-organism states rather than fixed categories. Blueprint: TEG-Blue maps the architecture of that gradient, what the body configures at each position, how patterns form, and what regulation and repair require.

What makes it TEG-Blue

Six pieces held together. The body and nervous system organise around safety, threat, protection, control, shutdown, regulation and repair. Emotional patterns are structured information, not noise, labels or automatic facts. Identity and survival strategies form through repeated patterns of organisation. Individual and social patterns are connected scales of the same gradient. Established research underwrites specific parts. The integration of those parts into a visual, usable gradient is the contribution.

What the Gradient does

It helps a person orient: what kind of pattern may be present; whether it is moving toward safety, threat, control, shutdown, regulation or repair; how visible behaviour may differ from underlying organisation; how feeling, interpretation, evidence and fact may separate or fuse; and where repair or clearer understanding may be needed. It distinguishes Fluid movement from Chronic rigidity. It is a tool for legibility, educational and reflective, not diagnostic.

Three scales

Personal: recognise your own states, and notice when your read of a situation is being shaped rather than informed. Relational: tell the difference between a boundary and quiet control; recognise a pattern before it has done its full damage. Systemic: see how the same patterns repeat in families, workplaces and institutions, and where they can be interrupted. Public language leads with the first two; systemic claims stay modest.

Positioning

The fields describe the parts. TEG-Blue places the parts in relation.

Established research across biology and physiology, affective and autonomic neuroscience, psychology, attachment, trauma and developmental science each makes one part of the pattern visible. The models people already know each answer their own question well. None was built to answer how the answers relate to each other. That relation is the architecture, and the architecture is the contribution. TEG-Blue replaces none of them.

Emotion distorted under survival

The same emotion carries different information depending on the organisation it arises from. Anger from safety and anger from chronic threat are not the same signal.

Harm that is aimed

Harm can be capacity failing: someone flooded, triggered, overwhelmed. That happens, and it is not the whole picture. Harm can also be repeated, selective and organised around another person's fear, hope or dependence. The two must never travel separately. Both come from one distinction: whether a pattern is Fluid movement the organism can return from, or Chronic organisation it is now living inside. Without that distinction every harm reads as capacity failing.

Not a ranking

People need therapy, and TEG-Blue does not replace it. Recognising a pattern is not recovering from it. Where someone needs clinical care, crisis support, legal support or safety planning, a map is not the thing they need. TEG-Blue does not tell you what to feel, what to do, or what to believe. It maps the territory; you choose how to navigate it.

The concept premises

Three premises that are one argument.

Premise

Emotions are information

Structured biological and relational information: real information, not automatic fact. Not vague feelings, moral labels, irrational noise or decorative intensity. When we ignore emotion we do not become more rational; we become less informed. Held in full by Emotions as Information.

First distortion

State shapes what feels true

Body and nervous-system states do not only change what people feel; they change what feels true. Under threat the system prioritises survival, protection, prediction, coherence and action over neutral fact-analysis. The feeling-fact collapse: "this is real inside me" becomes "this is therefore true outside me". A feeling can be completely real inside a person without being complete evidence about what is happening outside them.

Second distortion

Disconnection is not objectivity

When emotion and body state are unavailable as information, cognition can stay coherent while modelling the situation from incomplete data. "I do not feel it, therefore it is not real." "I can explain it, therefore I am in contact with the whole situation." Cognitive coherence is not reality contact. Reality contact is layered: internal, external, relational, contextual, state and repair.

The State-Reality Boundary

Keep the difference visible between what happened, what was felt, what the body predicted, what was assumed, what evidence is available, and what needs care, boundary, clarification or repair. The answer to either distortion is integration, not obedience to emotion or rejection of thought.

The four claims these premises hold together, and which Model 1 protects: emotion is information; state changes what feels true; emotional disconnection is not objectivity; mechanism does not erase responsibility.

Research posture

Grounded in science. Not validated as a whole.

Citation is not substrate

TEG-Blue does not connect research by collecting theories side by side. It asks what each field makes visible inside one larger pattern: field, finding, function, position in the architecture, relationship to the whole. A citation supports a sentence; established research underwrites part of the architecture; TEG-Blue places the parts in relation.

Three evidence levels

Established evidence: what independent research directly supports. Grounded synthesis: what can reasonably be concluded by bringing findings together; its strength is convergence and proportionate inference. TEG-Blue integration: the terminology and architecture that organise the synthesis. Do not demand Level 1 evidence for a Level 3 term; test whether Levels 1 and 2 responsibly ground it.

Working rule

Be rigorous about the evidence. Be flexible about the vocabulary we create to organise it. Define a term once, establish its evidence status, then use it consistently rather than weakening it with repeated hedging. Label where science ends, where grounded synthesis begins and where TEG-Blue takes responsibility for the integration. That boundary is not a flaw.

Safe to say

TEG-Blue is grounded in science and draws from several fields. Research areas illuminate specific parts of the map; each field remains itself. The integration is TEG-Blue's contribution. The Gradient is educational and reflective, not diagnostic. Emotions can be understood as patterned biological and relational information. Nervous-system organisation shapes perception, emotion, behaviour and repair capacity. A Position is a coordinate, not a person type.

Never say

"Science proves TEG-Blue." "Clinically validated." "Porges or Panksepp already said TEG-Blue." "TEG-Blue can read someone's nervous system" or "identify their true state." "TEG-Blue can identify narcissism or personality disorders." "ESS and CLS are two literal brain systems." "The body always knows the truth." "TEG-Blue diagnoses, treats, or predicts behaviour." Any specific mechanism, marker, named theory, clinical construct or outcome needs citation or audit before public use.

Ethics and responsibility

The Gradient explains organisation, not innocence.

Core guardrail

TEG-Blue can explain the mechanism behind a pattern without excusing its impact or removing responsibility. Understanding why a pattern emerged does not make it harmless. This governs every concept, page, tool and applied build.

Two duties at once

Do not dismiss a feeling, body signal or distressed response merely because it needs interpretation. Do not turn feeling, calm, certainty, explanation or state language into proof of what happened, what another person intended, or who a person is.

Calm is not safety

Distress is not danger. Control may look calm, coherent and reasonable after causing harm, while the person harmed looks distressed, angry or unstable because their nervous system is responding to pressure, threat or unrepaired harm. Control often makes its impact visible in the person being controlled first.

Protection is not manipulation

Nervous-system protection names a defensive pattern organised around threat, survival, control or shutdown; it can still cause harm, and the harm must be named. Intentional manipulation names a strategic pattern of using another person's perception, vulnerability, dependence, fear or hope to control their choices. They can overlap. Do not collapse deliberate control into protection, or protection into moral condemnation.

Responsibility scales

With awareness, repetition, power, available choice, refusal to repair, and impact on others. Keep harm attached to behaviours, patterns, mechanisms and conditions, not to people as fixed moral categories. Reduced access to emotion, empathy or self-awareness may explain why a pattern persists; it does not transfer the cost of change to the person affected.

Repair, and the reader contracts

Repair is not only apology. It means a pattern becomes clear enough that shared reality, responsibility, care, boundary and changed behaviour can return, which requires separating what happened, what was felt, what was assumed, what evidence is available, what rupture occurred, and what needs responsibility.

The same mechanism is translated differently by reader position. Whole-system, science-facing: Position as coordinate not identity; source support distinct from integration; composure is not evidence of safety. Self-facing: "I" and "my" framing; no labelling of emotions or prescribing the right action; self-reflection never becomes diagnosis of another person. Harm-facing: preserve the asymmetry of power, cost and agency; describe observable patterns before explaining the acting person's mechanism; never ask the person receiving harm to empathise with, regulate or repair the person causing it. Explanation must strengthen recognition; it must never relocate responsibility onto the person living with the harm.

Origins

The structure rose in resolution; it did not replace itself.

The mode count

Two modes (Connection, Protection) were the public entry by November 2025. Four modes (adding Control and Oppression) existed at the same time. Seven positions times Fluid and Chronic, fourteen formations, arrived with the Engine in July 2026. Each stage adds resolution; the four modes are a compression layer over the seven positions.

Maps first

The diagrams preceded the writing

Every diagram predates the written frameworks by about five months. The frameworks were written up from the maps, not the other way round. The map register records what each diagram first showed, where the idea lives now, and what was dropped, so a set-aside idea is not mistaken for one never had.

Renamed

Acute became Fluid

The earliest statement of the central distinction said acute is state movement and chronic is state rigidity. The rebuild renamed acute to Fluid organisation, bounded Chronic organisation into ten routes, and kept acute as the research term for event timescale only. The concept is quoted from the old text; the current terms come from the glossary.

Boundary

What TEG-Blue is not.

Not therapy, coaching, diagnosis, treatment or medical care. Not a claim that one discipline already contains the full account. Not a personality typology. Not a moral ranking of people. Not a way to identify someone's true internal state from the outside. Not a way to excuse harm because a mechanism exists. Not only a website, map, diagram, brand or educational project. Not an AI-aligned visual system. Not a bundle of citations used as symbolic authority.
