## Why this file exists

ESC needs evidence for particular claims, not one citation that appears to validate the whole cycle. This decomposition separates the temporal architecture from the biological, experiential, learning, relational and ethical claims that participate in it.

The cycle is therefore treated as a **TEG-Blue temporal model**. Research may directly study appraisal, interoception, regulation, recovery, avoidance, learning or social buffering without directly testing the complete ESC sequence or its named Fluid and Chronic forms.

## The governing Fluid–Chronic distinction

The repository already differentiates Fluid and Chronic grounding. ESC inherits that distinction; it does not redefine it.

| Cycle reference | Scientific question | Unit and timescale | What the evidence must show | What must not be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Fluid Cycle** | How does organisation change while present conditions change, and can the response use feedback, complete what is required and relinquish protection? | Within-person event and immediate recovery | Condition-sensitive change, feedback use, response flexibility, differentiated recovery and renewed availability | Calmness, one universal sequence, full conscious access or one completed physiological marker |
| **Chronic Cognitive-Override Cycle** | How can emotional-somatic information remain biologically active while becoming less consciously usable, less influential than an established cognitive model, or repeatedly carried into later conditions? | Repeated episodes to developmental time | Specific routes involving weighting, access, interpretation, avoidance, reconstruction, persistence, learning and interrupted recovery | That ESS is absent, CLS literally takes over, ME deliberately chooses omission, present danger is unreal or all Chronic organisation follows cognitive override |

The two references may contain the same broad functions. Their difference lies in responsiveness, weighting, access, recurrence and updating—not in two separate anatomies.

**Vocabulary decision:** within ESC, **Fluid Cycle** is the primary model name for what earlier material may call the **acute cycle**. The terms are related but not scientifically identical: *acute* names an event timescale, while *Fluid* names responsiveness to present conditions and feedback. Scientific source records should therefore retain “acute” when that is the study design and should not treat acute duration alone as evidence of Fluid organisation.

## Ownership and handoff rules

1. **Model 3 owns the temporal sequence.** It shows what changes, feeds back, completes, carries over and enters the next condition.
2. **Model 1 owns ESS, CLS and ME.** ESC can show their participation across time but must not redefine their functions or turn them into anatomical modules.
3. **Model 2 owns present organisation.** ESC receives Position, Fluid–Chronic depth, resource allocation and capacity access; it does not assign a Position from one step in the cycle.
4. **Regulation is continuous.** It includes adaptive allocation during demand as well as later loosening, restoration and updating.
5. **Completion is event- and demand-specific.** It is not synonymous with calm, discharge, catharsis, symptom disappearance or whole-organism recovery.
6. **Behaviour remains a separate evidence plane.** An organising pull or chronic route does not establish action, intent, harm, power or responsibility.

## Evidence labels

Each claim uses the repository’s two-axis convention:

- **Relationship:** `DIRECT`, `BRIDGE`, `INDIRECT` or `TEG-BLUE`.
- **Strength:** `A · CONVERGENT`, `B · SUPPORTED`, `C · PRELIMINARY`, `M · MIXED` or `U · UNTESTED`.

The label applies to the claim as phrased here. It is not a rating of the whole source family.

## Claim notes and scientific boundaries

### ESC-01 · Temporal process architecture

- **What research can ground:** dynamic and multicomponent emotion processes; feedback-sensitive regulation; within-event response and recovery trajectories.
- **What remains project-owned:** the exact ordering, naming and recurrence of the full ESC assembly.
- **Do not infer:** that every event moves through every step once, in a fixed order or with conscious access.

### ESC-02 · Conditions register and systems participate

- **What research can ground:** internal sensing, appraisal, component differentiation, bidirectional brain–body communication and context-sensitive evaluation.
- **Required correction:** “ESS evaluates while CLS interprets” is a useful educational distinction, not a biological finding that the organism runs two exclusive evaluation systems.
- **Do not infer:** one anatomical location for ESS, CLS or the Conditions Register.

### ESC-03 · Regulation continues

- **What research can ground:** stability through change, anticipatory allocation, context-sensitive strategy selection and multi-timescale stress response.
- **Required correction:** regulation can include mobilisation, protection, inhibition and resource prioritisation when those functions fit the demand.
- **Do not infer:** that regulation means low arousal, social compliance or successful conscious control.

### ESC-04 · Fluid responsiveness

- **What research can ground:** variation with present conditions, flexible deployment of responses, use of feedback, discrimination and recovery.
- **Required comparison:** within-person change across specified conditions and time points.
- **Do not infer:** that Fluid means calm, pleasant, healthy in every respect or free from cost.

### ESC-05 · Information is not the same as access

- **What research can ground:** distinctions among sensing, representation, attention, report, felt clarity, differentiation, interpretation and action use.
- **Open question:** how emotional-somatic information becomes consciously available to ME in the exact TEG-Blue architecture.
- **Do not infer:** that lack of report means lack of biological representation, or that strong feeling means accurate interpretation.

### ESC-06 · Chronic Cognitive Override

- **Proposed process:** ESS continues to participate, but its information may be poorly accessible, weakly differentiated, discounted or incorporated only after an established CLS account has organised meaning and response.
- **Governing research programme:** the [Science Grounding Protocol](../../evidence/protocol.html#override-title) requires “being taught to override emotion” to be separated into signal generation, bodily representation, subjective access, expression, regulation, action and emotion belief before any Chronic process claim is rewritten.
- **Candidate research bridges:** emotion socialisation, interoceptive access, emotion–cognition interaction, experiential avoidance, intellectualisation, belief maintenance, emotion differentiation, narrative coherence and metacognitive revision.
- **Current boundary:** no source in the intake pack directly demonstrates that “ME omits ESS and uses CLS because ESS is treated as noise.” The formulation is presently a **TEG-Blue hypothesis** requiring decomposition.
- **Do not infer:** deliberate refusal, absence of emotion, superior rationality, false perception, one chronic mechanism or reduced responsibility.

#### The seven distinctions that must remain visible

| Research layer | Question for the Chronic Cycle | Why ESC must keep it separate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Signal generation** | Did emotional significance arise? | A later cognitive account does not establish that ESS failed to activate. |
| **2 · Bodily representation** | Was internal bodily information represented? | Non-report or low felt clarity does not prove absent biological participation. |
| **3 · Subjective access** | Could ME feel, identify or differentiate what was happening? | Access may narrow even when information remains biologically influential. |
| **4 · Expression** | Could the information be shown, spoken or made visible? | Concealment, inhibition and non-expression are not the same as non-feeling. |
| **5 · Regulation** | Was the response modulated, suppressed, amplified, avoided or repeatedly reconstructed? | Each route may change the cycle differently and needs different evidence. |
| **6 · Action** | Could emotional-somatic information influence boundaries, choice or response? | Information can be present without becoming usable action guidance. |
| **7 · Emotion belief** | Was emotion treated as acceptable, safe, useful, dangerous or mere noise? | This is the closest layer to the proposed “ESS is noise” formulation, but belief must not be used as evidence that the earlier layers were absent. |

Every layer also requires context: who responded to the emotion, how consistently, under which cultural and material conditions, and whether repair or contradictory experience became available.

#### Bias and belief-maintenance processes to decompose

These routes may help explain how an established CLS account remains influential when feedback arrives. They do not constitute one mechanism, and none establishes Cognitive Override by itself.

| Research route | ESC question | Initial primary study to assess | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Confirmation bias / asymmetric updating** | Is evidence that agrees with the current account used more strongly than evidence that contradicts it? | [Kappes et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-019-0549-2) | A task-specific asymmetry in confidence updating does not demonstrate omission of ESS information. |
| **Disconfirmation bias / biased assimilation** | Is contradictory evidence scrutinised, reinterpreted or discounted more than compatible evidence? | [Lord, Ross and Lepper 1979](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.37.11.2098) | Findings about strongly held social attitudes cannot be silently generalised to emotional-somatic processing. |
| **Belief perseverance** | Does an account persist after its original evidential basis has been weakened or discredited? | [Ross, Lepper and Hubbard 1975](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1185517/) | Persistence does not prove irrationality, pathology or resistance to all forms of feedback. |
| **Interpretation bias under ambiguity** | Are ambiguous conditions more readily resolved through an established threat-related or otherwise congruent interpretation? | [Mathews and Mackintosh 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11195984/) | Experimental interpretation effects are context- and task-dependent and do not establish one stable personal bias. |
| **Attentional selection and disengagement** | Which cues receive attention, which are missed and which remain difficult to disengage from? | [MacLeod, Mathews and Tata 1986](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3700842/); [Kappenman et al. 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4255626/) | Threat-related attention findings are component- and measure-specific; dot-probe scores must not be treated as a universal trait marker. |
| **Mood- or state-congruent retrieval** | Does current organisation alter which memories, expectations or interpretations become easiest to retrieve? | [Kenealy 1997](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9225625/) | Retrieval accessibility is not memory truth, and available external cues can change retrieval. |
| **Motivated reasoning / motivated scepticism** | Do identity, role, goal or desired coherence change how supporting and opposing evidence is evaluated? | [Taber and Lodge 2006](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-5907.2006.00214.x) | Political-attitude experiments do not establish a general emotional-override mechanism or a person’s motive in one event. |
| **Cognitive-dissonance reduction** | Can an account change to become more coherent with an action already taken or a role already enacted? | [Festinger and Carlsmith 1959](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0041593) | Dissonance is a supporting coherence process, not a bias label, physiological state or sufficient explanation of behaviour. |

The proposed ESC handoff is: **current organisation shapes attention and retrieval → ambiguity is interpreted through an available CLS model → supporting and contradictory evidence may receive unequal weight → the account either revises or perseveres → the outcome contributes to completion or carryover.** Each arrow requires its own evidence. Prior knowledge, caution and stable belief can be adaptive; “bias” should be used only where a specified task demonstrates systematic selection, interpretation, retrieval or updating.

### ESC-07 · Completion

- **Working definition:** the demand-specific transition at which a response has done enough, the relevant condition has changed, or continuing recruitment is no longer required in the same form.
- **Possible indicators:** condition change, action completion, feedback uptake, reduced response demand and availability of alternative organisation. No indicator is sufficient alone.
- **Open question:** whether completion is scientifically useful as a distinct construct or should remain a TEG-Blue functional term.
- **Do not infer:** cathartic discharge, emotional expression as a requirement or whole-system restoration.

### ESC-08 · Carryover

- **Possible routes:** continuing conditions, learned weighting, generalisation, context-bound retrieval, constrained action, habit, perseverative representation, prolonged response, interrupted recovery and social transaction.
- **Required correction:** carryover can be accurate protection under continuing demand; it does not automatically mean mismatch with the present.
- **Do not infer:** a single residual substance, unfinished emotion or failure of will.

### ESC-09 · Relief is not necessarily updating

- **What research can ground:** avoidance can be reinforced when it reduces immediate distress; older learning can remain available after newer learning; context affects retrieval.
- **Do not infer:** that every withdrawal or relief-seeking action is avoidance, maladaptive or chosen without constraint.

### ESC-10 · Repetition and cumulative cost

- **What research can ground:** several forms of prolonged or repeated physiological activation, perseverative cognition, interrupted recovery and multisystem burden.
- **Do not infer:** one allostatic-load score, inevitable damage or a diagnostic chronic physiology.

### ESC-11 · Relational routes

- **What research can ground:** social buffering and interpersonal modulation of stress responses in particular species, developmental periods and relationship conditions.
- **Required correction:** social presence, synchrony and dependence are not equivalent to safety, care or consent.
- **Do not infer:** that regulation must be interpersonal or that reconnection is always the appropriate next step.

### ESC-12 · Return and recovery

- **What research can ground:** channel-specific recovery trajectories and delayed physiological recovery after stressors.
- **Project integration:** Return also includes renewed access, updating and relational repair where relevant; these are not reducible to physiology.
- **Do not infer:** one baseline, one clock or full completion from one measure.

### ESC-13 · Learning and integration

- **What research can ground:** new learning, context-sensitive retrieval, outcome updating, changed action–outcome expectation and the persistence of earlier learning.
- **Bias handoff:** confirmation, disconfirmation, interpretation, attention, retrieval and belief-perseverance research may help specify why available feedback does or does not revise a working CLS account; these routes must remain distinct from one another and from learning itself.
- **Framework handoff:** this claim gives F8–F12 a model-level base without making learning or integration one final step that every cycle must achieve.
- **Do not infer:** that exposure to new information guarantees learning, or that insight alone changes emotional-somatic weighting.

### ESC-14 · Behaviour, harm and responsibility

- **Governing rule:** internal organisation changes the conditions of participation; it does not specify what a person does.
- **Separate assessment:** behaviour, impact, power, available alternatives, feedback and repair require their own evidence.
- **Do not infer:** innocence, blame, danger, coercion or moral character from Fluid or Chronic cycle form.

## Priority evidence work before active-page promotion

1. **ESS accessibility and ME · first pass completed:** the review now distinguishes biological representation, task performance, felt access, differentiation, report, expression and action use. Direct within-person work across these layers remains a gap.
2. **CLS dominance / Cognitive Override · first pass completed:** the protected seven-layer programme and feedback routes have been assessed with supporting, mixed, limiting and correction evidence. The exact ESS–CLS–ME sequence remains **TEG-BLUE–U**.
3. **Completion:** test whether the term adds a defensible functional distinction beyond response termination, recovery, goal completion and updating.
4. **Carryover:** decide whether it is one umbrella term or several named persistence routes.
5. **Somatic and relational substitution:** do not add these as ESC mechanisms until they have their own claim decomposition and boundary review.

The first two priorities are now sufficiently developed for the public two-cycle prototype to retain Cognitive Override as an explicitly labelled **working TEG-Blue route**. The pass does not justify presenting it as an established biological mechanism, inferring it in a person or using one cognitive-bias result as evidence that ESS was omitted.
