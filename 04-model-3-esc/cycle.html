01 · Definition

One recurring event model with two forms

ESC is not another organ, nervous-system state or information system. It is the temporal model that follows what happens across one event and how that outcome enters the next.

Current condition → conditions register → ESS, CLS, ME and Gradient organisation participate → response → feedback → updated situation read → completion or carryover → Return, recovery, updating, learning and integration → next condition.

These are functional distinctions inside a recursive process, not fixed biological stations. Processes overlap, feedback can arrive at several points, and different parts of the organism can update at different rates. In this model, Chronic Cycle names the recurrent cognitive-override form—not every possible cause of persistent nervous-system organisation.

Not calm versus distressedFluid mobilisation can be intense and adaptive.
Not expression equals completionPressure reduction does not by itself establish updating, Return or recovery.
Not physiology equals behaviourOrganisation shapes the available field; ME still participates in what happens next.

Completion pathway

The relevant demand is sufficiently met, changed, escaped, repaired, recognised or accurately known.

  • Signal priority and prediction can update.
  • The previous recruitment is no longer required in the same form.
  • Return may begin before recovery is complete.
  • Recovery and restoration replenish and repair across their own timescales.
Carryover pathway

The condition continues, remains unmet or the coupled configuration does not sufficiently update.

  • Unresolved demand or residual activation may remain.
  • Prediction, response route and recruitment threshold may carry forward.
  • Reduced capacity and physiological or relational cost may enter later events.
  • Continuing danger may keep protection accurately recruited.
Completion and carryover can coexist. One part of the condition may resolve while another demand, cost, relationship or recovery process remains active.
Scientific trajectory bridge

Carryover is not one central system remaining switched on. A condition may continue; one channel may recover slowly; later demand may interrupt recovery; cognition may reconstruct the event; or learning may change later recruitment. The Signal Recruitment, Persistence and Recovery map connects these ESC outcomes to their scientific terms and measurement boundaries.

↓
Recursive closeNext conditions → next cycle

Updating, learning and integration are not required to wait until recovery is complete. They may begin during feedback, remain partial, unfold later or fail to generalise across contexts.

05 · Two forms

The difference is responsiveness

Fluid does not mean calm, and Chronic does not mean false. The key question is whether ESS and CLS can remain jointly usable by ME and whether relevant feedback can revise processing and organisation.

Fluid Cycle

Information remains available for revision.

ESS, CLS and ME can remain sufficiently connected for current conditions and feedback to alter what happens next.

  1. Current conditions can change the read.Prior learning informs detection without wholly deciding it.
  2. Difference remains usable.Embodied and reflective information can agree, conflict or unfold at different speeds.
  3. ME retains more than one possible route.Action can be delayed, revised, supported or redirected as new information arrives.
  4. The response can meet the relevant condition.Action, evidence or relational response may change the situation—or confirm that protection is still needed.
  5. The next entry carries usable learning.Completion may occur; Return may begin; recovery and integration continue on their own timescales.
Fluidity is the capacity to update—not an assurance of comfort, success or a rapid return to baseline.
Chronic Cycle · Cognitive Override

ME builds coherence through CLS while ESS is treated as noise.

Emotional–somatic information is still generated, but it is unavailable, undifferentiated, distrusted or given too little authority to participate sufficiently in what ME understands and does.

  1. Carryover shapes the starting field.Prior prediction, residual demand and current Gradient organisation influence what becomes salient.
  2. ESS still contributes information.Emotional–somatic relevance continues to affect physiology, salience and action readiness.
  3. The information does not become sufficiently usable by ME.The issue is conscious usability and authority—not the disappearance of ESS processing.
  4. CLS supplies the coherent account.A narrative, rule, role or goal makes sense of the situation using what is available.
  5. The outcome may stabilise pressure without updating the cycle.The response route, cost and narrowed organisation become part of the next entry.
The route can be learned and automatic. It may feel like competence, duty, certainty, self-control or simply reality.
Central Chronic distinction

ESS is not absent. Its information is omitted from the working account.

ESS registers → its information has reduced usability → CLS supplies coherence → ME participates through that account → pressure may change without sufficient completion → carryover enters the next cycle.

“Omission” does not require a deliberate choice. CLS is not inherently false or harmful; the chronic problem is recurrent closed dominance that is difficult to revise with relevant feedback.

07 · The Chronic Cascade

Override changes participation, then feedback shapes what can carry forward

This is the working Chronic route. It preserves the shared event spine while locating Cognitive Override in the relationship among ESS information, CLS interpretation and what becomes usable by ME.

01 · Current condition registersESS generates emotional–somatic information while CLS interprets, predicts and models the situation.

Model 2 records the simultaneous organism organisation. Nothing here requires conscious awareness, one-to-one agreement or a fixed order between the participating processes.

↓
02 · Availability profileAcross generation, bodily representation, access, expression, regulation, action and emotion belief, ESS information becomes unevenly usable.

The seven distinctions are not stages. They identify where emotional–somatic information remains active, where it becomes constrained and where “emotion is noise” may become part of the CLS model.

↓
03 · CLS account + ME participationA rule, role, explanation, narrative or goal becomes the coherent working account.

ME participates through what is available. The account may be intelligent, protective or temporarily necessary; Chronicity concerns recurrent dominance that remains difficult to revise, not the existence of CLS or reflection.

↓
04 · Response + feedbackME responds; the body, action outcome, environment and other people provide new information.

Feedback may change the condition, confirm that protection is still needed, reduce immediate pressure, contradict the CLS account or fail to become safe and usable.

↓
05 · Updated situation readDid the relevant condition and coupled configuration update, or did only immediate pressure change?

Relief is real information, but it does not by itself establish completion, reinforcement, accurate interpretation or future recurrence.

↓
Outcome A · Revision becomes possible

Feedback changes the working configuration

ESS information becomes more usable, the CLS account revises, the condition changes or the Gradient can reorganise. The event may move toward completion, Return and recovery.

A Chronic route can become more Fluid; no person or cycle is permanently assigned to one form.
Outcome B · Carryover remains

Pressure changes while the recruiting configuration persists or reconstructs

Residual demand, learned weighting, expectation, route availability, reduced capacity or cost enters the next condition. Continuing danger may also keep protection accurately recruited.

Carryover is decomposable. It is not a stored substance, one unfinished emotion or proof of past adversity.
↓
06 · Next conditionThe next cycle begins from the updated—or carried-forward—information and organisation now available.

Repeated carryover can contribute to recurrent or more restricted Chronic organisation. Repeated revision can widen later responsiveness without guaranteeing one permanent endpoint.

CONDITIONDid the relevant internal, material or relational condition actually change?
ORGANISATIONCould recruitment update and could wider capacities become available?
TIME + COSTWhat recurred, what accumulated, and did the cost remain internal or move onto another person?

Behavioural boundary: the cascade does not infer coercion, predation or harm from physiology. Harm and escalation require their own evidence: behaviour, power, viable refusal, impact, response to limits, recurrence and repair. See Behaviour and Responsibility.

08 · Bridge to Model 2

How a later cycle can begin

These are three relationships between demand, organisation and recovery—not three kinds of person, moral categories or a simple scale from calm to distressed.

Opening condition 01

Physiological baseline

A resting configuration in which resources are not currently recruited for a specific demand.

ESC
No active cycle demand is being tested, though learning and prior organisation remain.
Gradient
Baseline is not itself one Gradient Position; it is a resting condition from which organisation can change.
Capacity
A wider range may be available, but this cannot be assumed from low arousal alone.
Opening condition 02

Fluid activation

An acute, event-timescale reorganisation of resources and capacities in response to present or predicted demand.

ESC
Detection, response and feedback are active; intense mobilisation may be adaptive.
Gradient
Organisation shifts toward the biological problem currently being solved.
Capacity
Some functions are prioritised and others temporarily constrained; updating and Return remain possible when conditions change.
Opening condition 03

Chronic-shaped entry

A recurrent, persistent or difficult-to-revise organisation shaped by carryover, learned weighting, reduced capacity, repeated reconstruction or continuing adverse conditions.

ESC
The cognitive-override relationship may be more readily reconstructed, while other Chronic routes remain possible outside this specific cycle model.
Gradient
Position, depth, recruitment thresholds and resource allocation may become narrower, deeper or more rapidly reconstructed.
Capacity
ESS readability, flexibility, recovery and integration may become more constrained—not necessarily absent or permanent.
Cycle outcomeCompletion, partial completion, continuing demand or incompletion closes the current pass.
→
Carryover + learningPrediction, ESS usability, route availability, thresholds, resources and cost may change.
→
Next organisationThe Gradient records the configuration from which the next cycle now begins.

The Gradient describes the organisation present at each moment. ESC explains how outcomes and learning can contribute to the organisation encountered later. Fluid activation becomes relevant to chronicity because of persistence, recurrence, restricted updating, accumulated cost or continuing conditions—not because mobilisation was intense. In research contexts, acute still names the event timescale; in the ESC model, Fluid names the cycle’s responsiveness to present conditions and feedback.

09 · Shared vocabulary

Different words own different changes

These processes can overlap, but collapsing them into “regulation” hides what actually changed and which model should describe it.

Organisation

Regulation

The ongoing organisation and allocation of biological resources and capacities in relation to current demands. It does not mean calm.

Condition + response

Completion

The relevant condition has been met, changed, escaped, repaired, recognised or sufficiently updated so the previous recruitment is no longer required.

Reorganisation

Return

Movement toward a more flexible Gradient organisation when the previous demand no longer requires the same allocation of resources.

Restoration

Recovery

The replenishment, repair and settling processes that continue after or alongside Return. Recovery may take longer than the triggering event.

Changed expectation

Learning

Outcome information changes later prediction, weighting, access, skill, threshold or response availability.

Coordinated availability

Integration

Previously separated information, capacities or responses become more jointly accessible and usable by ME across later situations.

11 · Scientific boundary

The components have research homes

The complete two-cycle architecture and its exact assembly remain a TEG-Blue synthesis requiring claim-by-claim grounding.

Component evidence

Detection, mobilisation and recovery

Emotion processes, autonomic and endocrine recruitment, interoception, action readiness, stress-response termination, physiological recovery and learning.

Component evidence

Persistence and updating

Allostasis, perseverative cognition, state-dependent processing, reinforcement, habit, social buffering and context-dependent capacity restriction.

Supported interpretation

Relief and completion can diverge

Immediate pressure can change without establishing that the relevant condition, prediction or organism organisation has sufficiently updated.

Evidence plane

Behaviour and harm remain separate

No internal cycle configuration establishes intention, diagnosis, moral character, coercion, safety or what another person owes.

TEG-Blue synthesis / hypothesis

The shared ESC event spine, Fluid and Chronic Cognitive-Override comparison, seven-layer override audit, cascade structure, carryover formulation and exact interfaces among ESS, CLS, ME and the Nervous System Gradient presented here are theoretical model architecture—not established biological mechanisms in this assembled form. The focused Cognitive-Override evidence pass grounds the component distinctions and feedback routes while retaining that boundary.
