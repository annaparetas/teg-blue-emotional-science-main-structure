Signal Map · scientific bridge

Signal recruitment, persistence and recovery.

A signal does not simply switch on and off. The relevant condition may change while neural, autonomic, endocrine, immune, metabolic, cognitive and behavioural processes continue to change on different timescales.

Page responsibility · cross-model scientific bridge

Scientific-facing scope · response trajectories after biological recruitment begins

Governing questionOnce a condition has recruited biological activity, what may resolve, continue, recover slowly, recur, become reconstructed or change later recruitment?

This page owns

The translation between an observed trajectory and the scientific terms used to investigate it.

It receives

Recruitment hypotheses from Signal Neurochemistry, temporal structure from ESC, organisation and Return from the Gradient, and persistence routes from the Chronic scientific spine.

It does not establish

One stuck-on central system, one Chronic physiology, inevitable harm, or an emotion, mechanism or whole-person state from one measurement.

Completion is not a chemical event.

ESC uses completion for the event-specific point at which the currently required response no longer needs to remain recruited in the same form. Research may instead measure response termination, recovery kinetics, repeated recruitment, learning or cumulative multisystem cost. None of those measures alone proves that the event has completed for the organism.

01 · The missing bridge

One recruitment can lead into several trajectories.

The branches are not a ladder from healthy to unhealthy. More than one can occur in the same event, and different response channels may take different branches.

Current or represented condition Biological recruitment begins response + feedback

Condition changes

Recruitment can update

The required action, support or environmental change becomes available. Priority can fall while recovery continues.

  • response termination
  • channel-specific recovery
  • Return may become available

Condition continues

Recruitment remains relevant

Danger, pain, deprivation, uncertainty, relational rupture or demand may still be present. Persistence can remain condition-matched.

  • sustained demand
  • ongoing stressor exposure
  • restricted alternatives

Channels diverge

Recovery is uneven or interrupted

Some processes settle while others remain altered, or a later demand arrives before recovery has progressed.

  • delayed recovery
  • prolonged or repeated activation
  • blunted or poorly coordinated response

Later recruitment changes

Demand is reconstructed or more readily recruited

Anticipation, learned weighting, generalisation, context or altered thresholds can re-present significance after the original event.

  • perseverative cognition
  • generalisation or context-bound retrieval
  • sensitisation or non-habituation

Carryover is wider than residual activation. It can include an unresolved condition, altered physiology, reconstructed demand, changed expectation or narrowed action.

Chronic is wider than incomplete recovery. Continuing conditions, learning, development, social transaction and constrained action can each produce recurrence through different routes.

03 · Where allostasis fits

Adaptation, trajectory and accumulated cost are different questions.

Allostasis helps explain changing regulation under changing demand. It should not be asked to name every reason a response persists or every process TEG-Blue places inside Return.

Allostasis

Physiological viability can be maintained through changing activity across regulatory systems. The term concerns adaptive regulation, not only responses that fail to end.

Research-level regulatory principle

Response and recovery trajectory

A particular measure can rise, fall, remain altered, recur or become blunted. Different channels can follow different time courses inside the same event.

Episode- and measure-level observation

Allostatic load

Repeated, prolonged or inefficient patterns may contribute to cumulative multisystem cost. Current indices use heterogeneous biomarkers and do not reveal one causal route.

Longer-term multisystem construct
Allostasis ≠ failed completion  ·  allostatic load ≠ Chronic  ·  Return ≠ one baseline biomarker
