How to read this map

The diagram records which concepts and findings have been connected in this review. Select an emotion, chemical or sensory channel to inspect its links and open the underlying sources.

What does circle size mean?

Eigenvector centrality gives a larger score to a node connected to other well-connected nodes. This calculation uses 53 nodes and 115 undirected links, with equal weight for every recorded link. The largest score is scaled to 1; circle area follows that score.

Membership, candidate associations and measured null results all participate in that calculation. The score therefore describes the structure of this evidence map. It cannot establish biological importance, activation strength, causal direction or the strength of evidence. Hiding a layer leaves every score unchanged.

What has been reviewed, and what is still open?

The Belonging-at-Risk pass scoped nine entries and assessed 31 proposed chemical links: 7 human task or daily-observation findings, 7 null or conditional findings, 2 null results, 12 indirect links and 3 unresolved links. The unresolved links are Shame–noradrenaline, Guilt–dopamine and Disappointment–noradrenaline.

The remaining links retain their earlier source notes and limitations. This view omits Grief, Survival-Rage and the Body-condition signal roster; sensory channels shown here are a different layer. An omitted or isolated node is a gap in this map’s coverage.

The conditional membership notes remain working interpretations. The approved family definitions continue to own the site’s taxonomy. Sharing a chemical does not define a family.

How do evidence labels and Return questions work?

Each link keeps its own study conditions, source and limits. Where shown, DIRECT, BRIDGE, INDIRECT and TEG-BLUE describe the relationship to a claim; A, B, C, M and U describe evidence strength. Read the evidence protocol for the full definitions.

A reduction in pressure, a measured biological recovery and renewed usable capacities answer different questions. The Return prompts ask what has become available again in a particular episode. They do not infer a Gradient Position or a person’s organisation from an emotion.
