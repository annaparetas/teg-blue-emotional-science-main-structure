# Understanding one emotion in a new session

Start with the individual signal, then follow its explanations across the pages.
The map is an index of connected records. It is not a complete scientific account
of every signal. Read an open field as an unresolved question, not as evidence
that the process is absent.

## Read in this order

1. Read [Emotions as Information](../00-emotions-as-information/index.html) for the distinction between a signal, conscious feeling, interpretation and action.
2. Find the signal in the [Emotion Map](index.html). Open its [interactive record](map.html) or its section in [the generated signal records](signal-records.md).
3. Follow its exact Fluid and Chronic source links. Keep the signal's identity and family constant while comparing its participation. Body signals currently have no individual Chronic tables.
4. Read its Biology and Neurochemistry entry, including evidence status and limits. Family-level associations do not establish an individual emotion's mechanism.
5. Consult the model owner only for the question that needs it. Record what is still unresolved before drafting a synthesis.

The eight questions in each connecting record are a short reading interface to
[the existing analysis schema](notes/fluid-chronic-emotion-analysis-reference-schema.md),
particularly identity and function (Record A), physiological participation,
salience and felt access (Record C), Fluid and Chronic lanes (D–E), outcomes (G)
and ownership and evidence (H–I). They do not replace its deeper requirements.

## Keep two regulation questions separate

**What does the signal contribute to regulation?** Identify its registered
condition and proposed function. A function is not an instruction to act, and
is not by itself proof of evolutionary purpose.

**What regulates the signal's own participation?** Identify what may recruit,
sustain, reduce or redirect it, and what new information could update its
priority. Do not substitute a list of chemicals for this explanation.

Also keep pressure reduction, condition-specific completion, Return,
physiological recovery, repair and learning separate. A felt change does not
establish that all these outcomes occurred.

## Establish the status of each claim

- Approved architecture establishes the project's definitions, membership and ownership. It does not establish a biological mechanism.
- Working functional readings describe the current TEG-Blue account. Their presence in a complete roster is not evidence validation.
- Working grounding may be a family-level association, a system-level synthesis or a research hook. Preserve the stated level, context and measurement limits.
- “Separate account still needed” means the connected sources have not yet supplied that distinct explanation in the summary. Investigate the source before filling it.
- Love's [reference package](notes/love-reference-case-completion-and-public-boundary.md) is a structurally complete working example, not a template of answers for other emotions or public scientific authority.

When synthesising, name the signal, quote or paraphrase the relevant source
accurately, identify the conceptual owner and claim status, and state the
remaining gap. Do not infer a person's diagnosis, organisation, morality or
conduct from an emotion or its felt intensity.

## Maintain one source for each explanation

Edit the owning source page. The interactive map and `signal-records.md` are
generated views of those pages, not additional canon. Regenerate both with
`python3 scripts/build-signal-map-data.py` from the site root. Check them with
`python3 scripts/build-signal-map-data.py --check`, then run
`python3 scripts/check-local-links.py`.

New scientific explanations require emotion-specific review. Do not fill gaps
by copying another emotion's answer or promote a carried Engine note to site
authority. Record approved changes in [the decision log](notes/decisions.md) and
[STATUS](../STATUS.md); use [GLOSSARY](../GLOSSARY.md) for terminology.
