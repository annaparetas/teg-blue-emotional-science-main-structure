This file says what kind of claim each part of the map makes and where its
evidence lives. It does not restate the map. Read [index.html](index.html)
first.

## What kind of claim each level is

| Level of the map | Evidence level | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two signal groups (Body-condition, Emotion) | TEG-Blue integration | Distinguished by starting question, not by biology. Both recruit overlapping processes. |
| Five Body-condition families | TEG-Blue integration, provisional | Organising families over established regulatory physiology. Pain and tiredness are single-member families for now. |
| Essential-supply and Bodily-integrity records | Grounded synthesis | Each field rests on established regulatory and interoceptive research. Wording is TEG-Blue. |
| Four Emotion families | TEG-Blue integration, established within TEG-Blue | Membership is protected. The families are functional groupings, not established scientific categories. |
| Per-signal Fluid and Chronic readings | Working synthesis | Carried in for review. Individual records will be split out as each is reviewed. |
| Neurochemistry hooks | Grounded synthesis, papers pending | Individual pathways still require paper-level review. |
| Recruitment, persistence and recovery bridge | Grounded synthesis | Translates observed trajectories into the scientific terms used to investigate them. |

## Grounding pages in this folder
