## Governing observation

Love gives information about value. It does not establish relational status.

> **Love registers that a particular person carries deep emotional value and that connection with them is significant.**

Love does not by itself establish that the connection is reciprocal, safe, available or viable. It does not prescribe continued contact, closeness, forgiveness, sacrifice or any other behaviour.

## How to read the nine layers

The layers answer different questions about one signal. They are not nine stages through which Love must pass, and they do not describe a fixed causal or temporal sequence. Processes may overlap, recur or remain partly outside awareness.

Fluid organisation does not mean perfect access, complete factual accuracy or ideal behaviour. It means that relevant information and alternatives can remain sufficiently distinguishable and revisable for present conditions and feedback to change participation.

The identity of Love remains constant across the structure. Position, felt intensity, conscious access, interpretation, expression and outcome do not create or redefine it.

The [generic participation filter](nine-layer-emotional-signal-participation-filter.md) supplies the Model 1 overlay used here. Layers 1–4 can occur without becoming consciously available to ME. Layer 5 asks whether information becomes felt. Layers 6–8 examine recognition, interpretation and deliberate use while keeping automatic readiness and expression visible. Layer 9 follows response and feedback with or without conscious understanding.

## The relational role of another person

Another person's involvement must remain separated across several questions:

1. **Referent:** Love concerns a particular person. Their participation is not required for Love to exist.
2. **Internal acknowledgement:** ME may recognise Love without expressing it or receiving a response.
3. **Expression and reception:** Love may be communicated and understood without being reciprocated.
4. **Reciprocal relational aim:** Where a mutually recognised relationship is sought, another person's freely offered participation is required for that aim's completion.
5. **Feedback:** The other person's response supplies information about mutuality, availability, safety and viability. It does not determine whether the original Love was real.
6. **Conduct:** Love creates no entitlement to access, contact, compliance, loyalty, possession, forgiveness or continued relationship.

## Fluid event throughline

The following is an orientation, not a mandatory order:

```text
a particular person and connection carry deep value
        ↓
that condition acquires emotional significance
        ↓
distributed bodily and information processes participate
        ↓
some information may become represented, felt and differentiated
        ↓
ME may recognise Love and compare it with context and evidence
        ↓
ME chooses whether and how to participate
        ↓
the response and outcome become new information
        ↓
the relational read, present organisation and later participation may update
```

At every point, processes can overlap, remain partly inaccessible or change direction when new information arrives.

## Completion distinctions

Different parts of one event may have different outcomes:

- **internal acknowledgement:** ME recognises Love;
- **expression:** the feeling is communicated or deliberately kept private;
- **reception:** the other person understands what was expressed;
- **reciprocity:** the other person freely communicates whether they share the feeling or relational aim;
- **relational viability:** both people's wishes, boundaries and circumstances reveal what relationship is possible;
- **updating:** the response changes uncertainty, expectation or present organisation enough for the previous recruitment to change course;
- **recovery:** physiological, emotional or relational recovery may continue after the relational question becomes clearer.

A refusal can resolve uncertainty without providing the hoped-for relationship. Mutual Love can answer one question while incompatibility, repair or another condition remains open. Immediate relief does not by itself establish completion, and completion does not establish full recovery.
