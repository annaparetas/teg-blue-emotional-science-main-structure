## Decision

Love is approved as the first structurally complete Fluid–Chronic Emotion Analysis reference case.

The invariant held across both lanes is:

> **Love registers that a particular person carries deep emotional value and that connection with them is significant.**

“Complete” has a bounded meaning. The reusable fields are present, one Love identity is held across both lanes, the nine layers are filled, the direct comparison is visible, owner clauses are routed and empirical claims have placeholders.

It does not mean:

- the Nine-Layer lens is a validated biological sequence;
- the Love identity or proposed mechanism has completed evidence review;
- another emotion shares Love's content or response routes;
- cross-owner clauses have been accepted;
- the records are public authority;
- the public Emotional Signal Map may be changed.

The evidence passes attempted after checkpoint ddfdc9f produced no accepted findings and were deferred. The placeholders remain future work.

## ESM-owned clauses

The reference case settles these clauses inside ESM ownership:

- Love remains in the established Belonging family and relational-value working cluster.
- Love's working signal identity is held constant across Fluid and Chronic records.
- Emotion identity is distinct from Gradient organisation, conscious access, interpretation, expression, response route and outcome.
- Other emotions may coexist with Love without becoming Love.
- The same analytic fields are applied to both lanes.
- A response route is not an outcome.
- Emotion never prescribes or authorises behaviour.
- A feeling is informative but not a complete factual verdict.
- Public and development-only content must remain distinguishable.

The working Love identity still requires bounded evidence triage before public scientific authority is assigned. Settled ESM architecture and empirical validation are not the same decision.

## Outcome separation check

The completed Love records keep these outcomes separate:

| Outcome | Love reference-case distinction |
| --- | --- |
| **Pressure reduction** | Another person's response or a substitute route may change immediate pressure. |
| **Condition-specific completion** | Reciprocity, availability or viability may become clear enough for the event read to update. |
| **Return availability** | Present organisation may or may not permit transition toward a less costly configuration. |
| **Physiological recovery** | Bodily restoration may continue after a relational answer becomes clear. |
| **Repair** | Harm, accountability and changed conduct require the separate Behaviour/HPR record. |
| **Updating and learning** | Biological weighting and conscious understanding may change together, differently or not yet visibly. |
| **Carryover and later entry** | Unresolved condition, altered expectation, bodily demand and response history may enter a later event. |
| **Love's later course** | Love may remain, become less salient or coexist with another signal without transforming into it. |

None of these outcomes establishes all the others.

## Later public Emotional Signal Map candidate

The following material could become the first public worked example only after owner reconciliation, bounded evidence triage and separate implementation approval:

1. **The invariant Love statement** and a short explanation of what Love reports.
2. **What Love does not establish:** reciprocity, safety, compatibility, availability, consent, entitlement or another person's feelings.
3. **A simplified nine-question view** showing condition, significance, bodily participation, salience, felt access, differentiation, interpretation, possible use and feedback.
4. **A direct Fluid–Chronic contrast** that keeps Love's identity unchanged and describes Chronic as a change in access, weighting, flexibility and updating rather than a different emotion.
5. **The non-reciprocated Love example**, provided it retains consent and factual-verdict boundaries.
6. **The outcome distinction:** immediate relief is not necessarily condition-specific completion, Return, recovery or repair.
7. **A quiet conduct boundary:** Love can inform participation but never authorises behaviour.

“Public candidate” means eligible for later design and review. It is not publication approval.

## Material that must remain development-only

The following should not be copied into the first public worked example:

- owner-routing tables and session identifiers;
- CL-E01–CL-E16 placeholder codes and evidence-workflow notes;
- unreviewed claims about neural, autonomic, endocrine, allostatic or reinforcement mechanisms;
- the detailed substitute-route recursion;
- proposed developmental explanations;
- implementation gates, branch status and canonical-integration notes;
- cross-owner wording presented as if already accepted;
- Love-specific examples used as templates for other emotions;
- any claim that the nine layers are biological stages or a temporal ladder.

Some development-only material could later support specialist documentation after review. It should not carry the explanatory burden of the first public example.

## Release boundary

This package is canonical development architecture because its structure, content and unresolved claims are separated.

It is not ready for public implementation. The first public example should be smaller than the development record and should contain only the distinctions a reader needs to understand Love without mistaking it for reciprocity, safety, behaviour or outcome.
