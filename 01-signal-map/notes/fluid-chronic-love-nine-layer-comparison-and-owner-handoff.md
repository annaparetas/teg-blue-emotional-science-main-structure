## Decision

The nine-layer Love experiment is conceptually coherent enough to become the first complete Fluid–Chronic comparison source.

The comparison settles an ESM interface. It does not settle the biological mechanisms, developmental origins, Gradient mappings, event dynamics or evidence status supplied by other owners.

The invariant is:

> **Love registers that a particular person carries deep emotional value and that connection with them is significant.**

The comparison changes neither Love's Belonging-family membership nor its relational-value working cluster. It changes no other emotion's identity or membership.

## What the comparison establishes

Fluid and Chronic are not two kinds of Love.

- The **emotion identity** remains the same.
- The **present organisation and profile** may change what is weighted, accessible, distinguishable, revisable and available for use.
- **Conscious availability to ME** and **Possible ME participation** remain separate variables.
- Other emotions and bodily signals may coexist without becoming Love.
- Response and feedback may alter later recruitment without making one emotion transform into another.
- Neither lane predicts or authorises conduct.

## Classification axes kept separate

| Axis | What it asks | Must not be treated as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Signal group** | Body Signal or Situation Signal? | Emotion family |
| **Functional family** | What broad condition or relationship does the signal concern? | Gradient Position |
| **Working cluster** | What narrower functional relationship is being examined inside an established family? | A statistically established cluster or new family |
| **Individual emotion** | What specific information does this emotion contribute? | A complete verdict about events |
| **Present organisation** | How are organism-level resources and capacities organised now? | Emotion identity, personality, diagnosis or morality |
| **Fluid–Chronic profile** | How flexible, persistent, generalised and update-responsive is the pattern? | A binary person type or severity scale |
| **Conscious availability to ME** | What participating information becomes experienceable or inspectable? | Complete awareness or deliberate use |
| **Possible ME participation** | What can ME differentiate, question, interpret, express, inhibit, choose or learn? | Guaranteed accuracy or behaviour |
| **Response route** | What function may a response serve here? | Its outcome or ethical status |
| **Event outcome** | What changed: pressure, condition, Return, recovery, repair or learning? | Proof that all other outcomes occurred |

## Owner questions before public implementation

### Model 1

- Are the ESS and CLS contributions at each layer accurately bounded?
- Does Layer 5 remain the first explicit access question without implying one universal access moment?
- Do “Conscious availability to ME” and “Possible ME participation” stay meaningfully separate throughout?
- Can bodily-information, affective-sharing and mentalizing access vary as proposed without becoming Gradient Positions or person types?

### Model 2

- Which Layer 3, 4 and 8 clauses properly describe present organism organisation?
- Is “incomplete physiological restoration” sufficiently precise and bounded?
- How should action-readiness, controllability, transition and Return availability be expressed without importing temporal completion?

### Model 3 (ESC)

- How should event-specific completion, non-completion, feedback, carryover and later entry be represented?
- Which substitute-route relations can be retained as recursive possibilities?
- What is biological updating, what is conscious learning, and what remains unknown?

### Fluid–Chronic

- Which comparative terms describe a profile rather than a fixed state: persistence, repeated recruitment, generalisation, narrower routes, reduced updating and incomplete recovery?
- What observations would distinguish a recurrent Chronic pattern from an acute but proportionate Fluid response?

### F2

- Which developmental pathways may shape access, differentiation, meaning and familiar response routes?
- How should multiple pathways and present-day updating constrain any caregiver-origin explanation?

### Behaviour/HPR

- Where must the handoff separate internal pressure reduction from another person's impact?
- How should coercion, domination, accountability, stopping harm and repair remain visible without being recast as emotion mechanisms?

## Public implementation gate

Do not yet propagate the full Chronic Love record into the public Chronic Map.

Public implementation becomes appropriate only after:

1. the ESM identity and interface wording remain stable after Anna's review;
2. Model 1, Model 2, Model 3 (ESC) and Fluid–Chronic owners reconcile their clauses;
3. Behaviour/HPR confirms the conduct boundary;
4. a bounded evidence triage supplies clause-level status and limitations;
5. the public table can present the distinctions without implying a deterministic sequence, a fixed Chronic person type or a behavioural prediction.

The present result is valuable because the same Love signal can now be followed across condition, organism processing, conscious availability, possible participation, response and later course. What changes is no longer hidden inside the word Chronic.
