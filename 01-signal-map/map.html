Condition is not feeling. A record starts with what the organism may be registering; conscious access is one possible later step, not the measurement.
Foregrounding is not membership. A family may be especially readable under one Fluid reference position. The association never runs in reverse.
Relief is not restoration. Pressure can fall while the condition remains. Completion, updating, Return, recovery and repair are separate questions.
Emotion explains signal; it never authorises harm. Behaviour, power, impact and responsibility are assessed on their own evidence plane.
