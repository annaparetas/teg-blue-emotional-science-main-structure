Frameworks explain; they do not replace the models.

F1–F12 relationship

The frameworks are explanatory lenses that connect the models to biological history, development, social rules, valuation, bias, power, recovery, neurodivergence, intergenerational pathways and responsible integration.

Their numbering supports navigation. It is not a biological, developmental or moral sequence.
