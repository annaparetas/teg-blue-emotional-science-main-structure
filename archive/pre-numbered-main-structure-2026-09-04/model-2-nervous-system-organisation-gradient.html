What is the organism organised for now?

Model 2 working definition

The Gradient describes changing organisation of organism-level resources and capacities in relation to detected or predicted demands.

It owns present organisation, resource allocation, action-readiness, controllability, transition and current Return availability. It does not own emotion identity, behaviour or temporal outcome.

Non-inference rule

Organisation is not identity or conduct.

Never infer a person from a Position

A Gradient Position is not an emotion, behaviour, personality, diagnosis, moral category or biological worth scale. It shapes the field of information and action that may be available; it does not author, justify or excuse behaviour.
