Approved mapping

Four modes of organisation and participation.

ConnectionEvery Fluid position and route: X, A, A↔B, B, C, D and Z. Present-responsive awareness and participation remain sufficiently available. Connection does not mean calmness or Position A.
ProtectionChronic X, A, A↔B, B and Z. Protective organisation persists, narrows or becomes less responsive to present information.
ControlChronic C. Regulation is increasingly organised through managing uncertainty, conditions and outcomes.
DominanceChronic D. Internal organisation is structured as though asymmetric control over relevant others is the only viable route to stability, certainty or continuation.

Essential ethical boundary

Dominance is internal. Domination is observable.

Two-plane rule

Chronic D describes internal organisation. Domination describes what happens when power is used to reduce another person’s agency.

Dominance Mode does not establish Domination. Observable Domination does not establish Chronic D, physiology, personal history, concealed intent or diagnosis. When observing another person, name observable conduct, power and impact; do not assign their internal organisation.

Deferred decisions
