## Evidence level

TEG-Blue keeps three levels visible:

1. **Established evidence.** Emotion and interoception involve distributed
   neural, autonomic, endocrine, immune and metabolic processes; much of that
   processing does not become conscious; conscious feeling is not a direct
   measurement of the underlying condition.
2. **Grounded synthesis.** Treating emotion as information about the
   organism's relation to a condition, and feeling as conscious access to part
   of that process, is consistent with constructionist, interoceptive and
   predictive accounts without adopting any one of them.
3. **TEG-Blue integration.** The words *signal*, *body-condition signal*,
   *emotion signal*, *family*, and the emotion–feeling split as used here are
   TEG-Blue working language. They need not appear in Level 1 literature. The
   test is whether Levels 1 and 2 responsibly ground their territory.

The canon page is Level 3 language resting on Levels 1 and 2. No sentence on
it should be read as a Level 1 claim.

## Premises carried from the earlier foundation library

These were written between July and August 2026 in the older foundation files
(12, emotions as information; 13, emotional access and reality contact). The
canon page absorbs them. They are recorded here so the reasoning is not lost.

**The false binary.** Most readers arrive holding the opposite view: emotion
is the opposite of reason, noise, bias, distraction. TEG-Blue names that
directly. Emotion is not irrational; it is relational. It reports on what is
safe, dangerous, valued, and happening between people. Ignoring emotion does
not make a person more rational, only less informed.

**Real information, not automatic fact.** A feeling is real as an inner event.
Its source, scale and interpretation may still be wrong. Both halves must be
held at once.

**Two reality distortions.** Feeling-fact collapse: *I feel it, therefore it
is true.* Emotional-state disconnection: *I do not feel it, therefore it is
not real*, or *I can explain it, therefore I am in contact with the whole
situation.* Cognitive coherence is not reality contact. The answer to either
distortion is integration, not obedience to emotion or rejection of thought.

**Reality contact is layered.** Internal, external, relational, contextual,
state and repair. No single layer is the whole truth. Emotion without evidence
can collapse into certainty; evidence without emotional and relational contact
can become detached or incomplete.

**Survival before accuracy.** The system is built to keep the organism alive
first and accurate second. A feeling can be a true report of the body and a
poor report of the situation at the same time.

## Guardrails

- Do not use this concept to dismiss distress. That emotion needs
  interpretation does not make it meaningless.
- Do not use it to deny real danger, abuse, injustice, coercion or structural
  harm. Sometimes the body is detecting a real threat.
- Do not use it to make people distrust themselves. The aim is more accurate
  self-trust.
- Do not rank body over thought or thought over body. Both help; both distort.
- Do not force analysis or emotional access when someone is overwhelmed.
- Do not use reduced emotional access to excuse harm. Responsibility for
  impact and repair is assessed on its own evidence plane.
- Do not read "reality contact" as a clinical claim about psychosis or formal
  loss of reality testing.

## What TEG-Blue does not say

Emotion is irrational. Emotion is only intensity. Emotion is just a story the
mind creates. The body always knows the truth. If I feel it, it is fact. If
someone feels hurt, harm definitely happened. If someone feels unsafe, danger
is definitely present. Logic is better than emotion. Thinking is just a
defence. The goal is to be less emotional.
