## 2026-09-02 · Two signal groups, five Body-condition families

Approved: the Signal Map contains two groups, Body-condition signals and
Emotion signals, distinguished by their starting question. Five working
Body-condition families (essential supply, bodily integrity, thermal balance,
visceral capacity and elimination, restorative capacity). Family is an
organising level, not a biological identity. Essential-supply signals share a
problem, not a physiology.

## 2026-09-02 · Emotion and feeling distinguished

Approved: emotion names the organism-level process; feeling names conscious
access to part of it. ME does not create the emotion by noticing it. Conscious
access does not make interpretation factual.
