Reading rule

One concept, one folder, one canon page.

Open a concept folder and read its index.html. That page is the current site statement. Everything else in the folder is grounding for that page or notes behind it. Within the site, cross-references link to the owner instead of duplicating its definition.

Canon

index.html

What TEG-Blue says now. Opens with a status block: status, owner, last approved date, and what the page does not establish.

Grounding

grounding.md and grounding/

Evidence level, sources and the boundary of what the evidence supports. Never restates the definition.

Notes

notes/

Dated decisions, working drafts and carried source material. Notes may propose change; they do not override the canon page.

Status words

Three words, and only three.

Approved

Anna has approved the statement as TEG-Blue's current position. It may still carry open questions, and those are listed in STATUS.md.

Working

Under development or carried in for review. It may be structurally complete and still working. "Approved but not public" is working with a release note.

Archived

Kept for provenance only. It explains where an idea came from and is never current authority.
