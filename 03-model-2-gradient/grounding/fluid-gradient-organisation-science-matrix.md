# Fluid Gradient Organisation Science Matrix

Status: working comparison for conceptual and evidence review
Scope: Fluid Gradient only

Each column is a different nervous system organisation. Each row examines the same organisation through one functional science lens. These are characteristic patterns and working hypotheses, not diagnoses or unique biological signatures.

| **1 · Body organisation** | Restoration can predominate. Digestion, sleep pressure, tissue maintenance, pain, comfort, hunger and tiredness can become readable. Oxytocin is not love or proof of safety. | Noradrenergic, cholinergic, dopaminergic, HPA and other signalling may support vigilance, learning and updating. Direction and magnitude depend on expectation, controllability and prior learning. | Rapid sympathetic signalling and catecholamines support mobilisation; HPA/cortisol effects unfold on different timescales. | **4 · Timing pattern** | Low recruitment cost and flexible duration. Recovery can continue until a new demand, goal, bodily need or relational cue changes organisation. | Coordination can emerge rapidly, deepen through reciprocal evidence and remain available while cues support safety. ## Interpretive guardrails

- A Position describes coordinated whole-organism function, not a person, personality or diagnosis.
- The same mediator can participate in several Positions.
- No Position has a unique or diagnostic hormone, neurotransmitter, brain-region or autonomic signature.
- Intensity, duration, novelty, predictability, controllability, learning, health, medication, sleep and available support can all change the response.
- Fluid organisation tracks present conditions characteristically, but not mechanically or identically in every person.
- Low outward activation does not establish low internal cost, safety or completed recovery.
