This file says what kind of claim each part of Model 2 makes and where its
evidence lives. It does not restate the model. Read [index.html](index.html)
first.

## What kind of claim each part is

| Part of the model | Evidence level | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Changing allocation of resources and capacities under demand | Established evidence, drawn together | Homeostasis and allostasis; autonomic, endocrine, immune and metabolic regulation; stress-response coordination; threat imminence; salience and attention; motor preparation; interoception; social buffering; recovery; context-sensitive learning. No single field provides the whole Gradient. |
| Organisation as a coordinated, context-dependent field rather than a calm-versus-stressed switch | Grounded synthesis | Organisms cannot maximise every function at once; the same visible arousal can carry different meanings depending on fit, duration, action availability and outcome responsiveness. |
| The seven Fluid Positions as reference organisations | TEG-Blue integration | The letters and names are TEG-Blue working language. Each cell in the tables rests on a research domain named in the Grounding row; none is a discrete biological state. |
| The Chronic presentations | TEG-Blue integration over established routes | Sensitisation, generalisation, impaired safety learning, perseverative cognition, allostatic load, learned helplessness and related literatures ground the routes. Diagnosis does not define a Position. |
| Fluid and Chronic as reference orientations | Approved TEG-Blue integration | Individual dimensions and routes are supported; there is no universal Fluid–Chronic scale. |
| Persistence and flexibility profile | Proposed, revision required | Defined in the notes; not yet implemented. |
| Return as renewed flexibility | TEG-Blue synthesis | No one measure or research construct establishes it. Components map onto stress recovery, extinction and safety learning, social buffering, allostatic load. |
| Autonomic participation by Position | Working synthesis, papers pending | Functional shorthand. No pathway–state identities; "ventral vagal" and "dorsal vagal" are aliases, never mechanisms. |

Do not present a TEG-Blue synthesis as an established mechanism merely because
its components are individually supported.

## Grounding inside the tables

The Fluid and Chronic tables on [positions.html](positions.html) each end
with a Grounding row: one cell per Position naming the research domains that
ground it, the claim boundary, and candidate anchors (for example McEwen on
allostasis, Grupe and Nitschke 2013 on uncertainty and anticipation, Arnsten
2015 on acute stress and cognition, Ottaviani 2016 on perseverative
cognition, Kozlowska 2015 and Volchan 2017 on defensive immobility, Dichter
2018 and Hilton and Radatz 2025 on coercive control). Those rows are
orientation maps of relevant domains, not the final source trace. Row-level
grounding is open work.
