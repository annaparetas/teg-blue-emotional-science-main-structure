# Fluid–Chronic Profile and Visual Axis Decision

Status: proposed decision · revision required after combinatorial-field clarification · 19 August 2026
Scope: PEND-008 · NG-04 · `fluid-chronic-depth-model.html` · `fluid-to-chronic-inner-layers.html` · future Gradient controls
Purpose: define how the existing visual depth metaphor relates to the multidimensional persistence-and-flexibility profile established by the Chronic Scientific Spine

Companions: [Chronic Scientific Spine](../grounding/chronic-scientific-spine.md) · [Current Construct Claim Decomposition](../../evidence/claims/current-construct-decomposition.md#ng-04--fluidchronic-depth) · [Project Terminology Ledger](../../GLOSSARY.md)

## Decision in one sentence

Keep **visual depth** as the way the diagram shows movement between the Fluid and far Chronic reference planes, but use **persistence and flexibility profile** as the formal construct being described.

The visual axis is a map.

It is not the measurement.

## Later clarification · configurations are not middle layers

The bodily-information, affective-sharing and mentalizing controls generate uneven **capacity configurations**. Those configurations are not points or successive layers on the Fluid–Chronic axis.

Each configuration can have its own persistence-and-flexibility profile:

```text
capacity configuration × capacity quality × context × recurrence × use
```

For example, mentalizing-only access may be a temporary professional configuration, a context-specific protective solution or a recurrent and instrumentally used pattern. The capacity combination remains similar; its flexibility, development, adult maintenance and behavioural consequences differ.

The decision below therefore remains useful for defining profile dimensions, but its visual implementation must be revised alongside the [Relational Configuration → Clinical and Personality-Pattern Research Bridge](../../02-model-1-ess-cls-me/grounding/relational-configuration-clinical-pattern-bridge.md). No implementation should begin from the earlier profile-axis proposal alone.

## Why both layers are needed

A single visual direction helps people understand the broad movement from present-responsive organisation towards patterns that are more recurrent, narrowed, persistent or difficult to revise. Removing that direction would make the Gradient harder to read.

The science does not support converting this direction into one score. A pattern can be easy to recruit but quick to settle. It can generalise widely while leaving reflective interpretation available. It can remain context-specific but sharply restrict action. Continuing danger may sustain the response even when learning and updating remain accurate.

The model therefore needs two connected levels:

| Level | Purpose | What it can say | What it cannot say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Visual depth** | Orient the reader between two reference planes. | This pattern appears more present-responsive or more recurrently restricted along the dimensions currently visible. | The person is at one depth; every dimension has the same value; one total score exists. |
| **Persistence and flexibility profile** | Describe the dimensions separately. | Recruitment, weighting, context, access, action, persistence and recovery show this particular configuration. | The profile is a diagnosis, biological signature, moral ranking or complete explanation. |

## Recommended terminology

### Formal term

> **Persistence and flexibility profile:** a context- and time-specific description of how readily a pattern is recruited, how broadly it generalises, what information and actions remain available, how it responds to changed conditions and how recovery unfolds.

Use this term in grounding documents, claim records, explanatory notes and any future assessment method.

### Visual term

> **Fluid–Chronic visual depth:** the spatial metaphor used to display broad movement between present-responsive and recurrently restricted reference configurations.

“Depth” can remain in page titles and explanatory interface language when its visual role is explicit. It should no longer name the scientific construct by itself.

### Avoid

- depth score;
- chronicity score;
- nervous-system level;
- how Chronic a person is;
- deeper person or deeper state;
- progression from Fluid to Chronic;
- severity inferred from distance alone.

## The formal profile

The ten dimensions in the Chronic Scientific Spine should remain independently describable.

| Profile dimension | Core question | Direction is not automatically better or worse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Current demand** | What present bodily, relational or material condition is recruiting the pattern? | Sustained recruitment may correspond accurately to continuing danger. |
| **2 · Recruitment threshold** | What amount, clarity or repetition of information is sufficient to recruit it? | Rapid recruitment may be useful in a reliably dangerous setting. |
| **3 · Response magnitude** | How strongly do the relevant response channels change? | Strong response does not establish poor discrimination or Chronic organisation. |
| **4 · Generalisation breadth** | Across which cues, contexts, relationships or territories does it appear? | Broad learning may sometimes protect; narrow learning may also miss relevant danger. |
| **5 · Danger–safety discrimination** | How differently does the pattern respond to information associated with danger and safety? | This must be assessed separately from intensity. |
| **6 · Context sensitivity** | Does changed context alter recruitment, meaning or expression? | Context dependence can reflect accuracy, retrieval or constrained access. |
| **7 · Information and felt access** | Which bodily, emotional, relational and contradictory signals become usable? | More information is not automatically clearer or more accurate. |
| **8 · Action range and controllability** | Which responses seem possible, effective and safe under actual conditions? | Available action is constrained by capability, resources, consequences and power. |
| **9 · Persistence and outcome responsiveness** | Does changed evidence or changed outcome alter the pattern across time? | Persistence may reflect ongoing conditions rather than failed updating. |
| **10 · Recovery profile** | Which response channels settle, reorganise or become available again, and when? | Recovery is not one endpoint, compliance or forced calm. |

No total should be calculated. If a future tool needs comparison, it should compare the same dimension across time or contexts rather than add the dimensions together.

## How the ten dimensions fit the existing six visual variables

The current pages do not need ten equal cards. Their six variables can remain as the public-facing reading structure if the underlying dimensions are made visible.

| Existing visual variable | Formal profile dimensions carried | Recommended refinement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recruitment threshold** | Current demand · recruitment threshold · response magnitude | Rename the section **Recruitment profile** and show demand, threshold and magnitude separately. |
| **Information weighting** | Generalisation breadth · danger–safety discrimination · context sensitivity | Rename **Weighting and context**; do not imply that information simply becomes stronger or weaker. |
| **Felt access and differentiation** | Information and felt access | Retain, with the existing boundary between formation, access, identification and report. |
| **Interpretive flexibility** | Context sensitivity · information access · persistence/outcome responsiveness | Retain, but identify it as one contributor to updating rather than the controller of the profile. |
| **Action flexibility** | Action range and controllability | Rename **Available action and controllability** so structural and relational constraints remain visible. |
| **Updating, persistence and Return** | Persistence/outcome responsiveness · recovery profile | Rename **Updating and recovery profile**; show persistence and recovery as related but non-identical. |

This keeps the public view understandable while preventing the simpler display from becoming the scientific definition.

## What the visual axis should represent

The front-to-back direction should mean:

> **Broad orientation from present-responsive flexibility towards recurrent or persistent restriction across the dimensions being examined.**

It should not mean:

> time elapsed, trauma severity, physiological activation, moral seriousness, diagnostic severity or distance from reality.

The field should continue to locate **a pattern in a context and time**, never the whole person.

Recommended coordinate statement:

```text
context + organisation + processing layer + profile + time
```

Replace the earlier coordinate `depth` with `profile`. Visual depth remains how the profile is displayed, not an additional piece of evidence.

## The five visual layers

Retain five layers because they make the field navigable, but reclassify them as **reference configurations**, not stages or bins.

| Current label | Recommended label | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fluid reference | **Fluid reference** | Relevant dimensions remain sufficiently responsive to present conditions, changed outcomes and available recovery. |
| Recurrent bias | **Emerging recurrence** | A familiar route becomes easier to recruit or repeat, while substantial flexibility remains. |
| Learned restriction | **Context-bound restriction** | One or more dimensions narrow reliably within a defined territory, relationship or signal family. |
| Entrenched protection | **Cross-context persistence** | Restriction appears across more contexts or resists more kinds of corrective information, although dimensions can remain uneven. |
| Far Chronic reference | **Far Chronic reference** | A limit configuration in which several relevant dimensions show strong recurrence, restriction or poor updating; it does not require the original conditions to be absent. |

These labels describe common configurations. A real profile does not have to match one column completely.

## The most important correction to the far Chronic plane

Several current sentences define far Chronic as persistence “beyond the conditions that originally required it” or “without corresponding conditions.” That language excludes people whose danger, coercion, instability, pain or deprivation continues.

Recommended replacement:

> **Far Chronic reference:** a limit configuration in which several relevant processes have become strongly recurrent, restricted, persistent or difficult to revise. This may reflect earlier learning, continuing adverse conditions, repeated reconstruction, constrained action, interrupted recovery or several routes together.

The assessment of **current demand** must sit beside the profile. It prevents an accurate protective response to ongoing conditions from being misclassified as internal distortion.

## Recommended visual architecture

### Layer 1 · Keep the three-dimensional field

Retain:

- organisation across the horizontal axis;
- processing layer down the vertical axis;
- Fluid-to-Chronic visual depth between the reference planes.

Change the depth-axis label from:

> Depth · updating to restriction

to:

> Visual orientation · present-responsive to recurrently restricted

### Layer 2 · Show a profile beside the selected pattern

Selecting a reference configuration should reveal six public-facing profile groups. Each group can show several independent markers without producing a total.

```text
Recruitment profile        ○────●────○
Weighting and context      ○──●──────○
Felt access                ○──────●──○
Interpretive flexibility   ○────●────○
Available action           ○─●───────○
Updating and recovery      ○───────●─○
```

The marks represent a qualitative reading supported by context and evidence across time. They are not psychometric scores.

### Layer 3 · Open the formal dimensions progressively

Each public-facing group can expand to show the ten formal dimensions and their evidence questions. This gives the reader depth of explanation without presenting all ten variables at once.

### Layer 4 · Keep current conditions visible

Place a separate condition banner above every profile:

- continuing demand present;
- demand uncertain or mixed;
- demand has changed;
- insufficient information.

This banner is not another profile score. It is the context required to interpret persistence responsibly.

## Rules for controls and interaction

1. Do not create one Fluid–Chronic slider that claims to measure a person.
2. A visual layer selector may remain if labelled **reference configuration**.
3. Any editable profile controls must move independently.
4. Do not average the controls or convert them into one colour, number or diagnosis.
5. Always display the defined context and time period.
6. Allow “unknown” and “not assessed” for every dimension.
7. Show changed conditions separately from changed internal response.
8. Use comparison for the same pattern across contexts or times, not comparison between people.
9. Keep harmful behaviour, power and repair on their separate assessment plane.
10. Make clear that the profile is descriptive and educational, not a clinical instrument.

## Example profiles

### Same visible intensity, different organisation

Two people may both speak sharply during conflict.

- In one pattern, recruitment is strong but specific to an ongoing coercive interaction; discrimination and recovery remain available after safety is restored.
- In another, small cues recruit the response across several relationships; contrary information enters poorly and action remains narrowed after conditions change.

Visible intensity does not locate either pattern by itself.

### Same visual orientation, different routes

Two patterns may both sit near the far Chronic reference configuration.

- One may involve broad generalisation and context-bound retrieval of newer learning.
- Another may involve continuing danger, constrained action and repeated interruption of recovery.

The visual location is similar. The scientific and practical meaning is not.

## Decision boundaries

This decision does not:

- create a validated measure;
- establish thresholds between reference configurations;
- turn the profile into a diagnostic formulation;
- decide how trauma-specific evidence will alter F2;
- determine responsibility from internal organisation;
- authorise implementation before the terminology and visual relationship are approved.

## Proposed decision

Approve the following three changes together:

1. **Formal construct:** replace scientific-facing **Fluid–Chronic depth** with **persistence and flexibility profile**.
2. **Visual relationship:** retain depth only as a spatial metaphor for broad profile orientation; retain five layers as reference configurations rather than scores, stages or bins.
3. **Context correction:** make current demand a visible interpretive condition and remove every definition of far Chronic that requires the original danger or demand to be absent.

If approved, implementation should update the two depth pages, the project terminology ledger, NG-04 in the registry and decomposition, and later the active Gradient interface. The implementation should remain a separate commit from this decision record.
