Regulation and Return

Separate working file · extracted from the Gradient map for slower conceptual development · Read the governing model premise →

Provisional workbench. Nothing in this file is accepted into the main Gradient table. Its purpose is to preserve the questions and drafts while regulation, Return, recovery, restoration, and repair are reconsidered one distinction at a time.

Current proposition under review

Regulation is the continuous organisation and adjustment of energy, attention, perception, action, and available capacity in relation to changing conditions. Every Position may therefore be regulatory without “regulated” meaning calm, healthy, kind, accurate, or harmless.

Regulation
The proposed encompassing process running across Positions.
Return
The proposed movement that reopens baseline X through genuine Connection A.
Recovery
Physiological rebuilding and remaining cost across different timescales.
Restoration
The energy strategy associated with baseline X.
Repair
Impact, accountability, agency, and possible relational rebuilding after rupture.

Possible biological sequence

A working sequence to test, not an accepted series of stages or a universal clock.

01Condition or route changes
Has the demand or available response route actually changed?
02Signal receives update
Has the emotion received credible condition-relevant evidence?
03Recruitment can terminate
Can additional protective recruitment stop increasing?
04Return begins
Can the activated organisation begin releasing its hold?
05Capacity re-entry
Which awareness, reflection, and empathy capacities become usable?
06Connection A
Can regulation organise through genuine connection?
07Baseline X
Can lower-cost baseline access reopen while movement remains free?
Recovery may run alongside the sequence
Neural, autonomic, endocrine, immune, metabolic, muscular, pain, and sleep costs may recover at different rates.
Learning continues across later events
Re-accessing baseline once does not prove that the route will remain accessible.

Completion pathways under review

Environmental change
Danger ends, distance or shelter becomes available, or a material obstruction changes.
One’s own action
Protection succeeds, a boundary becomes effective, a task completes, or a goal changes.
Another nervous system
Recognition, reciprocity, clarification, co-regulation, accountability, or repair is genuinely relational.
Physiological recovery
The body receives rest, nourishment, treatment, sleep, or other restoration still required.
Grief and reorganisation
What cannot be restored is registered and life reorganises around the irreversible condition.
