Nine words that are not interchangeable

These words describe different parts of an event. Separating them prevents a signal changing, pressure falling or behaviour becoming quiet from being treated as proof that the whole organism has Returned.

Regulation
The ongoing organisation of energy, attention, perception, action and available capacity. Every Position is regulatory, so regulated never means calm, healthy or kind.
Return
Renewed availability of more flexible, less defensively dominated and more restorative organisation. Baseline X may become accessible without defining every Return route.
Baseline re-access
Renewed access to Baseline X where this Gradient architecture supports that description. One possible feature of Return, not a universal physiological set point.
Completion
Sufficient change in a particular condition, demand or task for the present recruitment to change course. It is demand-specific, not a whole-person endpoint.
Updating
Changed weighting, expectation, interpretation, readiness or future recruitment following new conditions or evidence. It may be partial or absent.
Recovery
The physiological rebuilding and remaining cost, across different timescales. It can still be running for hours after Return has begun.
Restoration
The energy strategy of Baseline X. The one organisation that rebuilds rather than spends.
Repair
Impact, accountability, agency, and possible relational rebuilding after rupture. Adjacent to Return, not contained inside it.
Carryover
Residual demand, changed weighting, depleted capacity, learned route or accumulated cost entering later conditions. ESC records it when the current pass does not end cleanly.

Cross-model handoff. The Signal Map names a possible completing condition. ESC records completion, updating or carryover through the event. The Gradient then asks whether Return becomes available and what recovery remains. These are linked observations, not a mandatory sequence. Completion and Return · what may change, what becomes available and whether another person is involved
Completion and relational participationWhere does the relevant condition exist, and who can change it? No other person is inherently required. Restoration can occur alone or with supportive company. Relationship is available. Actual or represented social availability may lower demand and widen participation; physical presence is not required in every event. The route depends on the uncertainty. Another person's response may be necessary for mutual clarification or repair; independent evidence, distance or a boundary may answer a different problem. Protection is not inherently relational. The organism may act, conditions may change or another person may provide practical help. Other people may be involved in several ways. Support, cooperation, obstruction or management can alter the demand without defining one completion route. No universal social rule. Maximum action may be solitary, coordinated or dependent on rescue; the present survival problem determines participation. Re-entry may need changed conditions or assistance. Social, medical or material help can matter without constituting one mandatory pathway.
Return route and renewed availabilityWhat may release the present recruitment, and what wider organisation can then become available? Restoration is already available. X is a reference condition, not evidence that every recovery channel is complete. The coordination demand may end while the value or support of connection carries forward. X may become accessible, but Return is not defined by a mandatory A → X route. Clarifying information may open connection, recruit active protection or permit grief and reorganisation. No one intermediate Position is mandatory. Changed conditions, effective action, assistance or new evidence can release immediate protection and open Return. Reduced capacity may instead move toward Z. The threat or need for management must change; exhaustion alone is not updating or Return. No passage through A is required in every event. Return opens when maximum force is no longer required and sufficient capacity remains. Depletion may move organisation toward Z. Lower output is not yet Return. Orientation, responsiveness and sufficient usable capacity must re-enter; the route is not fixed through A.
What endsWhat specifically stops when this organisation releases? No defensive recruitment needs to end. Particular demands may still change, and recovery can remain incomplete. The coordination demand: sustained attention to face, voice, timing and another person’s state. The demand ends; the company need not. What A gave carries into X — the lowered defensive load and the established belonging. The checking. Orienting closes when the information becomes sufficient. The mobilisation. Energy raised for a protective route is no longer being held ready. The management. Variables stop being tracked, predicted and held open. The force. Maximum output stops, well before its cost is recovered. Nothing ends. Conservation lifts only when capacity returns.

Current distinction · TEG-Blue synthesis. Relational restoration, organism-level Return and Return without relational restoration answer different questions:

  • Relational restoration. Mutual recognition, reciprocity, accountability, trust or repair may require another person's actual response because the unresolved condition exists between people.
  • Organism-level Return. Organisation may release through effective action, environmental change, assistance, new evidence or physiological recovery. Another person can help without being mandatory to every route.
  • Return without relational restoration. The relationship may remain unavailable or unrepaired while grief, an effective boundary, revised expectation or reorganisation permits updating to the changed reality.

Early dependency is the strongest overlap case: access to a caregiver may also provide warmth, nourishment, protection and conditions for recovery. That developmental coupling does not establish that every later Return must pass through Connection A.

Non-defensive is not the same as unactivated. A is an activation: coordination raises arousal even though nothing defensive is running. At A, resources support reciprocal attention, communication and adjustment. Defensive demand may be lower, but coordination still carries attentional and physiological demand. Supportive connection can aid recovery without being treated as metabolic storage, and restoration is represented most explicitly at X without being claimed to occur only there. Company is not what has to end; what changes is the demand the interaction places on the people involved.

Return availability is not repeated here. It is a row in the Gradient table, and duplicating it would create two descriptions of one thing that can drift apart. This file asks which route each organisation takes, not how available that route currently is.

Where a research term already exists

Several questions this file asks already have names in relevant research literatures. Using those names keeps individual claims checkable. Return remains the Gradient's synthesis term for a pattern that no one measure or research construct establishes by itself.

What the row asks Commonly used term Literature What the common term leaves out
What is this organisation working to accomplish? “Regulatory goal” Gross, process model Goal implies the organism chose. The Gradient’s Regulatory task is used instead.
Does another nervous system have to be present? Self-regulation · co-regulation · interpersonal emotion regulation Reeck et al., 2016 Nothing. Direct fit, and already cited in the Gradient table’s A column.
Why does another person’s presence change the response? Social buffering of the stress response Hostinar & Gunnar Well replicated for HPA and behavioural responses. Does not by itself establish that baseline is unreachable alone.
What separates X from A, if neither is defensive? Arousal — the activation axis of the circumplex model of affect Russell Names the axis: contentment is low-arousal positive, love and gratitude are high-arousal positive. Does not by itself establish how far arousal must fall for repair to run.
What does connection itself give the body? Brain opioid theory of social attachment; oxytocinergic and vagal participation in bonding Panksepp; Dunbar Establishes beneficial effects of bonding. Does not establish that A substitutes for the restorative processes that need no demand.
What has to update before checking closes? Safety learning · extinction · habituation of the orienting response Grupe & Nitschke, 2013 Studies a learned cue, not a whole organisation.
What does the body still owe afterwards? Stress recovery — cardiovascular recovery, vagal rebound, cortisol kinetics Time kinetics of acute endocrine response Measures one system at a time, against a pre-stressor value.
What accumulates when recovery does not complete? Allostatic load · insufficient recovery · perseverative cognition McEwen; Ottaviani et al., 2016 Composite construct with heterogeneous measures.
Does wider, less defensively dominated and more restorative organisation become available? none — This is where Return acts as a TEG-Blue synthesis. Baseline re-access can be recorded separately where this Gradient architecture supports it.

Four terms this file does not use

Regulated
In popular use it means calm. Every Position is regulatory, so the adjective would undo the definition of regulation in a single word.
Deactivation
Sounds like settling. In attachment research it names an avoidant strategy, which would collide with the Chronic A column.
Discharge
Belongs to B, C and D, where energy was mobilised and may not have been used. In A nothing was mobilised for defence; what ends is spending on coordination.
Window of tolerance
Widely used and thinly evidenced. If it appears it carries an evidence-status marker rather than structural weight.
