Approved architecture

Frameworks explain; they do not replace the models.

What a foundational framework is

A coordinating explanatory layer. Each framework asks a distinct question, gathers relevant mechanisms and hands a bounded contribution to the next layer. The label does not mean that a framework is an established scientific theory, or that resemblance to an external theory validates it.

The frameworks form a reading path, not a causal chain. A present behaviour cannot be used to reconstruct one childhood, a group cannot be treated as one nervous system, and an internal explanation cannot replace evidence about power, impact or responsibility.

Governing question

Which level of explanation owns each question, and what can it hand to the next without becoming a universal causal chain?

Four movements

Twelve questions in four groups.

F1–F3 · foundations and participation

Evolution, development and present adult patterns

What capacities are available, how participation develops, and what presently maintains or revises a response route. These three are the time depth of the field: how a person arrived at their configuration and organisation, and how they keep it.

F4–F7 · collective conditions and power

Rules, valuation, judgement and enforcement

How social and institutional conditions organise options without becoming one collective nervous system. F7 marks the threshold to the conduct plane.

F8–F10 · change, access and continuity

Distinct routes across people, environments and generations

What changes internally, behaviourally, materially, relationally and intergenerationally, and what does not follow automatically.

F11–F12 · integration and reflection

Complexity, history and future possibility

How to hold several levels of information without erasing evidence, power, responsibility or the possibility of revision.

Collection-wide work

The regulation thread is not yet traced.

Warning carried from the reviews

Regulation belongs across all twelve frameworks, but the collection does not yet define and trace it precisely. It must not become a catch-all for physiological organisation, emotion regulation, interpersonal coordination, short-term relief, compliance, control, completion, updating and Return. The Regulation Pass in Model 2's notes follows the complete route: detected or predicted condition, organism organisation and available information, conscious access and interpretation, possible action routes, immediate pressure change or relief, cost to self, other, relationship and system, and evidence of completion, persistence, updating or recurrence.

Three questions each transferred framework carries forward: F1, what condition, survival-relevant problem and completion route is the organism organising around; F2, what conditions did others change, what did the child learn about help and recovery, and did visible settling preserve signal access; F3, what response route changes immediate pressure, and what expectation, action or later conduct actually updates.

Boundaries

Every framework is a coordinating synthesis.

Each framework is a TEG-Blue coordinating synthesis, not an established scientific theory. Its component claims must be grounded separately using the fields listed in its review. Similarity to a published theory does not validate a framework. ESS and CLS are educational and functional model terms. Gradient organisation does not reveal diagnosis, developmental history, truth, morality or behaviour. Biological and developmental explanation never removes responsibility, impact or the need for protection and repair.
