F1 → completion and Return

The two survival problems can place the required change in different places.

This is a TEG-Blue synthesis, not a division of emotions into two fixed kinds. The active problem depends on what is at stake, where change is possible and what information would let organisation update. Read the canonical biological premise.

Organism-protection

The relevant condition is not inherently interpersonal. One's own action, environmental change or practical help may alter exposure, resources, obstruction or bodily demand.

Connection-preservation

When the condition is mutual availability, recognition, reciprocity, accountability, trust or repair, one organism cannot produce relational restoration alone.

Early dependency couples both

For dependent young, caregiver connection may also provide warmth, nourishment, protection, orientation and conditions for recovery. Preserving connection can therefore participate directly in organism-protection.

Developmental handoff · F2 Entry 002

Keep three outcomes separate: relational restoration; organism-level updating and Return; and Return without relational restoration through grief, an effective boundary, revised expectation or reorganisation. Another nervous system may be required for the first without being required for every form of regulation or Return.

Why this stays beside F1

Its job is to close the evolutionary account: capacities accumulated across deep time, then participate together in present organisation. Keeping the bridge beside F1 makes that handoff visible without turning the governing premise into another seven-column content page.

Why the detailed table stays in Model 2

The main Gradient owns the changing present configuration: bodily information, emotion, perception, mentalizing, affective sharing, action, updating, Return and repair. Those rows should be revised once in their canonical home, not copied here.

Reading rule. The condition cells are Fluid reference territories; they do not prove what is happening externally. The Position describes organism-level organisation and available capacities—not a person, diagnosis, behaviour, moral category or excuse. Behaviour, power, impact, alternatives, recurrence and repair remain a separate evidence plane. See How the Nervous System Gradient models organism organisation.
