F3 · Adult Pattern Maintenance and Revision

Adult prediction, meaning, self-position, response-route selection and revision across present relational events. How adults anticipate consequences, assign meaning, protect valued self-positions, select response routes and use feedback in ways that may contribute to repetition or revision. Neither the actor is cognition alone, nor is inaccuracy the conclusion.

The decision

Keep F3; retire False Coherence as its title and master mechanism.

"Adult Cognition and False Coherence" made cognition the sole actor and inaccuracy the conclusion before the account was tested. Adults act with bodily, affective, relational, remembered and contextual information of varying availability and quality. They anticipate consequences, assign meaning, protect valued positions, choose or recruit responses, experience immediate effects and sometimes revise when feedback arrives. F3 examines that process without assuming the resulting account is false, that stability is its purpose, or that one mechanism explains why it continues.

F3 is an explanatory layer, not a fourth model and not a shortened Model 3. It asks how an adult participates within an event; Model 3 owns the event's movement.

The question F3 owns

Given present conditions, what shapes participation, and how is feedback used?

Given present conditions and the information currently available, how do prediction, meaning, valued self-position and available response routes shape adult participation, and how is feedback represented and used?

F3 owns questions about

Anticipated relational and practical consequences; meaning assignment and felt certainty; valued roles, status, belonging and identity continuity; the response route selected from available options; experienced immediate relief, reward, conflict reduction or restored predictability; response to contradiction, changed conditions and another person's feedback; the differences among changed language, changed confidence, changed action and changed later conduct.

Two words, narrowly

Maintenance names relations that may keep a route available; it does not establish persistence over time from one event. Revision distinguishes changes in account, confidence, represented options and chosen action; it does not declare that the whole event updated. F3 does not own developmental origin, present organisation, the event sequence, completion, Return, recovery, carryover, learning across cycles, persistence, truth, diagnosis, harm, coercion or completed repair.

The core insight

An account can feel coherent without being accurate, revisable or consequential.

A person can form a coherent account from the information presently available, and that account can organise action or reduce uncertainty without incorporating all relevant bodily, contextual or interpersonal information.

Inquiry rules, not verdicts

Coherence is not accuracy. Felt certainty is not evidence. Immediate relief is not durable updating. Changed language is not necessarily changed access, expectation, action or conduct. Disagreement is not proof of defensiveness. Bodily intensity is not proof of an external interpretation. Reduced felt access is not objectivity. Explanation is not exoneration. Internal revision is not relational repair. None of these declares that a stable account is defensive or false.

The six F3 construct families

Six questions replace one mechanism.

FamilyGoverning questionBoundary
F3-A · Relational prediction and expected consequenceWhat is expected to happen after disclosure, disagreement, dependence, withdrawal, error, status loss or another's independent action?The prediction may be accurate, condition-matched or new; no pathology or childhood origin is assumed.
F3-B · Meaning assignment and felt certaintyWhat is a feeling, contradiction, delay, boundary, error or response taken to mean, and how certain does that become?No inference to hidden bodily truth, deception, or the correctness of another's account.
F3-C · Self-position and identity-contingent maintenanceWhich valued role, status, belonging claim or identity continuity is being protected, and what would revision appear to cost?A protected self-position does not establish diagnosis, fragility, a False Self or harmful behaviour.
F3-D · Relational response-route selectionWhich route is recruited: pause, support seeking, reassurance, accommodation, appeasement, negotiation, structure, withdrawal, concealment, imposition, retaliation or repair?The visible route does not identify one mechanism, state, personality or inevitable sequence; when a route imposes cost on another person, Behaviour and responsibility governs.
F3-E · Relief, reinforcement and replacementWhat immediate effect was experienced, what did the route support or replace, and is there evidence it became more available later?Relief does not establish reinforcement, recurrence, completion or updating. Model 3 compares immediate and later outcomes; the Chronic plane owns demonstrated recurrence.
F3-F · Feedback, revision and repair readinessHow are contradiction, impact, failed prediction or changed conditions represented and used, and what changes across account, confidence, options and action?Not whole-event updating, Return, recovery or learning. Agreement does not prove insight; internal revision does not complete accountability, restitution or repair.

Provisional TEG-Blue integrations supported by component literatures, not validated types or stages. Reinforcement is claimed only when later learning or increased route availability is evidenced. Repair readiness is a question about participation in feedback; it cannot stand in for accountability, restitution, restored agency or completed repair. The families and the six evidence clusters behind them are in the six-cluster synthesis.

F3 inside an event

F3 uses Model 3 without becoming it.

F3 can be applied at several moments of an event, but it does not own the pass. At entry, ask which learned expectations, valued roles and anticipated consequences are relevant. At signal and organisation, ask what information is participating in prediction and meaning. At access and meaning, apply F3-A to F3-C. At action, apply F3-D, described behaviourally. At outcome, apply F3-E. At feedback, apply F3-F. At Return and later entry, note any revised prediction, self-position or option available for later use.

The minimum route audit, whenever F3 says maintenance, relief, revision or reinforcement

What condition was present? What information was available? What action or inhibition actually occurred? What changed immediately? What changed later across updating, recruitment, recovery, Return and recurrence? Who carried the cost, what power was involved, and did viable refusal remain?

F3 asks why a particular interpretation or route was plausible, valuable or revisable for the adult participant. Model 3 asks what actually happened through time and what the next event begins inside.

A branching field

The same pressure can be followed by very different routes.

Pausing and gathering information; seeking support or reassurance; accommodating or appeasing; negotiating or setting structure; choosing distance or a boundary; concealing or avoiding; imposing, retaliating or coercing; accepting feedback and repairing. Internal pressure does not select the branch. Goals, power, options, concern, habits, culture, consequences and deliberate participation all matter.

Harm becomes assessable only when behaviour, authority, imposed cost, option-field restriction, impact, recurrence and response to accountability are examined directly. The surface route does not reveal what it accomplished: pausing, reassurance, withdrawal, structure, confrontation or connection may change the condition, support later capacity, reduce pressure without updating, or produce a mixed outcome. The route audit, not the route's appearance, determines what can be said. Model 3's relational-escalation sequence must not be imported into F3 as a cognitive or biological pathway.

The status of False Coherence

A historical recognition label, decomposed.

  1. Available informationBodily, affective, relational, remembered, contextual.
  2. Prediction, interpretation, certainty, self-positionF3-A to F3-C.
  3. Available response routesF3-D.
  4. Selected action and immediate effectF3-E.
  5. Feedback use and possible revisionF3-F.

An account is not false because it is coherent, protective, intense, resistant to challenge or different from another person's. The inquiry is whether it stays connected to available evidence, current conditions, alternative explanations, another person's separateness and later consequences, and whether it can revise when those change. Placed inside an event, the slice sits between Model 3's entry and its feedback and updating; it is not a replacement cycle.

Handoffs

Four connections stay visible.

Across Model 3

F3 variables sit inside the event; Model 3 records actual action, immediate and later outcomes, updating and what enters the next cycle.

To the Chronic plane

When recurrence, generalisation, rigidity, cumulative cost or updating difficulty becomes the question. One response, insight failure or relief event is not Chronic.

To Behaviour and responsibility

Whenever a response affects another person's agency, safety or options. F3 cannot derive coercion, intent, responsibility or dangerousness from an internal account.

To F4

When a personally held expectation becomes part of a shared norm, rule or institution, but only after collective processes and power are investigated independently.

F2 contributes developmentally shaped possibilities and expectations; this is an explanatory handoff, not a claim that a person moves through F1, F2 and F3 as stages. F3 does not convert one event into a maintained pattern, private coherence into collective rules, internal revision into whole-event updating, or insight into repair.

Boundaries and provenance

Retired wording, and the grounding route.

Narrowed or retired

"The lock." "The cost: truth." Development has already "configured" the adult. Cognition building an account "from its own output". False Coherence as a complete story whose job is stability. Relief as "precisely why" the story persists. Insight failing because the same system produced it. Questioning the narrative "registers as threat". Skills growing while "the body has learned nothing new". Discomfort attributed outward so a boundary becomes attack. Others recruited to regulate through closeness, distance, management or control as one continuum.

Grounding fields

Cognitive psychology and science; social psychology; personality and individual differences; affective science and interoception; judgement and decision science; learning, reinforcement and habit research; clinical and psychotherapy-process research; relationship, family and interpersonal research; communication and conversation analysis; sociology and microsociology; cultural psychology; organisational behaviour; moral psychology and ethics; legal and forensic psychology, and criminology, only where a psycholegal or violence question is actually present; philosophy of mind and phenomenology. Priority evidence: ecologically valid interaction research, longitudinal and within-person designs, behavioural observation, multi-informant evidence, and studies that separately measure explanation, confidence, action and later conduct.
