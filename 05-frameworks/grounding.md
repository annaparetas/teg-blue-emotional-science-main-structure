## What kind of claim a framework is

Every framework is a **TEG-Blue coordinating synthesis** (Level 3). It asks a
question, gathers mechanisms from several fields, and hands a bounded
contribution to the next layer. Its component claims are grounded
separately, each at its own level, using the fields listed in the
framework's review. Similarity to a published theory does not validate a
framework; a finding in one field does not validate the sequence.

Each review ends with a **grounding science and knowledge fields** map: a
research route, with what each field can ground and what it cannot
establish alone. Forensic psychology enters only where a psycholegal
question is actually present.

## Provenance

The twelve frameworks began as the F1–F12 map assembled from legacy
framework and SPINE materials, with the old titles (Biological Evolution,
Developmental Calibration, Adult Cognition and False Coherence, Rules
Regulate, Worth Hierarchies Regulate, Bias Regulates, Domination Regulates,
Self-Reconnection and Repair Pathway, Neurodivergent Configuration and
Adaptive Fit, Rebuilding Generational Bridges, Paradox Holds What Logic
Cannot, Retrospective ESS–CLS Disconnection). The architectural reviews of
21 to 22 August 2026 gave each a provisional current title, its owned
question, its handoffs and its grounding route. The map on the frozen
`frameworks/index.html` was updated from those reviews on 22 August 2026.
