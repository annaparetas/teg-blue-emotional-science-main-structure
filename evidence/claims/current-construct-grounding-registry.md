This registry begins with the current system rather than the original external-framework list. It identifies what each construct is trying to explain before sources are selected.

The entries describe present project roles. They do not mean that every construct has already received a complete evidence review.

## Registry key

### System role

- **PROCESS** — part of the functional Inner Compass sequence
- **ORGANISATION** — part of the Fluid or Chronic Gradient
- **CAPACITY** — something that can remain available, narrow or become difficult to access
- **BRIDGE** — connects several parts of the system
- **ETHICAL** — protects a distinction involving behaviour, impact, responsibility or repair
- **INTEGRATION** — a TEG-Blue assembly whose components may be grounded separately

### Grounding state

- **ACTIVE** — explained in a current page
- **WORKING** — under active development
- **UNMAPPED** — present in the system but not yet entered into a claim-level source record

## Next claim-level pass

For each construct, the next pass should separate:

1. **Definition claim** — what TEG-Blue means by the term.
2. **Phenomenon claim** — what observable or reportable pattern the term points to.
3. **Mechanism claim** — what processes may contribute.
4. **Development claim** — how the pattern may be learned, altered or maintained.
5. **Application claim** — what the construct helps someone notice or do.
6. **Boundary claim** — what must not be inferred.

These claims may require different sources even when they appear in the same sentence.
