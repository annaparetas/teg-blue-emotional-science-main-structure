# Scientific Lineage and Integrative Grounding

Status: working foundation for the Inner Compass, the Nervous System Gradient and the wider TEG-Blue system
Purpose: recover the breadth of the original TEG-Blue science work without restoring claims that the present system has outgrown

Research-family companion: [Trauma, Stress and Adversity Research Map](../../05-frameworks/F02/grounding/trauma-stress-and-adversity-research-map.md)

## The layer that was missing

The present science work asks two necessary questions:

1. What changes while present conditions change?
2. What was learned, and what stays available after conditions change?

Before either question, another one is needed:

> **What kind of claim are we making, at what level of human life, and what kind of knowledge could responsibly inform it?**

This question matters because TEG-Blue describes more than biological activity. It follows what happens as a living person encounters conditions, makes sense of them, responds within relationships and systems, and gradually learns from what follows. Neuroscience can inform part of that process. It cannot establish the whole of it.

The original research-alignment work kept this wider field visible. It connected nervous-system science with attachment, trauma, identity, family systems, neurodiversity, power, culture, philosophy, education and AI. Its breadth remains useful. Some of its individual explanations, alignments and certainty levels now need to be rebuilt.

This note provides the structure for doing that.

## The organism-in-context loop

The common process running through the current system is:

> **Conditions → detection → organisation → signals → lived access → participation → action → consequence → updating, learning or Return**

The arrows do not describe a rigid sequence. Earlier learning can shape detection. Anticipated consequences can shape action before anything happens. Other people and systems can alter the conditions at every point. Several parts of the loop can also happen together.

The loop provides one shared reference without reducing every phenomenon to one mechanism.

| Part of the loop | Working question | Current TEG-Blue home |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conditions | What is happening inside, outside and in represented or anticipated experience? | How the Body Reads Conditions |
| Detection | What information becomes available to the organism, and through which routes? | Sensory-to-Inner-Compass track |
| Organisation | What is being prioritised, inhibited, recruited or held available? | Inner Compass sequence · Nervous System Gradient |
| Signals | What bodily and situational information becomes available? | Body Signals · Situation Signals · Signal Neurochemistry |
| Lived access | What can ME feel, notice, name, interpret or hold in awareness? | ESS · CLS · ME · relational capacity configurations |
| Participation | What can be paused, reflected on, chosen, communicated or revised? | Conscious Participation |
| Action | What behaviour becomes possible, likely, costly or constrained? | Behaviour Across the Gradient |
| Consequence | What happens to the body, other people, the relationship and the wider situation? | Behaviour and responsibility · repair work |
| Updating, learning or Return | What completes, changes, persists, generalises or becomes easier to release? | Regulation and Return · Fluid–Chronic depth model |

## Six interacting levels

The same event can be described at several levels. The descriptions may connect, but they are not interchangeable.

### 1 · Biological

This level includes sensory and interoceptive processing, autonomic and endocrine participation, neural coordination, immune and metabolic processes, action preparation, energy use and recovery.

It can help explain how an organism coordinates under particular conditions. It cannot by itself tell us what an event means, whether a behaviour is ethical or what history produced an individual pattern.

### 2 · Experiential

This level includes felt experience, emotion, attention, awareness, meaning, memory, internal conflict and the person’s access to what is happening.

First-person experience is evidence about lived experience. It is not a direct measurement of a specific brain region, hormone or autonomic pathway.

### 3 · Developmental

This level includes caregiving, emotion socialisation, learning, repetition, timing, developmental opportunity, threshold formation, generalisation and repair.

Developmental evidence can help explain how patterns become more available or persistent over time. A group association cannot reconstruct one person’s origin story.

### 4 · Relational

This level includes co-regulation, attachment, trust, rupture, mutual influence, roles, dependence, conflict, coercion, boundaries and repair.

Relational patterns cannot be understood by reading one nervous system in isolation. The behaviour and power of other people remain part of the conditions.

### 5 · Behavioural and ethical

This level includes observable action, impact, available alternatives, responsibility, boundary-setting, accountability and repair.

A protective organisation can help explain pressure on behaviour. It does not determine the behaviour, establish intent or remove responsibility for its effects.

### 6 · Material, cultural and systemic

This level includes physical safety, money, housing, access, disability, social position, institutional rules, cultural meaning, exclusion, oppression and collective histories.

These are not background variables added after an individual explanation. They can be part of the conditions the organism is accurately responding to.

## Time runs through every level

| Timescale | What it allows us to examine |
| --- | --- |
| Moment-to-moment | Detection, state change, action readiness, behaviour and early recovery |
| Across an event | Escalation, completion, updating, interpersonal consequence and Return |
| Repeated | Anticipation, sensitisation, habit, recurring roles and accumulated cost |
| Developmental | Learning opportunities, caregiving, emotion socialisation, thresholds and changing capacities |
| Intergenerational | Family practices, stories, material conditions, institutions and possible biological pathways that require separate evidence |
| Institutional and historical | Rules, power, cultural meanings and conditions that can persist beyond one relationship or lifetime |

“Intergenerational” must not be used as shorthand for genetic or epigenetic inheritance. Family behaviour, social learning, material conditions, institutional continuity and biological pathways are different routes and need different evidence.

## Three systems, three scientific identities

### The Inner Compass

The Inner Compass is a **functional process model**. It follows how conditions become biological and experiential information, how that information may become available to ME, and how participation, action and updating can occur.

It does not claim that conscious awareness controls the whole process or that every part occurs in a fixed order.

### The Nervous System Gradient

The Gradient is an **organisation model**. It describes characteristic changes in whole-organism priorities, available capacities, action tendencies, cost and Return across present and learned conditions.

Its Positions are TEG-Blue integrations. They are not validated biological categories, personality types, diagnoses or tools for inferring a person’s history from behaviour.

### TEG-Blue

TEG-Blue is the **larger integrative system**. It connects organism-level processes with lived experience, relationship, identity, behaviour, responsibility, power, culture, repair and design.

Not every TEG-Blue construct belongs inside the Gradient. A concept can inform how the wider system is used without becoming a nervous-system Position or biological claim.

## Knowledge families must remain distinguishable

The original alignment table placed theories, experiments, therapy models, disciplines, philosophies and cultural traditions in one list. They can all contribute, but not in the same way.

| Knowledge family | What it can contribute | What it should not be asked to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empirical mechanism research | Measured relations among conditions, processes, responses and outcomes | Prove an entire Position or worldview from one measure |
| Developmental and longitudinal research | Change, timing, recurrence, context and possible learning trajectories | Reconstruct an individual history from a group pattern |
| Clinical and practice models | Useful distinctions, intervention practices and accumulated clinical observation | Stand in for independent evidence of every explanatory claim |
| Lived-experience and participatory knowledge | Phenomena, language, priorities, hidden costs and questions research may miss | Establish a unique biological mechanism without corresponding measures |
| Social, cultural and critical scholarship | Power, meaning, institutions, roles, exclusion and collective conditions | Diagnose an individual nervous-system organisation |
| Philosophical and ethical work | Clarification of knowledge, interpretation, responsibility, dignity and limits | Be graded as if it were an experimental treatment study |
| Indigenous and community knowledge | Relational, ecological, ethical and cultural understandings within their own traditions | Be extracted from context or used as decorative validation |
| Education and design frameworks | Accessibility, learning environments, implementation and system design | Establish clinical or biological causation |
| AI and affective-computing research | Interaction design, model limits, risk and machine processing of human signals | Claim direct access to emotion, intent or nervous-system state from behavioural data |

## Relationship labels for lineage sources

The science protocol already labels evidence by its relationship to a specific claim. Lineage work needs a related but slightly wider set of roles.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **DIRECT** | Research tests substantially the same process, condition, population and timescale as a current claim. |
| **BRIDGE** | Research supports a component process but not the complete TEG-Blue construct. |
| **INTERPRETIVE** | A theory or discipline helps explain meaning or relationships among observations. |
| **PRACTICE** | A clinical, educational or community model contributes a way of responding or intervening. |
| **LINEAGE** | A source helped shape TEG-Blue’s questions or language but does not currently ground an empirical claim. |
| **TEG-BLUE** | The project proposes the integration. Its parts may be grounded even though the complete assembly has not been directly tested. |

A source may have more than one role, but each assigned role must name the claim or use it applies to.

## What to recover from the original 40-framework alignment

The old table should be treated as a **lineage inventory**, not a validation table.

### Biological, emotion and learning contributors

Polyvagal Theory; Somatic Marker Hypothesis; predictive processing and threat research; interpersonal neurobiology; adversity research; attachment research; stress and possible intergenerational biological pathways.

These entries need claim-level review. Broad phrases such as “state before story”, “the amygdala detects threat” or “biological transmission of trauma” should be separated into narrower testable statements.

### Psychological and clinical contributors

Cognitive dissonance; Winnicott; Internal Family Systems; Rogers; Jung; Judith Herman; Somatic Experiencing; parts-oriented trauma work; self-compassion; narrative therapy.

These sources may contribute interpretive or practice language even where their full theoretical explanations are not established as biological mechanisms.

### Relational, social and power contributors

Social identity; system justification; critical race, feminist and disability studies; Bourdieu; Goffman; Hochschild; betrayal trauma; coercive control; gaslighting; authoritarianism; obedience and institutional normalisation.

These sources recover something the biological pages cannot carry alone: actual power, social reward, role pressure, deliberate strategy, institutional conditions and epistemic harm.

### Neurodiversity, disability and education contributors

The neurodiversity paradigm; Intense World Theory; executive-function and sensory-processing research; Universal Design for Learning; differentiated instruction; social-emotional learning; the social model of disability.

These entries should distinguish empirical findings, contested theories, rights-based paradigms and design practices. Difference, disability, distress and environmental mismatch should not be collapsed.

### Cultural, philosophical and epistemic contributors

Ubuntu and Māori traditions; fallibilism; hermeneutics and other approaches to interpretation.

These require precise sourcing and respect for internal difference. Broad cultural traditions should not be reduced to one sentence or recruited merely to make TEG-Blue appear universal.

## Framework intake record

Every framework or knowledge tradition reconsidered from the old work should receive this record:

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Specific theory, research programme, practice model, discipline or tradition |
| Knowledge family | Empirical, developmental, practice, lived-experience, social/cultural, philosophical/ethical, community, education/design or AI |
| Source status | Primary source, systematic review, consensus statement, critique, practice manual or secondary explanation |
| Phenomenon | The specific part of human life it helps us examine |
| Level | One or more of the six levels, without treating them as interchangeable |
| Timescale | Moment, event, repeated, developmental, intergenerational or institutional/historical |
| Contribution | The narrow insight TEG-Blue may responsibly use |
| Cannot establish | The inference that must not be made from it |
| Relationship role | DIRECT, BRIDGE, INTERPRETIVE, PRACTICE, LINEAGE or TEG-BLUE |
| Current construct | The present Inner Compass, Gradient or wider TEG-Blue construct it may inform |
| Evidence status | Strength, consistency, uncertainty, criticism and counterevidence where applicable |
| Translation sentence | “TEG-Blue uses this insight to…” |
| Boundary sentence | “This does not mean…” |
| Review record | Reviewer, date, search route and next review point |

The old star rating should not return. Conceptual influence, empirical support, practical usefulness and cultural importance are different judgements.

## Working safeguards

- Begin with the phenomenon and claim, not with a favoured theory.
- Keep present conditions visible before interpreting a response as learned mismatch.
- Do not use one level to erase another.
- Do not turn behaviour into a hidden-state detector.
- Do not infer intention, morality or history from physiology.
- Do not use protection to erase impact, responsibility or repair.
- Do not turn a diagnostic group average into a person.
- Keep mixed, null, resilience and adaptation findings in the record.
- Keep cultural knowledge in context and identify who is authorised to interpret it.
- Mark the point where TEG-Blue begins to integrate rather than report established findings.
- Revise the model when evidence narrows a claim. Do not search only for evidence that preserves the model.

## Development route

1. Use the companion construct registry to identify what the current system actually claims.
2. Assign each claim a level, timescale and evidence role before searching.
3. Reassess the original 40 frameworks as a lineage inventory.
4. Keep only connections that remain useful to a present construct or an explicitly named wider TEG-Blue question.
5. Add primary research, reviews, critiques and counterevidence to each retained entry.
6. Move settled distinctions into an active Scientific Lineage and Integrative Grounding page.
7. Keep the source records underneath that page so public explanations remain traceable.

The purpose of the new layer is not to make TEG-Blue appear supported by everything. It is to show where different forms of knowledge meet, where they remain separate, and where TEG-Blue is making its own integration.
