# Integrated Grounding Review · 19 August 2026

Status: PEND-001 completed
Scope: all scientific-grounding documents and active F1/F2/Inner Compass/Gradient changes created or revised on 19 August 2026
Purpose: decide what remains authoritative, what overlaps legitimately, what is stale and which three tasks follow

Companions: [Scientific Architecture Inventory](scientific-architecture-inventory.md) · [Project Terminology Ledger](../../GLOSSARY.md) · [Pending Tasks Register](../../STATUS.md)

## Review outcome

The work now has a coherent scientific architecture. The central layers no longer need another broad framework map.

Three things are established at the level required to continue:

1. **The explanatory layers have distinct ownership.** F1, F2, the Inner Compass, Fluid, Chronic and Harm–Power–Repair ask different questions and hand work forward rather than explaining one another by substitution.
2. **The unit of grounding is the claim, not the whole TEG-Blue construct.** Definition, phenomenon, mechanism, development, application and boundary claims can require different evidence.
3. **The main terminology corrections are coherent across current active pages.** Resource allocation, multiple Chronic routes and bodily information access/use have replaced the earlier universal or one-dimensional language.

The remaining problem is not lack of architecture. It is uneven record state. Some documents still describe work as pending after it was implemented. Several source summaries are duplicated. Chronic still lacks one governing scientific spine, and the evidence base remains first-pass for most claims.

The project should now move from architecture into evidence infrastructure and one carefully bounded framework spine.

## Legitimate overlap that should remain

### Registry versus claim decomposition

The registry answers “What is this construct and who owns it?” The decomposition answers “What kinds of claims are contained inside it?” These are complementary, not duplicates.

### Terminology ledger versus improvement register

The ledger records decisions. The improvement register develops candidates. When they differ, the ledger governs.

### Trauma map versus F2 crosswalk

The trauma map classifies research families by knowledge role. The F2 crosswalk asks where those families may inform F2. Neither should absorb the other.

### F1/Fluid/Chronic crosswalk versus Chronic spine

The crosswalk assigns research families across layers. The future Chronic spine must define Chronic's own claims and evidence routes. The crosswalk should not become that governing document.

### ESS/CLS decision versus component decomposition

The decision protects the educational purpose and boundaries. The component file makes the systems small enough to investigate. Both are required.

### Harm–Power–Repair versus behaviour-cost work

HPR defines the ethical and relational claim families. The behaviour-cost layer examines what actions do in a situation. They should connect without becoming one file.

## Genuine unresolved scientific work

### Source records are not centralised

The same paper may appear in a pilot, crosswalk, active page and terminology note with slightly different summaries. One canonical source record does not yet exist.

### Evidence status is not visible in the registry

“Active” means the construct appears in the model. It does not mean its definition, phenomenon, mechanisms and development have received equal evidence review.

### Chronic lacks a governing spine

The possible Chronic routes are now clearer, but their definitions and source routes remain spread across several files.

### ESS and CLS are decomposed but not source-grounded component by component

The fifteen component families are small enough to investigate. Most have only routed research territories rather than canonical source records.

### Trauma and adversity maps remain incomplete intake

The supplied 37-part and 31-part texts ended partway through their final entries. The maps are useful but should not be described as complete inventories.

### HPR is architecturally clear but evidentially inherited

The twelve claim families came from a valuable old-engine dossier. Primary-source status, counterevidence and legitimate-power comparisons remain to be reviewed.

### Return remains multidimensional but incompletely reconciled

The project now rejects one endpoint and one universal incomplete-Return cause. It still needs channel-specific recovery evidence and consistent active definitions.

### Several Gradient terms remain open

Position, Baseline, Strategic Management, Power Mobilisation, Shutdown and Fluid–Chronic depth still require decisions or evidence. They should not be changed ahead of their supporting work.
