# GLOSSARY

The only terminology authority. One entry per term: what it means in
TEG-Blue, which folder owns it, and whether the wording is approved or still
working. When a canon page and this file disagree, the canon page is edited or
this file is; the disagreement is not left standing.

Decisions not yet carried in from the old terminology ledger are listed at
the end.

## Foundations

**TEG-Blue · The Emotional Gradient Blueprint** · Approved · Foundations. A
layered visual framework that maps how emotions, nervous systems, survival
strategies, identity, social patterns and repair capacity form and evolve.
Grounded in science; the integration is the contribution. Educational and
reflective, not diagnostic. Not therapy, coaching, typology or moral ranking.

**Three evidence levels** · Approved · Foundations. Established evidence;
grounded synthesis; TEG-Blue integration. Do not demand Level 1 evidence for
a Level 3 term; test whether Levels 1 and 2 ground it. Be rigorous about the
evidence, flexible about the vocabulary.

**Feeling-fact collapse** · Approved · Foundations. "This is real inside me"
becoming "this is therefore true outside me". The first reality distortion.
The second is emotional-state disconnection: "I do not feel it, therefore it
is not real". Neither is a clinical claim.

**The harm pair** · Approved · Foundations. Most harm is not aimed (capacity
failing); some harm is aimed (repeated, selective, organised around another
person's fear, hope or dependence). The two must never travel separately.

**Calm is not safety** · Approved · Foundations. Distress is not danger.
Control may look composed after causing harm; its impact often appears first
in the person being controlled.

**Emotion** · Approved · 00. The coordinated organism-level process through
which bodily or situational significance participates across the organism.
Can shape physiology, attention, perception and action-readiness without
becoming conscious.

**Feeling** · Approved · 00. The conscious experience of some of that
emotional information when it becomes available to ME. May be partial,
mixed, faint or not yet named. Not interchangeable with emotion.

**Information, not instruction** · Approved · 00. Emotion informs
participation; it does not authorise action and is not a complete verdict
about reality.

**Action-readiness** · Approved · 00. Preparation for possible response. Not
behaviour. Behaviour, impact and responsibility are assessed separately.

**Organism-protection** · Approved (working architectural language) · 00.
Maintaining the organism's viable condition, bodily integrity, boundaries,
resources and capacity.

**Connection-preservation** · Approved (working architectural language) ·
00. Maintaining access to people or groups whose availability affects care,
belonging, cooperation, information, shared effort, protection or
restoration. Not conformity, compliance or self-erasure.

## Signal Map

**Signal** · Approved · 01. A functional umbrella term for an organised
change that carries information about a condition and can influence
regulation, attention or readiness. May or may not become consciously felt.
Not a fact, a single biological object or an instruction.

**Body-condition signal** · Approved · 01. A signal that begins with a
condition inside the body that regulation tracks. Starting question: what
internal condition is the organism regulating? Earlier name: Body Signal
(still used in the rosters).

**Emotion signal** · Approved · 01. A signal carrying information about the
organism's relation to a current, remembered, imagined or anticipated
condition. Starting question: what does this condition mean for the
organism? Earlier name: Situation Signal (still used in the rosters).

**Family** · Approved · 01. An organising layer between a signal group and an
individual signal. Not a single neural or chemical system, not a sequence,
not a behavioural instruction.

**Body-condition families** · Approved, provisional membership · 01.
Essential-supply, Bodily-integrity, Thermal-balance, Visceral-capacity and
elimination, Restorative-capacity.

**Emotion families** · Approved, protected membership · 01. Belonging,
Belonging-at-Risk, Threat-Protection, Survival-Rage. Functional groupings,
not stages. Characteristic Fluid associations (A, A↔B, B, D) are
illustrative and never run in reverse.

**Completion** · Approved · 01 and 04. The event-specific point at which the
currently required response no longer needs to remain recruited in the same
form. Condition-specific, not discharge and not a chemical event.

**Possible completing condition** · Approved · 01. The condition that would
answer a signal. Replaced "what the signal needs" on 23 August 2026.

**Distorted Signals** · Working · 01. A separate section of the Chronic
roster, not emotion rows.

**Nine-layer filter** · Working · 01. A reusable filter for examining one
signal across condition information, ESS and CLS contribution, organism
participation, conscious-access transition, ME participation and feedback,
with emotion identity held stable.

## Model 1

**ESS · Emotional Somatic System** · Approved name; expansion open · 02.
Distributed biological information processing concerned with bodily
information, emotional significance, biological weighting, felt qualities
and action-readiness. Not "conscious emotion" and not "the body without the
brain". Open: whether the E stands for Emotional or Embodied.

**CLS · Cognitive Logical System** · Approved · 02. Processing through
which information may be represented, compared, sequenced, contextualised,
imagined, expressed symbolically, organised into explanations, planned with
and reconsidered. Not all cognition, not pure logic, not a command centre.

**ME** · Approved · 02. The living person who may turn toward, examine,
communicate, inhibit or act with what becomes available. Not a third system,
not a homunculus, not an executive outside the organism. Write "ME", never
"the ME".

**Coordinated conscious access** · Approved · 02. The relationship through
which some participating information may become experienceable or
inspectable by ME. Not another biological system.

**Three capacities** · Approved dimensions; single name open · 02. Bodily
information access and use (feel myself; process: interoceptive
processing); affective-sharing access (feel another, self and other
distinct; process: affective sharing); mentalizing access and use (read and
revise another; process: mentalizing). Each names how much of a process ME
can notice and use; the process continues regardless. They vary unevenly and
do not define a Position, personality, diagnosis or morality. Other names in
use: three forms of access, ME-participation terms, relational capacities.
Proposed 3 September 2026, awaiting Anna's confirmation: the first two are
ME reading ESS, the third is ME using CLS.

**ME Access Dials** · Working · 02. The Model 1 add-on,
`02-model-1-ess-cls-me/me-access.html`: the three capacities as slider-toggles with four bands each, the
configuration they combine into, the eight-configuration field, the five
quality dimensions and the mode a pattern tends toward if it stabilises.
Model 1 owns it; the Inner Compass reads its setting. Replaced the 2
September controller and the carried capacities page on 3 September 2026.

**Empathic concern** · Approved · 02. A relational output: whether another
person's welfare can motivate, restrain or redirect action. Not a fourth
capacity; cannot be inferred from the three.

**Configuration** · Working · 02. The current relationship between the three
capacities. Eight binary configurations (000 to 111) are entry points, read
through five quality dimensions: availability, differentiation, self–other
distinction, accuracy and updating, flexible recruitment. A configuration is
access information, never a verdict, personality or diagnosis. Anna's three
worked examples: 001 mentalizing only, 010 affective sharing dominant, 111
all three available. Which mode a configuration tends toward if it
stabilises is a working rule on the add-on, not yet approved.

**Presence, access, differentiation, interpretation, use** · Approved
distinction · 02. Information present in the organism is not information
accessible to ME, which is not information differentiated, which is not
information checked against evidence, which is not information used with
care. Conditional steps, not a ladder.

**Integration** · Working definition · 02. Sufficient differentiated and
revisable access for emotional–somatic information, reflective
interpretation, conditions, evidence, impact and possible action to inform
one another. Not calm, agreement, fusion or maximal awareness.

**Hurt / harm** · Approved distinction · 02 and 07. Hurt is felt impact:
pain, offence, fear, loss, shame, disappointment. Harm is damage, violation,
coercion, deprivation or restriction of safety and agency. Hurt can signal
harm; hurt can also follow a boundary interrupting an expectation.

## Model 2

**Nervous System Organisation Gradient** · Approved name · 03. Changing
organisation of organism-level resources and capacities in relation to
detected or predicted demands. Owns present organisation, resource
allocation, action-readiness, controllability, transition and current Return
availability. Does not own emotion identity, behaviour or temporal outcome.

**Position** (X, A, A↔B, B, C, D, Z) · Approved letters; display wording
open · 03. Fluid names: X Baseline (safe to be alone; restoration and
readiness); A Connection / Belonging (safe with others); A↔B Safety Checking
(safety or belonging uncertain); B Protection / Defence (threat); C Strategic
Management (persistent threat); D Power Mobilisation (life threat); Z
Shutdown (mobilisation unavailable or too costly). Chronic presentations:
Chronic Elevated Baseline, Chronic Conditional Belonging, Chronic Safety
Checking, Chronic Protection / Defence, Chronic Control & Strategic
Management, Chronic Dominance & Repression, Chronic Collapse / Shutdown.
Never a person's identity or conduct. Open: "Gradient Position" vs
"reference organisation" vs "organisation profile". Note: the 27 August 2026
summary page labelled X and A↔B differently; the tables govern until Anna
says otherwise.

**Baseline (X)** · Approved display term; candidate replacement open · 03.
Relative low-protective-demand organisation where restoration is represented
most explicitly. Candidate display term: Restorative Availability. Not a
universal set point and not the mandatory destination of every Return.

**Strategic Management (C)** · Approved display term; formal companion open
· 03. Sustained protection through prediction, planning and management.
"Strategic" must not imply deliberate manipulation. Formal companion:
Sustained Threat Management if threat is required, Persistent Demand
Management if wider consequential demand belongs.

**Power Mobilisation (D)** · Approved display term with boundary · 03.
Maximum force, movement and survival action under life threat. "Power" means
defensive capacity, never social dominance; formal name Extreme Defensive
Mobilisation. Dominance and Domination are separate terms owned by the Inner
Compass.

**Shutdown (Z)** · Approved as an accessible umbrella · 03. Freezing,
immobility, dissociation, collapse, fainting and exhaustion are not one
process. Formally: reduced-output, immobilisation and disconnection routes;
name the route in evidence claims. "Dorsal vagal" is an alias, never the
mechanism.

**Persistence and flexibility profile** · Proposed, revision required · 03.
A context- and time-specific description of how readily a pattern is
recruited, how broadly it generalises, what information and actions remain
available, how it responds to changed conditions and how recovery unfolds.
Ten dimensions, no total. Replaces "depth" as the construct; visual depth
stays as the diagram's orientation.

**Chronic routes** · Approved · 03. Prolonged persistence; interrupted or
incomplete recovery; repeated reconstruction; lowered recruitment threshold;
broader generalisation; reduced discrimination; context-bound updating;
restricted action expectancy; perseverative reconstruction; ongoing
correspondence. No one route defines Chronic.

**Acute** · Approved · 03. Research term for event-timescale exposure or
response. Not a synonym for Fluid.

**Recovery** · Approved · 03. The physiological rebuilding and remaining
cost across different timescales. It can still be running after Return has
begun. Restoration is the energy strategy of Baseline X; baseline re-access
is one possible feature of Return, not its definition.

**Fluid organisation** · Approved · 03. Present-responsive organisation that
remains sufficiently coupled to current conditions, available action and
updating.

**Chronic organisation** · Approved · 03. Recurrent narrowing,
generalisation, persistence or difficult revision in recruitment, access,
interpretation, action or recovery. A pattern, not a kind of person. Not
severe, chosen, fake or permanent.

**Fluid–Chronic depth** · Working, revision required · 03. To become a
persistence and flexibility profile recording threshold, context
sensitivity, access, action range, persistence and recovery, rather than one
severity dimension.

**Return** · Approved · 03. Renewed availability of more flexible, less
defensively dominated, more restorative organisation across several
channels. Not calm and not forgiveness.

**Regulation** · Open · 03 (definition pending across all levels). Not a
synonym for calm, low arousal or parasympathetic dominance. Reduced visible
distress may reflect suppression or compliance.

## Model 3

**ESC · Emotional–Somatic Cycle** · Approved name and abbreviation · 04.
Model 3. Follows how response meets changing conditions and becomes feedback
that can influence completion, carryover, updating, learning and later entry.
Renamed from "Emotional Systems Cycle" on 23 August 2026. Proposed expanded
name "Emotional–Somatic–Cognitive–Logical Cycle" is not adopted.

**Carryover** · Approved · 04. What enters later conditions when part of
the demand, organisation or outcome remains active or has changed future
readiness: residual activation, changed weighting, altered threshold, a
learned route, reduced capacity, revised concepts, relational or material
cost. Decomposable; not an emotion stored intact.

**Fluid Cycle** · Approved · 04. The event route in which ESS, CLS and ME
stay sufficiently connected for present conditions and feedback to revise
what happens next. Fluidity is the capacity to update, not comfort.

**Chronic Cognitive-Override Cycle** · Approved · 04. The one recurrent
Chronic route ESC models in detail: ESS information is generated but
becomes unavailable, undifferentiated, distrusted or under-authorised, CLS
supplies the coherent account, pressure may change without completion, and
carryover enters the next cycle. Not the whole of Chronic organisation;
write the full name where that could be mistaken.

**Seven override distinctions** · Approved · 04. Signal generation, bodily
representation, subjective access, expression, regulation, action, emotion
belief. Research distinctions, not stages; evidence at one cannot stand in
for another.

**Completion profile** · Approved · 04. Completion is never a yes-or-no
verdict. Ask separately: condition, information, recruitment, recovery,
Return, carryover. Answers can differ.

**Mixed outcome** · Approved · 04. Completion and carryover coexisting: one
part of a condition resolves while physiology, relationship, cost, grief or
recovery remains open.

**Shared event record** · Working · 04. The coupling interface for one
event: at each moment, record conditions and signal, the Model 1 processing
configuration, the Model 2 organisation configuration, the observable event,
and ESC's transition, separately, preserving what is unknown.

**Emotional Systems Cycle** · Provenance · 04. The earlier public label,
replaced by The Emotional–Somatic Cycle on 23 August 2026.

## Frameworks and product

**F1–F12 · Foundational Frameworks** · Approved as lenses · 05.
Coordinating explanatory layers, each owning one question and handing a
bounded contribution to the next. Four movements: F1–F3 foundations and
participation; F4–F7 collective conditions and power; F8–F10 change, access
and continuity; F11–F12 integration and reflection. Numbering is
navigation, not sequence. Not established scientific theories.

**F1 · Foundations of the Fluid Gradient** · Working (fifth pass) · 05.
Evolutionary foundations of present-responsive whole-organism organisation.
Two timescales: capacities became available; capacities organise together.
Many-to-many into the positions; positions are not evolutionary stages.
Old title: Biological Evolution.

**F2 · Developmental Shaping of Emotional Participation** · Working (fifth
pass) · 05. Two inseparable jobs: capacities widen from birth, and repeated
relational outcomes shape what can be noticed, trusted, expressed, acted on
and included in the self. Seven separable layers. Old title: Developmental
Calibration; "calibration" survives only with its target named.

**Emotion invalidation** · Working · F2. Repeated interactions in which a
child's emotional experience or report is treated as meaningless, false,
excessive, shameful, dangerous, inconvenient or proof the child is the
problem, without helping them examine signal, situation, interpretation and
action separately. Not every disagreement, correction or limit.

**The acceptable character** · Working · F2. A practised organisation of
expression, behaviour, attention and self-explanation built around the
version of the child most likely to preserve care, approval, safety or
belonging. A relationship-preserving role, not a false person or a type.

**F3 · Adult Pattern Maintenance and Revision** · Working (second pass) ·
05. How prediction, meaning, self-position, response routes, immediate
consequences and feedback keep an adult pattern going or let it change. Six
construct families (F3-A to F3-F). Old title: Adult Cognition and False
Coherence; False Coherence survives only as a historical recognition label,
decomposed.

**Route audit** · Working · F3 and 04. The minimum questions whenever
maintenance, relief, revision or reinforcement is claimed: what condition
was present; what information was available; what action or inhibition
occurred; what changed immediately; what changed later; who carried the
cost, what power was involved, did viable refusal remain.

**F4 · Shared Rules and Collective Organisation** · Working (first pass) ·
05. How groups create, transmit, apply and revise explicit and implicit
expectations, and how those shape participation, belonging, behaviour and
access. Six construct families (F4-A to F4-F). A family, group, culture or
institution is not one nervous system. Old title: Rules Regulate.

**F5 · Social Valuation, Status and Unequal Conditions** · Working (first
pass) · 05. The observable processes by which groups and institutions rank
people, roles, needs or knowledge, and what those rankings do to
recognition, credibility, protection, access, resources and exposure. Human
worth is an ethical commitment; social valuation is what is observed.
Seven construct families (F5-A to F5-G). Old title: Worth Hierarchies
Regulate; the Filter of Worth survives as a decomposed recognition label.

**F6 · Bias, Categorisation and Revisable Judgement** · Working (first
pass) · 05. How prior learning, categories, affect, identity, incentives and
institutional practice shape what is noticed, weighted, inferred, decided
and corrected. "Bias" must say which of its meanings it names before it is
explained. Seven construct families (F6-A to F6-G). Bias is not a shared
Chronic state. Old title: Bias Regulates.

**F7 · Coercive Enforcement, Power and Restricted Agency** · Working
(first pass) · 05. How power, threats, punishment, surveillance,
restriction, deprivation or force make another person's refusal or
independent action meaningfully less possible. The enforcement threshold is
where the conduct plane begins; Domination is not an internal
configuration. Eight construct families (F7-A to F7-H). Old title:
Domination Regulates.

**F8 · Recovery, Reconnection and Repair as Distinct Routes** · Working
(first pass) · 05. What may change within a person (conditions, access,
capacity, updating, recovery) and the additional work required when
behaviour has affected another person's safety, agency or trust. Several
observable routes, assessed separately, replace one pathway. Eight construct
families (F8-A to F8-H). Old title: Self-Reconnection and Repair Pathway.

**F9 · Neurodivergent Variation, Disability and Environmental Fit** ·
Working (first pass) · 05. How differences in sensing, attention,
communication, movement, learning and recovery interact with environments,
support and power to shape access, participation, disability and cost.
Difference, disability, distress and mismatch stay separate; autistic
burnout is not a Gradient Position. Eight construct families (F9-A to
F9-H). Old title: Neurodivergent Configuration and Adaptive Fit.

**F10 · Intergenerational Pathways, Caregiving and Change** · Working
(first pass) · 05. How health, caregiving, learning, relationships,
resources, institutions and cultural practices remain continuous or change
across generations, route by route. "Unprocessed" is replaced by a
specified adult-to-child route; family continuity creates no duty to
reconnect. Eight construct families (F10-A to F10-H). Old title:
Rebuilding Generational Bridges.

**F11 · Ambivalence, Complexity and Responsible Integration** · Working
(first pass) · 05. How more than one feeling, need, perspective or level
can matter at once, with a method for distinguishing ambivalence,
contradiction, complexity, uncertainty and competing demands so that
evidence, choice, power and responsibility stay visible. Eight construct
families (F11-A to F11-H). Old title: Paradox Holds What Logic Cannot.

**F12 · The Human Retrospective: Emotional Information, Power and
Possibility** · Working (first pass) · 05. How some cultures learned to
mistrust emotional and bodily information, what became easier to sustain
when those signals were poorly understood, and what may become possible
when people and institutions read them more accurately. Plural, testable
and forward-looking; not a species-wide story of ESS–CLS disconnection.
Carries the warning that the regulation thread across all twelve is not yet
complete. Eight construct families (F12-A to F12-H). Old title:
Retrospective ESS–CLS Disconnection and Cultural CLS Elevation.

**Inner Compass** · Approved · 06. The complete public experience. Brings
together emotional information, Models 1 to 3, Fluid–Chronic organisation
and the separate conduct-and-power record.

**4-Mode Gradient** · Approved mapping; visual grammar deferred · 06. The
Inner Compass's central orientation map: four solid, ordered modes on which
the public tools build. A mode is the position the organisation is
organised around and returns to (3 September 2026; until then "a
regulation dependency" and "a fork"). Connection: organised around A on the
Fluid plane, visiting every other position and returning. Protection:
organised around B on the Chronic plane, with the protecting neighbours X,
A, A↔B and Z. Control: Chronic C. Dominance: Chronic D. The mode words also
name the core positions as they show in one behaviour (A, B, C, D), each of
which can be visited or stabilised. A synthesis layer, not Model 4.

**Dominance / Domination** · Approved boundary · 06. Dominance is internal
organisation (Chronic D). Domination is observable conduct that reduces
another person's agency. Neither establishes the other.

**Behaviour / HPR** · Approved boundary; public page pending · 07. Conduct,
impact, accountability and repair, assessed on their own evidence plane.
Three planes, described separately before being read together:
organisation, behaviour pattern, power use. Organisation ≠ behaviour
pattern ≠ power use.

**Enforcement threshold** · Approved · 07 and F7. Where power, threats,
punishment, surveillance, restriction, deprivation or force make another
person's refusal or independent action meaningfully less possible. The
conduct plane begins here; legitimate authority can be bounded without
crossing it.

**Organism-protection / connection-preservation** · Approved · 07. The two
interacting survival functions: viability served through direct protection
of the organism, and through maintaining access to consequential others.
Functional problem-spaces, not two anatomies; the survival function and
defensive recruitment are different rows.

**Evidence labels** · Approved · Evidence. Axis 1, relationship to the
claim: DIRECT, BRIDGE, INDIRECT, TEG-BLUE. Axis 2, strength: A convergent,
B supported, C preliminary, M mixed, U untested. Written as a pair, for
example BRIDGE–B.

## Evidence language

**Level 1 · Established evidence. Level 2 · Grounded synthesis. Level 3 ·
TEG-Blue integration.** · Approved · README and every grounding file. A
Level 3 term need not appear in Level 1 literature; the test is whether
Levels 1 and 2 responsibly ground its territory.

## Retired claims (do not reintroduce)

"Emotion is data." "Chronic Survival is incomplete Return." "Body energy
strategy." "Accumulation at A." Debris, somatic debt. Four fixed
nervous-system types. Percentage claims such as "31% Protection". Power
Mode, Power–Domination. "M1/M2/M3" as names for the current models (those
letters named retired legacy systems).
