Model 3 working definition

Model 3 follows how response meets changing conditions and becomes feedback that can influence completion, carryover, updating, learning and later entry.

ESC is the canonical abbreviation for Model 3. It is not an additional model beside Models 1–3.

Temporal ownership

Keep outcomes separate.

Response and feedback

What happened, what changed and what information the outcome supplies.

Completion and non-completion

Whether the relevant condition changed enough to support updating.

Carryover and learning

What remains influential and what may alter later weighting, expectation or participation.

Later entry

What the organism brings into the next event without implying one fixed loop.

Provisional working idea · not approved

Should the full name make CLS participation visible?

Emotional–Somatic–Cognitive–Logical Cycle

Anna proposed this new name as an initial idea. It may better express participation by both ESS and CLS, but it must be tested against Model 3’s temporal ownership, readability and the established ESC abbreviation before adoption. The current canonical name remains Emotional–Somatic Cycle.

Owner boundary

Time does not absorb the other models.

Model 1 owns ESS, CLS, coordinated conscious access and possible ME participation. Model 2 owns present organisation. Fluid–Chronic owns persistence and recovery profile. Behaviour/HPR owns conduct, impact, accountability and repair. Model 3 follows their changing event relationships through time.
